Audience: CEO, Assets CxO and CFO. Fifteen minutes; ask for a bounded next stage.
Sergeant: lead with outcome and decision. Evangelist: one shared programme
record serving warmer homes. Scientist: alternatives, causal tests and cash
exposure. Therapist: dignity, safe contact and resident choice.

Epic means a legible mission, not inflated certainty. Use active language and
one argument per slide. No autonomous regulatory, consent, technical, funding,
quality or payment authority. Distinguish requested ambition, research
hypothesis, owner exercise direction, proposed design, observed feasibility
and untested benefit. Role agents are research instruments, not staff interviews.
Do not expose resident records, private addresses, endpoint names or secrets.
Use exact dated source sections in notes and short visible status footers.
