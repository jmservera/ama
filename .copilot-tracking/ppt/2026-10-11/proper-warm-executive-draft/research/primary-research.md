## Customer Offer, Workflow and Adoption
The proposed customer offer is one staff-facing retrofit workbench: connected
home, building, scheme, work-package and service records; role-specific queues;
and visible ownership, next action, evidence, blocker, capacity, quality,
handoff and finance state. Azure SQL, versioned Blob evidence and one controlled
API support the exercise design. Deterministic rules control priority and
transitions. AI supplies bounded, source-linked choices, drafts or flags; it
does not decide, approve, consent, submit or pay.

The intended process spans identity and intake, prioritisation, technical
assessment and resident choice, funding/capacity checks, work release, provider
delivery, independent quality and defect handling, resident handover and
financial closure. Residents retain safe non-digital contact and measure-level
choice. Contractors receive scoped work and return evidence through approved
channels; neither group is assumed to log in to the internal staff tool.

Excel remains useful during a controlled transition: profile owners and
workbooks; shadow one-way imports; time-box parallel operation with named
owners, quarantine, reconciliation and exit thresholds; train staff through
role-based task walkthroughs; then make the workbench the write path and Excel
an API-fed report. No permanent dual write. Workbook formats, tenant capability,
training needs and exit thresholds are unknown and must be validated.

Expected benefits are hypotheses: a common case may improve handoff visibility,
reduce effort or rework, and track accepted homes more reliably; resident and
contractor impacts may include clearer choices, updates, scope and return
paths. No saving, throughput increase, resident outcome, security certification
or user acceptance is claimed. Compare matched process outcomes and paired
tasks on equal inputs; count full effort, errors, delays, returns, safety,
quality, equity, cash and displacement.

The security design proposes server-enforced role/case/field permissions,
minimum-necessary model inputs, a single API write path, independent human
gates, versioned evidence, audit, quarantine and manual fallback. Exercise
records are constructed and non-identifying. Authorization, leakage, recovery,
privacy, monitoring and operational resilience need tests and named approval;
cloud hosting alone does not establish security.
