## Prioritized surgical improvements — not blockers

1. **Slide 2, funding card: preserve the exact original ask.** “THE ORIGINAL ASK” currently says “Can we prepare the claim?” The original `Agentic Architecture Brief` asks for funding applications and reporting. The later research legitimately shifts attention to claimable evidence and cash, but presenting the shifted task as the original slightly weakens the discovery reversal. Replace only the question with **“Can we prepare the application?”** Keep slide 4's ledger/claim/cash challenge. No new section or slide is needed.

2. **Slide 15, programme comparison: name both arms.** The left card says matched localities, but neither it nor the spoken paragraph explicitly names the concurrent locality without agents. The right-hand paired no-AI task test is already clear, so this is not a missing evaluation design. Replace the left card's first line with **“Existing teams: assisted vs usual delivery”**, retaining matched localities in notes. Replace the first programme-comparison sentence in notes with **“Compare an assisted locality with matched existing delivery without agents, using the same event capture and frozen cohort, against an instrumented pre-period.”** This makes the contrast defensible without adding a dense methods section or claiming randomization.

3. **Slide 12, speaker notes: cut the unnecessary register drop.** “A binary adapter cannot prove a three-class evaluation” is accurate provenance but disproportionate in the main executive pitch. Replace that sentence with **“The observed task does not prove wider accuracy or net value.”** Retain the exact binary/three-class limitation in the evidence register and technical Q&A if challenged. The slide itself is legible and makes the selection-versus-generation distinction properly.

These are precision/attention improvements, not requests to expand scope. Do not add blanket disclaimers, invented consultation or new sections to cure them.
