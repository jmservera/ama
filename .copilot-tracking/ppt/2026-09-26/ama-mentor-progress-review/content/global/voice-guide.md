# Voice Guide

* First person, candid and reflective: this is a progress conversation with a mentor, not a sales pitch.
* Action titles: each title states the takeaway in one line of 65 characters or fewer.
* Keep evidence classes visible: case fact, sector hypothesis, planning assumption, unknown.
* Plain ASCII punctuation, no em dashes, British spelling (programme, organisation).
* Numbers carry their status: "provisional", "modelled", "opportunity, not award".
* Speaker notes carry the story and the "why"; slides carry the evidence.
