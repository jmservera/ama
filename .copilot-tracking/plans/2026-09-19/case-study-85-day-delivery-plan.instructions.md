# Implementation Plan: Case Study 48 85-Day Delivery

## Overview

Use 2026-09-19 as planning day zero, then prepare an evidence-led,
decision-focused C-suite proposition, architecture package and optional bounded
demonstration across exactly 12 work sessions in the 85-day inclusive window
from 2026-09-20 through 2026-12-13.

## Objectives

### User Requirements

* Produce a thorough week-by-week plan for 85 calendar days with one workday per week - Source: conversation on 2026-09-19
* Win four or five CxO evaluators during the first 10 minutes of a one-hour presentation - Source: conversation on 2026-09-19
* Provide high- and medium-level architecture with justified decisions and trade-offs - Source: conversation on 2026-09-19
* Include a working demonstration when feasible without making it a critical dependency - Source: conversation on 2026-09-19
* Challenge the supplied solution request and identify a better evidence-backed approach - Source: conversation on 2026-09-19
* Use synthetic data and fictional role agents to address company-information gaps - Source: conversation on 2026-09-19
* Protect time for mentor feedback, unexpected events and complete prototype restarts - Source: conversation on 2026-09-19

### Derived Objectives

* Ask for capped readiness and shadow-pilot authority rather than a five-agent rollout - Derived from: case-study addendum, BRD and research synthesis
* Keep safety, resident consent, professional judgement, finance, procurement and quality acceptance outside AI authority - Derived from: regulatory and repository evidence
* Use deterministic lifecycle and evidence controls before bounded AI assistance - Derived from: auditability, testability and decision-right requirements
* Gate working code in Session 8 and maintain a static fallback - Derived from: fixed capacity and executive reliability
* Label company facts, sector hypotheses and unknowns throughout - Derived from: fictional-company evidence limitations
* Define a benefits and evaluation scorecard that can govern progression from readiness to pilot - Derived from: need for attributable executive value

## Success Criteria

* The final ask is clear, bounded and repeatable after the first 10 minutes - Traces to: user pitch requirement
* The full narrative fits one hour with at least six minutes for challenge - Traces to: user presentation requirement
* High- and medium-level architectures show deterministic controls, human authority and fallback - Traces to: architecture requirement
* Every major decision records evidence, alternatives and trade-offs - Traces to: justification requirement
* Synthetic evidence is labelled, non-identifying and validated - Traces to: synthetic-data requirement
* Session 6 can rebuild the high-level proposition, Session 9 can discard and
  rebuild the prototype, and Session 11 protects narrative and assurance
  recovery - Traces to: contingency requirement
* Working code is included only when it improves decision confidence and survives cold-start testing - Traces to: optional demo requirement
* The benefits scorecard names baseline owners, target status, measurement
  windows, attribution and stop thresholds - Traces to: executive business-case requirement
