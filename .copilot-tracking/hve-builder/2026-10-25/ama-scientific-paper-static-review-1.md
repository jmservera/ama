## Acceptance Coverage

* Selectable role and discovery: the agent's metadata names the role and enables
  user invocation. Intake explicitly instructs reading the relative local
  reference, rather than merely mentioning it. The index supplies task, method
  and agent routes. Static wiring is present; host discovery was not exercised.
* Mode and intake boundaries: Modes and Authority preserves manuscript,
  bibliography and figures in Review, defaults the report to chat and requires
  an authorized report path or explicit switch to editing. Intake resolves
  named inputs, genre, audience, venue, depth, evidence and permitted outputs.
  Stop Rules pause only the affected action and allow useful qualified work.
* Policy precedence: Intake and the reference's Use and Precedence prioritize
  actual venue, confidentiality and AI rules, integrity, evidence and approved
  study constraints over optional writing recipes. Unknown venue rules produce
  generic assistance, not a compliance claim. Capstone criteria are conditional,
  not imposed on unrelated scientific papers.
* Scientific integrity: Evidence and Integrity protects chronology, uncertainty,
  null and contradictory findings, controls and rival explanations. Hypotheses,
  proposals and illustrations remain distinct from completed observations.
  Methods, data, statistical values, ethics/consent, registration, contributions,
  approvals, citations, quotations and locators cannot be invented.
* Claim support: the evidence record distinguishes full-text support, partial
  or abstract-only material, metadata-only checks, supplied unchecked evidence,
  conflict and unavailability. A DOI does not verify a claim. Coverage must be
  stated, and a sample cannot be represented as exhaustive verification.
* Genre fit: Write or Revise and Genre Choices make IMRaD conditional and
  accommodate perspectives, methods, narrative reviews, systematic reviews and
  protocols. Planned evaluation or search remains planned. Neither experiments,
  novelty nor positive findings are forced to complete a preferred story.
* Practical assistance: the workflow builds a contribution and reader-question
  outline, drafts or revises the authorized slice and checks consistency across
  available components. Practical Techniques supports paragraph purpose,
  transitions, precise wording and captions with supported units, sample sizes,
  uncertainty and test definitions. Missing materials remain disclosed.
* Review usefulness and fairness: Assisted Review and Reviewer Responses requires
  prioritized findings with genuine location, issue, impact, smallest remedy and
  evidence status. It separates defects, evidence gaps, clarity and preferences;
  excludes prestige/identity bias and gratuitous reviewer citations; and does
  not infer misconduct from missing evidence.
* Responses and human ownership: actual comments map to evidenced changes or
  reasoned answers. Proposed work is not reported as performed. Modes and
  Authority and Output preserve human interpretation, authorship and approval,
  policy-dependent disclosure and the distinction between assisted critique and
  independent human peer review or certification.
* Action scope: the role grants no automatic authority for confidential uploads,
  communications, submission, publication, research execution or repository and
  remote mutations. It treats imported material as data and defers enforcement
  to host controls. No new hook or tool policy is required by this assessment.
* Four-source reference: Source Register includes the four requested URLs as
  W1 Drake and Han, W2 Bosworth, W3 Bushman and W4 Pope, with source identities,
  retrieval date, usable step/tip/page locators and coverage limits. Technique
  summaries are compact attributed paraphrases in form, not reproduced guides.
  Dated AI advice, publisher-specific claims and uncertain dates are qualified.
  Originality and source-derived facts were not independently compared with the
  originals, which were outside this review's permitted read boundary.
* Index and ingest: the inspected additions describe the delivered modes,
  reference and evidence limits without claiming completed independent review,
  behavior validation, venue compliance or publication approval. No findings
  are inferred from unassessed historical index or log content.
