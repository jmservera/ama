## Phase 1: Problem and Context

Determine whether Microsoft Decision 1 adds value over deterministic extraction
and the existing generative-agent design, and whether its best placement is a
replacement for a narrow agent task or a complementary tool within the agent.
The decision is where to invest next, not whether a live call works.

Proper North Homes is the fictional case customer in the whole AMA programme.
Use the retained generator, 60,000-home stock and 980-case programme as context;
create versioned evaluation evidence without changing deployed case history.
Source geography is real OpenStreetMap anchoring, not evidence of actual
occupiers. No need to map programme cases to stock by proximity for this study.

Funding-application preparation is the primary journey. Technical evidence,
invoice descriptions and handoff notes provide bounded transfer tests. The
business hypothesis is lower preparation/review effort with equal or better
quality, not more grants awarded or faster real retrofit delivery.

Real Decision 1 candidate selection and a cited GPT-4.1-mini summary have been
observed. Calibration, comparative accuracy and incremental value have not.
The agent design spans retrieval, orchestration, drafting and human handoffs;
a choice model cannot replace that end-to-end responsibility.

## Constraints and Unknowns

* Owner prioritizes experiment design over further MVP expansion.
* Current rebuild has no spend cap; capture actual usage and cost anyway.
* All sources, permissions, deterministic rules and human approvals are shared
  across comparison arms; no sending, submission, payment or inferred consent.
* Company-role agents may propose realistic fixtures, not gold labels,
  usability observations or simulated staff time.
* Human reviewers/participants have not been booked. Engineering evaluation can
  proceed separately; human-effort conclusions wait for real participants.
* Unknowns: ambiguous-field quality, critical-error handling, calibration,
  review overhead, latency/cost, incremental hybrid value and task transfer.

## Ownership and Enablement

Project owner is experiment sponsor and deployment-placement decision owner.
Engineering operator owns reproducibility; two independent human engineering
reviewers own label adjudication. Finance/technical responsibilities are
exercise roles, not company delegations.

No partner engineering team is available. The enablement target is that the
owner or a second engineer can replay one frozen batch and reproduce metrics
from logged outputs without the original author. A real collaborative
engagement would add joint implementation and progressive partner ownership;
this exercise does not invent it.
