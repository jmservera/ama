Verdict: proceed. Gating on the flag alone preserves fail-closed behaviour: with the flag on, a storage failure still raises and is never reported as enabled. No DPIA, consultation, notice or dual-role rule changes. Off-state loses the informational requested/activated fields (False); acceptable since capture cannot be used.
