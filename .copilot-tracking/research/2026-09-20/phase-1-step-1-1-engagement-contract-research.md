## Scope

Plan only Phase 1 Step 1.1 of the 85-day delivery: convert the drafted
engagement framing into an accepted, evidence-labelled contract and locked
review calendar. Do not expand into traceability, detailed architecture,
synthetic-data design, production deployment or portfolio rollout.

## Confirmed Inputs

* The Chief Executive chairs the final meeting and holds decision authority.
* The Executive Director of Assets is the accountable operational sponsor.
* The delivery uses eleven focused sessions through 2026-12-05.
* The period from 2026-12-06 through 2026-12-13 is finishing margin only.
* Human approval remains mandatory for consent, safety, PAS 2035 outputs,
  finance, procurement, claims, payments and quality acceptance.
* The existing engagement contract content is drafted in
  `.copilot-tracking/research/2026-09-19/case-study-85-day-delivery-research.md`.

## Required Outcome

Create
`.copilot-tracking/changes/2026-09-19/case-study-85-day-delivery-changes.md`
as the durable Step 1.1 implementation record. It must contain:

1. Step status and confirmation log
2. One-page goal card
3. Decision ladder
4. Audience and influence map
5. Evidence classification legend and initial claim register
6. Eleven-session review calendar
7. Timed one-hour presentation shell
8. Acceptance record and unresolved items

## Planning Decisions

* Use the detailed eleven-session delivery sequence as the default.
* Treat Step 1.3 sponsor and evidence validation as an asynchronous gate rather
  than a numbered session unless the sponsor reallocates a session.
* Treat the existing 11-slide deck as reference-only until the user confirms it
  is the editable baseline.
* Preserve proposal and unknown labels. Do not convert them into company facts.
* Record missing human confirmations as acceptance blockers rather than
  inventing names, dates, availability or authority.

## Acceptance Blockers

* Sponsor approval of the one-sentence final ask
* Confirmation of exactly four or five executive audience roles
* Exact appointments, review owners and feedback deadlines for all sessions
* Confirmation of whether the existing deck is a baseline or prior reference
* Acceptance owner for evidence thresholds and contested-claim removal

## Validation Basis

* Every goal-card claim has a source and accepted evidence label.
* The final ask states what is approved and excludes production rollout.
* The audience has four or five confirmed roles, including the Chief Executive.
* All eleven sessions have exact slots, owners, deadlines, gates and fallbacks.
* The presentation timing totals 60 minutes, including a 10-minute opening and
  6-minute structured challenge.
* The finishing margin contains no planned recovery for substantive work.
* Step 1.1 remains in progress until sponsor or proxy acceptance and calendar
  evidence are recorded.
