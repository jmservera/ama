## Research Questions and Answers

### Q1: What already exists in `src/core` and `infra/` on `feat/platform-115-blob-immutability`, and what gaps remain for Objectives 2 and 3?

* **Existing baseline**:
  * `infra/modules/storage.bicep` defines `containers` with `'ledger-digests'` at index 5 (`C1`), enables blob versioning (`isVersioningEnabled: true`, `C2`), defines unconditional `digestRetention` (`Microsoft.Storage/storageAccounts/blobServices/containers/immutabilityPolicies@2024-01-01`) with `immutabilityPeriodSinceCreationInDays: ledgerDigestImmutabilityDays` and `allowProtectedAppendWrites: false` (`C3`), and exports `ledgerDigestContainer` (`C4`).
  * `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/ledger_digest_blob.py` implements `verify_container_retention_lock` (`C7`), `verify_blob_retention_headers` (`C8`), `BlobLedgerDigestStore` (`C9`), `publish_ledger_digests` (`C10`), `verify_ledger_digests` (`C11`), and `run_scheduled_verification` (`C12`).
* **Remaining gaps**:
  1. **Bicep container-level `immutableStorageWithVersioning`**: `blobContainers` in `infra/modules/storage.bicep` only sets `properties: { publicAccess: 'None' }` (`C1`) and never enables `immutableStorageWithVersioning: { enabled: true }` on `ledger-digests`.
  2. **Bicep fail-closed validation against unlocked/mutable digest containers in `production` profile + runtime lock wiring**:
     * `infra/main.bicep` exposes `ledgerDigestImmutabilityDays` (`@minValue(1)`, default `365`, `C5`) without explicit development-only opt-out controls (`ledgerDigestImmutableStorage`, `ledgerDigestRequireRetentionLock`) or `guard.bicep` enforcement (`C6`) rejecting mutable (`ledgerDigestImmutableStorage == false`), sub-floor (`ledgerDigestImmutabilityDays < 365`), or unlocked (`ledgerDigestRequireRetentionLock == false`) digest container posture when `deploymentProfile == 'production'`.
     * `infra/modules/containerapps.bicep` configures `ledgerVerify` (`C13`) without passing `ZF_DIGEST_REQUIRE_RETENTION_LOCK` or `ZF_DIGEST_MIN_RETENTION_DAYS`, leaving scheduled verification defaulting to `ZF_DIGEST_REQUIRE_RETENTION_LOCK=false` in `production` profile!
  3. **`src/core` publisher/verifier container immutability & retention-lock posture metadata and fail-closed enforcement**:
     * `verify_container_retention_lock` (`C7`) returns `"state"`, `"retention_days"`, `"locked"`, omitting canonical `"immutability_policy_state"` and ignoring `container_properties["immutableStorageWithVersioning"]`.
     * `BlobLedgerDigestStore` (`C9`) and `publish_ledger_digests` / `publish_store_digests` (`C10`) do not inspect container immutability policy/properties or `PUT`/`GET` WORM response headers (`x-ms-immutability-policy-mode`, `x-ms-immutability-policy-until-date`), do not record `immutability_policy_state`, `retention_days`, `locked`, and do not fail closed when `require_retention_lock=True` and the container/blob retention lock is absent, `Unlocked`, or below `min_retention_days`.
     * `verify_ledger_digests` (`C11`) and `run_scheduled_verification` (`C12`) only return `retention_lock_verified: bool` without structured posture metadata (`immutability_policy_state`, `retention_days`, `locked` and honesty-boundary metadata), and `verify_ledger_digests` does not pass `x-ms-creation-time` + `min_retention_days` into `verify_blob_retention_headers` when checking remote blob WORM headers (`C11`).
     * `BlobLedgerDigestStore._urlopen_transport` (`C9`) chains raw `exc` (`from exc`), risking secret URL exposure in tracebacks if an uncaught exception propagates (`RV-001` from prior review).

### Q2: Does `infra/network-posture-inventory.json` or `infra/scripts/bicep_inventory.py` require updates?

* `infra/network-posture-inventory.json` is absent in this repository.
* `infra/scripts/bicep_inventory.py` (`C14`) tracks entrypoints, modules, resource types, outbound dependencies, and resource counts embedded in `infra/README.md` (verified by `infra/tests/test_bicep_architecture_inventory_136.py`, `C18`). Keeping `immutableStorageWithVersioning` as a property on `blobContainers` and adding parameters to `main.bicep`, `guard.bicep`, `storage.bicep`, and `containerapps.bicep` adds zero new ARM resources or module edges, preserving `bicep_inventory.py --check` unless a resource count changes (we will run `python3 infra/scripts/bicep_inventory.py --check` to verify).
* `infra/tests/test_environment_quota_parameters.py` (`C17`) asserts `len(self.guard["parameters"]) == 29` and checks that every `guard.bicep` parameter has `@minLength(1)` and `"Empty means"` in its `@description(...)`. Adding `productionProfileRequiresImmutableLedgerDigests` to `guard.bicep` requires updating that parameter-count assertion to `30` and adhering to the exact `@description('... Empty means ...')` convention.

### Q3: How is the honesty boundary preserved while enforcing fail-closed behavior?

* ADR-0002 (`C19`) and Issue #115 require digests to land in an immutable container with a retention lock while keeping Legal retention-period sign-off (#121) and live Azure `lockImmutabilityPolicy` execution human/cloud-gated:
  * In ARM (`Microsoft.Storage/.../immutabilityPolicies@2024-01-01`), `PUT` creates the policy in `Unlocked` state (`state` is read-only on ARM `PUT`, `C15`).
  * In Bicep `production` profile (`deploymentProfile == 'production'`), `guard.bicep` fails closed at template validation if `ledgerDigestImmutableStorage == false`, `ledgerDigestRequireRetentionLock == false`, or `ledgerDigestImmutabilityDays < 365`, and `containerapps.bicep` sets `ZF_DIGEST_REQUIRE_RETENTION_LOCK='true'` and `ZF_DIGEST_MIN_RETENTION_DAYS=string(ledgerDigestImmutabilityDays)` on `ledgerVerify`.
  * At runtime in `src/core`, both publisher and verifier compute and record explicit posture metadata (`immutability_policy_state`, `retention_days`, `locked`, `worm_lock_human_gated: True`), failing closed (`LedgerDigestBlobError` on publish when `require_retention_lock=True`; `intact: False` / `LEDGER_DIGEST_ALERT` on verify when `require_retention_lock=True`) whenever the container/blob policy is absent, `Unlocked`, or below `min_retention_days`. Neither offline tests nor Bicep compilation claims that live Azure `lockImmutabilityPolicy` has been executed or that Legal has approved the 365-day default schedule.

## Risks, Open Items, and Potential Further Research

* **Risk — ARM `null` vs omitted `immutableStorageWithVersioning`**: Verify `az bicep build` and `az bicep lint` on `infra/modules/storage.bicep` and `infra/main.bicep` with `bicepconfig.json` analyzer rules so conditional `immutableStorageWithVersioning` produces zero Bicep errors/warnings and evaluates cleanly in `test_bicep_policy.py`.

Supporting BOTH supplied ARM `retention_policy`/`container_properties` AND data-plane response headers (and container properties probe when supported by transport, falling back cleanly when a minimal mock transport only handles `?comp=list` and blob paths) ensures fail-closed enforcement in every execution path without breaking existing mock transports.
