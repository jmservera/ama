## Executive Summary

Research is in progress. The presentation needs the full Azure service bill,
not just model tokens, and a bounded AI usage scenario with separate chat and
priority-task allowances.

## What You May Not Know

Scale-to-zero for application replicas does not establish zero charges for
storage, databases, registry, networking or monitoring. Source configuration
and any current management-plane observation will be distinguished.

## Findings

No unit prices or cost totals are accepted yet.

## Recommendation and Alternatives

unit prices. Compare protected task allowances with a single shared chat
