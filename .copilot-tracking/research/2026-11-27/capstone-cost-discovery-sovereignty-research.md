## What You May Not Know

GlobalStandard does not establish UK-only inference even when the resource
is in the UK. Entra's listed core-store geographies do not provide a UK-only
identity promise. Azure Local now documents local Registry and Key Vault,
and Microsoft announced SQL Server and Foundry Local general availability
on 17 November; saying that every managed component disappears would be
wrong. Blob and the connected-to-disconnected control-plane transition remain
preview. The current GPT-4.1 family has a published 14 April 2027 retirement
schedule; a successor needs quality, price and regional availability checks.
