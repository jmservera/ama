### Decision ladder

1. Confirm the final audience, chair, decision authority and evidence standard.
2. Approve the capped readiness and shadow-pilot design exercise.
3. Use the exercise to identify the binding retrofit-flow constraint and define
   measurable entry, continuation and stop gates.
4. If readiness gates pass, seek a separate decision for a bounded shadow pilot
   using synthetic or appropriately governed data.
5. Treat any live pilot, production deployment or portfolio expansion as a
   later decision requiring its own evidence, assurance and approval.

## Planning Findings

### Executive proposition

The strongest proposition is not approval for five autonomous agents. It is
approval for a capped readiness and shadow-pilot exercise that discovers the
binding retrofit-flow constraint and tests whether deterministic lifecycle
controls with bounded AI assistance improve safe, resident-accepted,
evidence-complete handovers.

This keeps the pitch commercially ambitious while making the requested decision
proportionate to the evidence. The first 10 minutes should establish the
decision, resident and business stakes, core insight, high-level architecture,
labelled value scenario and stop conditions.

### Delivery capacity

The delivery plan provides 11 focused work sessions followed by one finishing
margin week. Capacity is divided into nine production sessions and two feedback
or recovery sessions. Session 6 protects architecture restart capacity.
Session 9 can discard and rebuild the narrow prototype after the Session 8
review. Session 11 completes mandatory assurance, final validation and the
readiness freeze. The final margin week is not available for new evidence,
architecture, features or prototype rebuilding.

### Evidence and regulatory posture

* Company-specific throughput, stock, grant, cost, borrowing, resident and
  supplier figures remain unknown unless separately validated.
* Sector comparators and calculated scenarios must not be presented as Proper
  North Homes facts.
* Awaab's Law Phase 1 has applied since 2025-10-27. Phase 2 applies from
  2026-11-30, inside the delivery window.
* The 2026 social rented sector MEES government response supersedes the
  addendum's consultation-only wording, but implementing and commencement
  status still requires legal confirmation.
* UK GDPR requires purpose limitation, minimisation, accountability and
  appropriate safeguards. Vulnerability information may be special-category
  data, and a later real-data pilot may require a DPIA.
* Resident consent, safety decisions, PAS 2035 professional outputs, finance,
  procurement, claims, payments and quality acceptance remain human decisions.

### Architecture direction

Use a portfolio control plane that integrates systems of record rather than
replacing them. Put deterministic state transitions, dated policies, evidence
gates, work queues, approval records, audit and reconciliation at the centre.
Restrict AI to source-linked recommendations, drafts, reconciliation and flags.
Show trust boundaries and manual fallback paths explicitly.

### Demonstration strategy

Use genuinely synthetic, non-identifying data with a manifest, dictionary,
validation rules and hidden ground truth. Build only one coherent slice:
property timeline, portfolio queue, deterministic blocked-state explanation and
one bounded AI summary. Decide in Session 8 whether a live demo adds more value
than tested screenshots or a recorded walkthrough.

### Presentation structure

* 0-10 minutes: decision-led executive pitch
* 10-18 minutes: evidence, regulatory context and unknowns
* 18-28 minutes: resident journey, target operating model and high-level design
* 28-38 minutes: medium-level design, controls and trade-offs
* 38-44 minutes: optional demo or fallback
* 44-50 minutes: economics, attribution and downside
* 50-54 minutes: roadmap, gates, owners and decision request
* 54-60 minutes: structured challenge, conditions and decision confirmation
