tenure cases and report absent whole-programme coverage honestly.
Existing challenge tests already preserve unrelated decisions and independent
hazard intake. Adding another generic challenge engine is rejected.
Reflection: implement safe intake and hold only; do not implement a purported
Legal approval or operational nomination eligibility policy.
