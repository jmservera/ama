## Findings in scope

* H1 (retained physical-dispatch High): after `Window.first_byte` returns, a process suspended before `super().send` keeps a user-space timer and no further authority check. Its durable restamp can lapse, another process can take the slot, and the first process can still transmit. Another last check, renewal, timer or disclosure does not remove that interleaving; only an independently owning sender or a provider-side fence would, and neither exists or may be inferred or built here. The E6 two-process tests stall at the financial `dispatch_check`, before the transport fences, so they never exercised this boundary. Decision for this cycle: the real-provider simulation route is code-enforced unavailable. `Ai.send_fence_supported = True` is a false capability advertisement and is removed (`False`). The lane refuses a real `Ai` before admission, credential, financial reservation or socket. Nothing public (environment, request body, settings) can set the capability; only code can. Fixture-only and scripted plumbing paths remain and are labelled as such, never as real AI. The private captured-reading route stays OFF.
* H2 (new ordinary-runtime High): `http.client.HTTPConnection.getresponse` calls `self.close()` when the response will close, and `urllib.request` `do_open` closes `h.sock` and sets it to `None` after headers for persistent responses. The `Window` timer is cancelled by `close()` or looks up `self.sock` late, so the absolute deadline does not own the socket through `response.read`. A peer that sends headers and trickles the body keeps the call alive past the deadline. Fix: the window captures a stable shutdown handle (the live socket object, taken after connect) and is cancelled only by the owner of the whole bounded response (the opener result wrapper that closes after `read` completes or fails), never by `close()` of the connection. Socket shutdown ends the local read only; no claim is made that remote provider computation stops.

E6 recorded them as present before its edits (at the merged base); the file is in this workstream's typed scope, so they are fixed here by wrapping only.
