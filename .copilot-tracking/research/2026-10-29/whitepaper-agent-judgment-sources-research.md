## Contradictions, Qualifications and Uncertainty

No direct source-versus-case contradiction was established. Skills evidence
challenges an unqualified instruction-accumulation-is-harmful interpretation;
the existing text already permits beneficial maintenance. Anthropic challenges
treating formal approval as proof of independent human judgment; the manuscript
already distinguishes outcome evidence from different reviewers, but needs
that distinction made explicit in discussion.

One internal exemplar qualification is worth retaining for the authors:
Section 5.4 says no-hint effects are small for success-only trajectories, but
Table 16 includes Gemini Terminal-Bench-2 0.7923 normal versus 0.4231 no-hint
at 5s0f. Do not reuse that broad sentence or reconcile it speculatively.
The appendix also reports framework/model changes together; portability is
not a clean framework-only effect. Neither issue is needed for the planned
manuscript claims. Source methods are an exemplar to inspect critically,
not a reporting-quality certification.
