## Questions and Evidence Criteria

1. Does PRD FR-020 through FR-024 plus the generated 43.8M-row history make Fabric useful now, independently of low average event rate?
2. Do shared capacity, mirroring allowances and managed capabilities materially weaken the proposed defer/sidecar framing?
3. Which objections are false or overstated under current documentation?
4. What burden remains after correcting those objections, and where does the SQL/Power BI alternative carry comparable cost or security work?
5. What minimum new evidence could change the parent decision without treating absence of a Fabric implementation as evidence of no need?

Contrarian evidence goal: identify the strongest defensible case for Fabric now, distinguish it from proof of immediate adoption, and expose any factual overstatement in the parent framing.

## Strongest Case for Fabric Now

The strongest supported pro-Fabric case is a **selective, read-only analytical implementation now**, not an operational replacement: FR-020 through FR-024 already require portfolio, time-history, forecast and evidence views; the reconciled 43.8M-row two-year synthetic run makes historical analytical modelling a concrete near-term engineering concern; and Azure SQL mirroring plus OneLake/Direct Lake can reduce the bespoke replication and full-refresh work needed to explore a governed analytical projection. Existing approved shared Fabric/P capacity with demonstrable spare CU and licensed viewers could make this materially cheaper and simpler than standing up a separate SQL read-model plus Power BI refresh/gateway path. A GA read-only Fabric data agent could later provide staff analytical Q&A over those curated measures.

This is a plausible case, not a proof of immediate need: neither shared capacity/headroom nor a named decision-user, required latency/concurrency, analytical projection, source-field authority, access model or comparative performance/cost result was established.

## Factual Corrections and Boundaries for Parent Synthesis

* Do not say there is no analytics requirement because Fabric is not implemented. FR-020 through FR-024 state material reporting/history/forecast requirements; implementation absence proves only that the chosen platform is not yet demonstrated.
* Do not use $131.4M$ as an observed workload. It is $3 \times 60{,}000 \times 730$ scenario arithmetic. The observed synthetic run is 43.8M daily rows and separately does not establish production telemetry, three/day cadence, peak rate or query shape.
* Do not treat the 2.08/s average as a decisive no-Fabric argument. It is relevant to continuous stream sizing, not historical storage or interactive analytical queries.
* Do not reject all current ledger tables, `NVARCHAR(MAX)` JSON text or a `BIGINT` field named `rowversion` based on mirroring restrictions for ledger-history/native JSON/native rowversion. Test actual selected tables and LOB sizes.
* Do not claim private/keyless Eventstream or private identity-based Azure SQL mirroring is impossible, that data agents remain core-preview, or that F64 is universal. The product documentation contradicts each generalization, subject to feature and tenant verification.
* Do not call F2 a complete private-solution price or charge 4 CU/member-hour to every private route. The VNet-gateway meter is route-specific; source/gateway/identity/region and destination design determine the actual cost.
* Do not infer no actual Azure use ever occurred from source review. The evidence reviewed distinguishes authored/proposed topology and bounded provider observations from full live-service acceptance.

## Remaining Contradiction and Gap Record

* The strongest pro-Fabric evidence is requirements plus synthetic scale and product capability. It does not demonstrate a live operational workload, actual production data size, user concurrency, tenant capacity or agreement price.
* Managed mirroring reduces replication plumbing but not semantic-model design, permitted-field selection, source-versus-history treatment, source access-control recreation, monitoring, recovery, retention or human data governance.
* The parent may preserve API/core/Azure SQL as operational authority without treating that constraint as a rejection of a read-only analytical Fabric sidecar.
* No evidence establishes that SQL read models plus Power BI are inadequate, or that Fabric will meet a particular refresh/latency/security target better. The choice remains unresolved pending the smallest conditions above.
