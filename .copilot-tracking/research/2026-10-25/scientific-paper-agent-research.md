### Conditions and Rejected Universals

* Structure: IMRaD is useful for many empirical reports (W3 p. 16; W4 tip 4), not a gate for a perspective, conceptual/method paper or review. For non-empirical work, assess its thesis, reasoning/synthesis, source basis, counterarguments, limitations and implications. A proposed method is not a performed evaluation; a narrative review is not automatically systematic.
* Drafting order: offer W1's thesis/reasoning scaffold, W2 tip 12's methods/results-first route, or W3 p. 16's outline/figures-first route as options. W1's post-research manuscript advice does not prohibit protocols, notes or exploratory outlines, and does not authorize rewriting a hypothesis as preregistered after results are known.
* Voice/tense and length: W4 tip 5 explicitly says active voice when appropriate; W3 p. 7 recommends past tense for completed results and present for ongoing/general claims. Use genre, meaning and actual venue style rather than universal impersonality, voice, tense or paragraph/sentence counts. Preserve technical precision when simplifying.
* Novelty/metrics: W2 tips 4-5 promote novel/exciting results and citation appeal; W1 step 2 favors a two-part narrative and step 10 a positive ending. These do not justify excluding replication, null/negative results, overstating novelty, hiding limitations, choosing quality by journal metrics or citation counts, or promising acceptance. W2 tip 20's response-time statement is publisher-specific promotional context, not an operational promise.
* Statistics and images: W3 pp. 17-18/28's null-hypothesis/p-value framing is not appropriate to every design; neither significance nor a favored threshold is a universal acceptance rule. Document the actual design, estimates, uncertainty, assumptions and analysis, with expert review where needed. W2 tip 7 broadly rejects image manipulation; W3 pp. 39-40 distinguishes misleading selective alteration from disclosed, venue-permitted transformations. Do not impose either a blanket editing permission or a blanket ban on legitimate image processing.
* AI: W3 pp. 46-47 is explicitly initial January 2023 opinion, not current policy. W1 advises human intellectual framing before polishing; C6 permits capstone AI drafting while retaining human insight and evidence. Apply current, actually supplied/verified venue or course rules and disclosure requirements; never infer a universal ban, universal permission or automatic plagiarism verdict from these guides.
* Preferences versus integrity: W3 p. 14 warns against excessive compression; W1 step 7 calls for examining unmatched items rather than fabricating a connection. Do not remove uncertainty, inconvenient findings, qualifications or attribution to make a story cleaner. W3 p. 19's request to add meaningful data can become a proposal for genuinely new work only, not an assertion that work happened.
