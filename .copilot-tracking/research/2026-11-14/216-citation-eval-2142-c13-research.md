## Synthesis and Recommendations

* Accepted recommendation (`convergence`):
  1. `RV-030`: Require `"status"` in `_FRESHNESS_STATUSES` on every constituent entry in `_card_provenance_valid`; when constituent `"status"` is `"in_cadence"` or `"out_of_cadence"`, require valid ISO day `"last_success"`, integer `"cadence_days"` (`1..36600`), and `"due_on"` equal to `last_success + timedelta(days=cadence_days)` (plus active-clock inequalities against `as_of_day` when `require_active and as_of_day is not None`); when constituent `"status"` is `"unknown"`, reject non-`None` `"due_on"` or `"last_success"`. Also enforce the same constituent completeness inside `_active_bundle_nonconflicting`.
  2. `RV-031`: Broaden the compliance/legal-breach entry in `_REFUSAL_PATTERNS` to refuse property/building/dwelling/home/case/work compliance determinations, breach/violation determinations, statutory-duty satisfaction questions, and inspection-pass judgments while preserving all factual provision, cadence, and recorded-fact queries.
  3. `RV-032`: Require explicit Compliance gate-owner agreement (`compliance_owner` or `refusal_owner` matching `COMPLIANCE_GATE_OWNER`; if both are present, both must match) alongside `owner == LEGAL_GATE_OWNER` in `agreement_gaps`, and check optional `compliance_owner_id` / `refusal_owner_id` distinctness against `independent_grader_id`.
  4. `RV-033`: Require finite positive `max_ai_cost_per_accepted_check_gbp` in caller-supplied `benefit_thresholds` (setting zero repository default target per OD-51) and enforce `ai_cost_per_accepted_check <= max_ai_cost_per_accepted_check_gbp` in `compare_chat_against_rule_cards`, failing closed to `cards_only` when absent, invalid, or exceeded.
