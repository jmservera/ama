### PC-001 [High]: Pre-projection `ai_consent_state` check in `RegulatoryChat` leaks unauthorized case state, breaks `ChatNotFound`, and omits `ask()` transactional re-verification

* Related IDs: Caller Req 1, Caller Req 2, Caller Req 3, `C6`, `C7`, `C16`, `C17`, `P01`, `P01-T01`, `P04-T01`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/issue-164-chat-eval-revocation-phase-details.md (`P01`, `P01-T01`), src/core/src/zava_forward/core/regulatory_chat.py (lines 258-269, 348-384, 482-561, 662-695), src/core/src/zava_forward/core/chat_isolation.py (lines 92-97), src/core/src/zava_forward/core/store.py (lines 1251-1256), src/core/src/zava_forward/core/chat_panel.py (lines 768-771, 855-862), src/agents/tests/regulatory_gate_fixture.py (line 31)
* Concern: `P01` and `P01-T01` combine `consent_guidance(self.iso.ai_consent_state(cid))`, `self.refuse(question)`, and fallback `REFUSED + out_of_scope(question)[1]` into one `_refusal(self, question: str, cid: str) -> str | None` helper called at the start of `bundle` and `foundry_query`, and add transactional consent re-verification only to `doubt()`. In `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/chat_isolation.py`, `ai_consent_state(cid)` calls `self.store._case(cid)`, which reads raw unprojected `self.cases[cid]` without checking `user` access and raises `KeyError(cid)` if the case does not exist. Calling `ai_consent_state(cid)` before `detail = self.store.detail(cid, user)` confirms `detail is not None`: (1) leaks a resident's `ai_data_use_consent` status to callers who lack visibility into `cid` instead of raising `ChatNotFound(cid)`; (2) raises uncaught `KeyError(cid)` instead of `ChatNotFound(cid)` when `cid` is missing or deleted mid-thread; and (3) evaluates `consent_guidance` ahead of `self.refuse(question)` and `out_of_scope(question)`, inverting the refusal order in `ChatPanel._ask` (`chat_panel.py` lines 855-862) and overriding `REASONS["legal"]` (`regulatory_gate_fixture.py` line 31) when an out-of-scope question is asked on a consent-withdrawn case. In addition, `RegulatoryChat.ask` builds `b = self.bundle(...)` outside `with self.store.lock: with repo.txn():` (`regulatory_chat.py` lines 487-505), leaving a TOCTOU window if `ai_data_use_consent` is withdrawn concurrently before the turn and any conflict auto-referral (`self._refer`) are committed.
* Impact: Violates cross-case access isolation (`ChatNotFound` contract), risks breaking `regulatory_gate_fixture.py` refusal wording precedence on consent-withdrawn cases, and leaves an unguarded TOCTOU window in `RegulatoryChat.ask`.
* Smallest useful change: Split question-only refusal from post-projection case AI-consent checking in `P01` and `P01-T01`: evaluate `_question_refusal(self, question: str) -> str | None` (`self.refuse(question)` first, else `REFUSED + hit[1]` when `hit := out_of_scope(question)`) before `self.store.detail(cid, user)`; evaluate `consent_guidance(self.iso.ai_consent_state(cid))` in `bundle` and `foundry_query` only after `detail = self.store.detail(cid, user)` succeeds (`detail is not None`); and re-verify `consent_guidance(self.iso.ai_consent_state(str(thread["case_id"]))) is None` inside `RegulatoryChat.ask`'s `repo.txn()` (raising `ChatForbidden` before persisting citations or auto-referring if withdrawn concurrently) as well as inside `doubt()`.
* Action owner: planning parent (`rpi-plan`)
* Exact resolving evidence: Revised `P01`, `P01-T01`, and `P04-T01` in .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/issue-164-chat-eval-revocation-plan.md and .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/issue-164-chat-eval-revocation-phase-details.md specifying pre-`detail` `_question_refusal(question)`, post-`detail` `ai_consent_state(cid)` checking in `bundle` and `foundry_query`, and transactional `ai_consent_state` re-verification in both `ask()` and `doubt()`.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction

### PC-003 [Medium]: Vacuous `expect={"error": null}` loophole in `expected_outcome_problems` and conflicting 4-part `review_effort` schemas across Plan AC and `agents.chat_eval`

* Related IDs: Caller Req 4, `C10`, `C11`, `C13`, `P01-T02`, `P02-T01`, `P03-T01`, `P04-T01`, `P04-T02`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/issue-164-chat-eval-revocation-plan.md (Functional Requirements and Acceptance Criteria line 107), .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/issue-164-chat-eval-revocation-phase-details.md (`P01-T02`, `P02-T01`, `P03-T01`), src/agents/src/zava_forward/agents/chat_eval.py (lines 82-87, 292-312, 487-498), src/agents/src/zava_forward/agents/chat_heldout.py (lines 55-110), src/agents/tests/test_chat_eval_revocation_effort.py (lines 124-131)
* Concern: Two contract ambiguities weaken Criterion 3: (1) `P01-T02` and `P02-T01` require `expected_outcome_problems(spec)` to check that each held-out turn freezes `expect.kind` or `expect.error`. In `agents/chat_eval.py` lines 302-311 (`_check_turn`), `if exp.get("error") != error:` evaluates `None != None` (`False`) when `exp = {"error": None}`, while `"kind" in exp` is `False`, allowing any reply kind to pass vacuously; similarly, a scenario with `turns: []` or an invalid/empty `kind` would pass a naive key-presence check. (2) Plan Acceptance Criteria line 107 specifies 4 review-effort fields `(reviewer_seconds, source_open_seconds, correction_seconds, escalation_and_rework_seconds)` across `core.ai_eval`, `agents.chat_eval`/`chat_heldout`, and `/api/ai/report`, whereas `agents.chat_eval.REVIEW_EFFORT_INPUTS` (`chat_eval.py` lines 82-87, locked by `test_chat_eval_revocation_effort.py` line 128 and exposed directly in `/api/ai/report`'s `chat_evaluation = chat_eval.run_eval()` in `P03-T01`) defines the 4 canonical inputs `("reviewer minutes per reviewed answer", "reviewer minutes per rejected or withheld answer", "rework and escalation minutes", "comparator minutes on the non-AI path (case card)")`.
* Impact: Leaves a vacuous-pass loophole in held-out outcome freezing for `{"error": null}`, invalid `kind`, or empty `turns`, and creates contradictory `review_effort` schemas between `core.ai_eval` and `agents.chat_eval` / `/api/ai/report`.
* Smallest useful change: Update Plan Acceptance Criteria, `P01-T02`, and `P02-T01` so that: (a) `expected_outcome_problems(spec)` rejects empty `scenarios` or empty `turns` on any scenario and requires every turn's `expect` mapping to specify either a valid closed-set `kind` (`{"answer", "abstain", "withheld", "refused", "fallback"}` for case-scoped chat, plus `"conflict"` where regulatory harness turns are supported, with `error` absent/`None`) or a non-empty string `error` (with `kind` absent); and (b) `core.ai_eval` aligns its 4-component `REVIEW_EFFORT_INPUTS` and `review_effort` report payload with `agents.chat_eval.REVIEW_EFFORT_INPUTS` (`status="not measured"`, `minutes=None`, `missing_inputs`, `saving_claim=None` unless all 4 inputs are explicitly supplied, while keeping net comparison against the non-AI case-card comparator).

* Exact resolving evidence: Revised Plan Acceptance Criteria and `P01-T02` / `P02-T01` in .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/issue-164-chat-eval-revocation-plan.md and .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/issue-164-chat-eval-revocation-phase-details.md defining closed-set `expected_outcome_problems` validation and a single canonical 4-component `REVIEW_EFFORT_INPUTS` contract across `core`, `agents`, and `api`.
