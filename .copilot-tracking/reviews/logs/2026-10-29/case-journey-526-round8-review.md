### RV-001 [Medium]: Rebuilt raw forms lose the eventual outcome feedback

* Related scope: P01-T01/P01-T02, rebuilt resident/challenge handlers, preserved outcome/history feedback and accessible async status.
* Evidence: src/api/src/zava_forward/api/web/panels.js:170-177 and :366-373 update and focus only the status/button captured by the original handler. A same-case refresh can replace the resident form while its non-aborted POST is pending. The completed handler sets session.historyBarrier, but writes its history-check instruction into the detached form. The current form's guard at :158 or :335 then silently returns.
* Independent reproduction: Using the repository DOM fixture and shipped api/residentForm/formFor factories, held the first POST, mounted a new same-session form, then returned 503 with each of uncertain and committed. In all four family/outcome combinations: dispatch count 1, transport lock released, session barrier true, current status empty, current button enabled. Clicking that visible button dispatched nothing and still produced no status. The history-check instruction existed only in the detached original status. This probe did not require a second pending click.
* Browser/test gap: src/api/checks/case_journey_browser_check.py:405-440 deliberately produces a duplicate-pending message, then checks lock/barrier/field retention and dispatch count, not the visible final outcome instruction. The raw unit matrix similarly inspects the original button and session barrier. Thus both can pass while the replacement form shows either no outcome or a stale pending message.
* Impact: The duplicate write is prevented, but staff see an enabled control that does nothing and receive no current explanation or recovery instruction after an unconfirmed/committed result. Merely declaring a status region on a detached node does not provide the required accessible async feedback.
* Destination: Later rpi-implement, within the accepted correction direction.
* Smallest useful next action: Publish raw mutation outcome/history state to the current same-session form or persistent workspace status, including when its original form was replaced. Keep duplicate-pending feedback distinct from a server outcome. Extend the existing matrix/helper with reconstruction followed by the outcome without a duplicate click; assert visible final feedback, retained edits and exactly one POST.
