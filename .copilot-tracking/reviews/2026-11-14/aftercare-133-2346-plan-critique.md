### PC-005 [Medium]: Proposed `PARAPHRASE_PATTERNS` in P02-T01 introduce an ownership false positive (`is responsible for`) and leave singular-possessive (`'s`) and present/gerund blame gaps

* Related IDs: Objective 2, AC-03, AC-04, RV-003, RV-004, P02-T01, P04-T01

* Concern: Three regex issues appear in `P02-T01`: (1) `\b(?:is|are|was|were)\s+responsible\s+for\b` has no subject or object bound (unlike `RV-004`, which scoped the subject to `(?:{_RETROFIT}|{_HOUSEHOLD}s?)`), so it falsely rejects legitimate neutral ownership/process copy such as `"The named owner is responsible for arranging the home visit."` (2) `\b(?:resident|occupant|tenant|household|user)s?'?\s+(?:error|mistake|misuse|neglect|lifestyle|habits?|behaviour|behavior)\b` uses `s?'?`, which matches `occupants'` or `occupant'` but fails to match standard English singular possessives (`"occupant's habits"`, `"resident's lifestyle"`, `"user's mistake"`) because `_variants` (`aftercare.py:194-204`) preserves ASCII `'s` with the apostrophe before `s` (compare `aftercare.py:109,125`, which uses `s?'?s?`). * Action owner: Planning parent (`rpi-plan`)
