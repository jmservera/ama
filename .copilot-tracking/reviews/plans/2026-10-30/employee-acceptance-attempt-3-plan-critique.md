### PC-001 [High]: Planned employee evidence exceeds the caller's boolean-only boundary

* Related IDs: AC-02, AC-03, P01-T02; caller BOOLEANS ONLY direction.
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-10-30/employee-acceptance-attempt-3-plan.md:13 permits boolean/geometry evidence; :24 restates the boolean-only requirement; :46 calls for geometry and marker counts. .copilot-tracking/details/2026-10-30/employee-acceptance-attempt-3-phase-details.md:39 explicitly extracts booleans, counts and geometry, and :40-42 include active-skill/AI metadata, marker totals and layout geometry without an exclusively boolean output contract.
* Concern: The implementation is directed both to retain only booleans and to extract nonboolean employee observations. Counts and bounding-box measurements are not booleans even when they omit private identifiers. The conflict is explicit, rather than a hypothetical disclosure in code not yet written.
* Impact: A successful run could produce prohibited evidence and require invalidating or discarding its acceptance output despite consuming the limited sign-in budget.
* Smallest useful change: Define an allowlisted employee-observation schema containing boolean predicates only. Compute count comparisons, geometry thresholds, AI-versus-rules, selected-skill agreement and layout relationships in-browser; export only their truth values. Omit unperformed checks and distinguish them with non-sensitive execution-status bookkeeping rather than false acceptance booleans. Keep sanitized provider no-route text and run-provenance fields as explicitly separate, caller-authorized evidence classes, not exceptions for authenticated UI text.
* Action owner: Planning parent, with runner maintainer implementing the schema.
* Exact resolving evidence: Plan and P01-T02 agree on this schema; offline serialization tests reject numbers, strings, arrays and geometry objects in employee-observation/binding fields, and demonstrate boolean-only projections for chat/map/case. No live attempt is needed to resolve this finding.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction. The caller has already selected booleans only; no significant or divergent user decision is required.

### PC-002 [Medium]: Strict admission lacks a pinned source for its trusted comparison inputs

* Related IDs: Q2, C3, AC-02, P01-T01/P01-T02; prior RV-002.
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-10-30/employee-acceptance-attempt-3-plan.md:41 requires exact app/provider origins and :45 requires exact expected account email and canonical identity fields. .copilot-tracking/details/2026-10-30/employee-acceptance-attempt-3-phase-details.md:27 repeats these requirements. Supplied research C3 describes the response contract, but neither candidate document identifies the authoritative configuration/source for the expected account, deployed employee origin, provider-origin allowlist or exact required canonical identity fields.
* Concern: The desired checks are appropriate, but their independent expected values and concrete schema are not reproducibly bound to an existing trusted input. An implementer must choose them or recover undocumented context. Deriving an expected account from the response being tested would reintroduce the old circular identity admission problem.
* Impact: A fail-closed implementation may stop unnecessarily, or an ad hoc implementation may admit an unexpected identity/origin/schema while claiming strict verification.
* Smallest useful change: Pin the trusted source/configuration keys for employee app origin, allowed provider origins and expected account, plus the required /api/me field paths/types and role/view-only rules. The expected account must come from preexisting authorized context, never the observed session or a display name. Fail before browser launch if these inputs are missing or ambiguous. Reference sensitive identity input by its guarded source, without printing it or adding it to evidence.
* Action owner: Planning parent, using already authorized configuration and the supplied C3 source pointers; runner maintainer enforces the contract.
* Exact resolving evidence: P01-T01 identifies the immutable configuration/source contract and concrete schema; constructed tests cover missing trusted inputs, wrong expected account, wrong role/provider, redirected/non-JSON/unsupported response and allowed authenticated-but-unbound response, without recording identity values.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction and bounded evidence completion. Do not invent identity values or expand scope; no new user decision is demonstrated by the supplied inputs.

### PC-003 [Medium]: Attempt and submission accounting is not defined across interrupted executions

* Related IDs: AC-01, P01-T01/P01-T02, validation lock; prior RV-004.
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-10-30/employee-acceptance-attempt-3-plan.md:42 counts sessions before navigation and :44 records intent before keypress. .copilot-tracking/details/2026-10-30/employee-acceptance-attempt-3-phase-details.md:28 preserves intent, :36 allows a second attempt only after safe definitive failure, and :43 produces evidence/manifest at completion. None specifies task-wide persisted admission state or what happens after a process interruption before completion evidence is written.
* Concern: The requirements establish ordering within a running process, but not whether a counted launch or uncertain submission survives interruption. A replacement invocation could reset an in-memory counter or treat a missing final record as no attempt. This is a missing lifecycle contract, not a claim that the unimplemented runner already retries.
* Impact: Task-wide at-most-two accounting and unknown-delivery evidence could be lost precisely on an abnormal path, permitting an unauthorized extra attempt or misleading known-not-submitted evidence.
* Smallest useful change: Require an exclusive task-scoped run guard and durable nonsecret launch/submission-intent state before the relevant side effects, using the existing single manifest allowance. An interrupted or unreadable prior state must fail closed with no automatic relaunch, replay or new authentication action; preserve the consumed count and uncertain delivery. Finalize hashes only after terminal evidence is frozen. This does not authorize another attempt.
* Action owner: Planning parent and runner maintainer.
* Exact resolving evidence: P01-T01/P01-T02 state the interruption/re-entry policy; fake-only tests cover interruption after launch admission and after submission intent, a second invocation against incomplete state, exhausted budget and competing invocation refusal. The file-count lock remains at most two evidence JSONs plus one manifest.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction. The caller's task-wide cap and no-uncertain-retry direction already determine the safe behavior; no significant or divergent user decision is required.
