### PC-001 [High]: Adding `conflicting` to `sensor_view.signature()` breaks same-bucket conflict downgrade in `Assistants._view()` and weakens the 6-hour proactive rate limit

* Related IDs: Issue #1288 AC 4, AC 6; Research `C4`, `C5`, `C6`; `P01`, `P01-T01`, `P02`, `P02-T01`, `P03-T02`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-14/quality-1288-sensor-ai-2142-phase-details.md (`P01-T01`, `P02-T01`, `P03-T02`); src/core/src/zava_forward/core/sensor_view.py:480-496; src/core/src/zava_forward/core/assistants.py:102-115, 257-303, 1022, 1054-1055, 1320-1340; src/api/tests/test_quality_sensors_c7.py:488; src/api/tests/test_quality_sensors_e1.py:255
* Concern: `P01-T01` adds `conflicting=bool(sensors.get("conflicting_readings"))` to `sensor_view.signature(sensors)`, which `assistants.scheduling_signature(snapshot)` embeds as the coarse 6-hour scheduling bucket (`src/core/src/zava_forward/core/assistants.py:107-115`). In `Assistants._view()` (`assistants.py:1022, 1054-1055`), `newer` requires `scheduling_signature(current) == scheduling_signature(body["snapshot"])`, and `keeps` requires `newer`. When a proactive flag is captured on clean readings (`conflicting=False`) and a conflicting reading subsequently arrives in the same 6-hour bucket (`conflicting=True` - or introduces an additional `provenance_class` into `classes`), `scheduling_signature` changes, `newer` and `keeps` become `False`, and `_view()` calls `review_flag(..., actionable=False, newer_readings=False, current=current)`. Because `P02-T01` evaluates `elif not actionable or state != "ready": status = "not-current"` ahead of `elif clashes: status = "conflicting-readings"`, `review_flag` returns `"not-current"` instead of `"conflicting-readings"`, defeating `P02-T01` and `P03-T02`'s own same-bucket conflict test. In addition, on cases already eligible through quality checklist gaps (`assistants.py:257-272`), toggling `conflicting` as readings enter and leave `WINDOW_MAX = 12` inside one 6-hour bucket changes `coarse` in `_due()` (`assistants.py:1320`), permitting extra proactive model invocations within a single 6-hour bucket. Meanwhile, exact capture staleness already uses `assistants.signature(snapshot)` (`assistants.py:102`), which hashes the full `snapshot`. Separately, `P02-T01` writes `review_flag(task, provenance=(), actionable=False, withheld=False, *, newer_readings=False, current=None)`, changing the existing `actionable=True` default and positional compatibility in `assistants.py:278` relied on by direct callers (`assistants.py:1055`, `test_quality_sensors_c7.py:488`, `test_quality_sensors_e1.py:255`).
* Impact: Causes same-bucket conflicting arrivals to render as generic `"not-current"` rather than `"conflicting-readings"` (failing AC 6 and `P03-T02`), opens a 6-hour proactive scan rate-limit bypass under sliding-window conflict churn (violating AC 4), and breaks existing positional/default callers of `review_flag()`.
* Smallest useful change: Leave `sensor_view.signature(sensors)` unchanged (do not add `conflicting` to the coarse 6-hour bucket signature), preserve `def review_flag(task, provenance=(), actionable=True, withheld=False, newer_readings=False, current=None):`, and in `P02-T01` ensure `Assistants._view()` / `review_flag()` surfaces `status="conflicting-readings"` (with `autonomous=False`, `level=None`) whenever `state == "ready"` and not `withheld` and either actionable `captured_conflicts` exist or `current_conflicts` exist for a task whose non-sensor scheduling anchor/release/epoch and 6-hour sensor bucket remain current (even if `newer` was cleared by a newly-arrived conflicting row's `provenance_class`).
* Action owner: planning_parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Revised `P01-T01`, `P02-T01`, and `P03-T02` in `.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-14/quality-1288-sensor-ai-2142-plan.md` and `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-14/quality-1288-sensor-ai-2142-phase-details.md` keeping `sensor_view.signature()` unchanged, preserving `review_flag()` parameter defaults, and specifying same-bucket `current_conflicts` precedence over `"not-current"`.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction; no user decision required.
