* Related IDs: `FR-02`, `AC-02`, `C2`, `P01`, `P01-T01`, `P01-T02`
* Evidence: `.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-c13-plan.md` (`FR-02`, `AC-02`), `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-c13-phase-details.md` (`P01-T01` item 2, `P01-T02` item 2), `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/regulatory_eval_gates.py` (`_REFUSAL_PATTERNS` lines 124-184, `ADVERSARIAL_PROMPTS` lines 193-203, `DEFAULT_EVAL_CASES` lines 205-345, `refuse_question` lines 617-627), and `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/chat_panel.py` (`normalise_policy_text` lines 216-220).
* Concern: `P01-T01` item 2 sketches `is/are ... (property|home|...) ... (legally |statutorily )?(non-?)?compliant`, `\bnon-?compliant\b`, `\bdoes\s+(?:this|the|our|my)\b[^.;!?]{0,60}\b(?:comply|satisfy|meet|violate|breach|pass\s+inspection)\b`, and `\b(?:have\s+we\s+met|has\s+(?:the\s+)?landlord\s+met|satisf(?:y|ies))\s+(?:our\s+|the\s+|this\s+|its\s+)?(?:legal\s+|statutory\s+)?(?:duty|...)s?\b`. Four issues remain:
  * Plural, past, modal, and `it`/`these`/`we` subjects bypass `\bdoes\s+(?:this|the|our|my)\b`: Prompts such as `"Do these works comply with the standard?"`, `"Do we comply with Awaab's Law?"`, `"Does it comply with the hazard rule?"`, `"Did the landlord breach the hazard timeline?"`, and `"Has this home breached Awaab's Law?"` are missed.
