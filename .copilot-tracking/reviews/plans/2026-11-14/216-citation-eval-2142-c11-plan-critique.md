### PC-002 [High]: Validate `expected` self-consistency in `_classify_refusal_confusion` and `_baseline_projection_matches` so corrupted ground truth cannot pass sub-1.0 gates as `"false_refusal"`

* Related IDs: `FR-01`, `FR-02`, `FR-03`, `C2`, `C3`, `C4`, `RV-023`, `RV-024`, `RV-025`, `P01-T01`, `P01-T02`
* Evidence: `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/regulatory_eval_gates.py` (`_inspect_bundle_surface` lines 764-785, `_classify_refusal_confusion` lines 1525-1538, `evaluate_citation_and_refusal_gates` lines 2065-2085, `_baseline_projection_matches` lines 2523-2538, `_score_structural_baseline` lines 2644-2667); `.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-c11-plan.md` (`FR-02`, `FR-03`); `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-c11-phase-details.md` (`P01-T01`, `P01-T02`)

Under owner-agreed sub-1.0 thresholds (`min_rates = 0.8`), a case whose `expected` violates `RV-023` (multi-card multi-provision ambiguity without `provision_ids`), `RV-024` (`kind="answer"`, `conflict=False` with `src@v1#0001` + `src@v2#0001`), or `RV-025` (`kind="conflict"` with non-empty or malformed `expected["facts"]`) paired with a canonical refusal `actual` is classified as `"false_refusal"` (`invalid_response == 0`, `dataset_gaps == []`), allowing both `citation_validity` and `refusal_accuracy` gates to return `PASS`. ### PC-003 [Medium]: Enforce `_active_bundle_nonconflicting` in `answer_contract`, guard malformed dict access and `@v` splitting, and reject versioned home-fact collisions
