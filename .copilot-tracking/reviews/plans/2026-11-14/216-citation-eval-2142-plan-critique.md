## Verdict

* Verdict: Revise
* Rationale: The candidate plan has a sound `src/core`-native architecture, clear ADR 0005 governance honesty, and strong test coverage, but four concrete contract mismatches and underspecified invariants across `P01-T01`, `P01-T02`, and `P02-T01` (notably `RegulatoryChat.bundle["cards"]` lacking per-card `provisions`, changed-source `conflict` turns having `cards = []`, `case_set_sha256` needing to exclude `actual` outputs, and zero-lift/review-effort/clock-free boundaries) could cause runtime or gate-logic defects if left implicit. All four findings are direct planner corrections (`planner_direct`) requiring no user input.

### PC-001 [High]: Reconcile `deterministic_rule_cards` and paired card-link parity with `RegulatoryChat.bundle` card, source, and conflict shapes

* Related IDs: Deliverable 2, C3, C4, P01, P01-T02, P02-T01
* Evidence: src/core/src/zava_forward/core/regulatory_chat.py:255-293, src/core/src/zava_forward/core/source_monitor.py:100-128, .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-phase-details.md (P01-T02)
* Concern: `P01-T02` specifies `deterministic_rule_cards(bundle: Mapping[str, Any])` extracting approved cards with nested `provisions`, and specifies `compare_chat_against_rule_cards` verifying that every expected source has a deterministic card link and provision link. In `RegulatoryChat.bundle` (`regulatory_chat.py:279-290`), each item in `bundle["cards"]` contains `{id, status, approved_by, approved_on, effective, exercise, link, safe_default, clock, source_freshness}` and has no `"provisions"` key; provisions live in top-level `bundle["sources"]`, while `card["source_freshness"]["sources"]` (`source_monitor.py:107-112`) records `source_id` entries. Furthermore, on a changed-source conflict (`regulatory_chat.py:255-272`), `citation_eligibility` is not `"active"`, so `RegulatoryChat.bundle` sets `result["conflict"] = True`, populates `result["sources"]` with old and new provisions, and executes `continue` before appending to `result["cards"]`, leaving `bundle["cards"] == []`.
* Impact: Reading `card["provisions"]` directly from `bundle["cards"]` fails or yields empty provision lists, and requiring active card links on `expected_kind == "conflict"` cases causes `DEFAULT_EVAL_CASES` (which includes changed-source conflict escalation) to always report `missing_link_case_ids`, making the planned positive `measured_value = True` / `"chat_with_cards"` test path in `P02-T01` unreachable.
* Smallest useful change: Update `P01-T02` so `deterministic_rule_cards(bundle)` projects top-level `provisions` from `bundle["sources"]` and populates each card's `provisions` by matching `source["id"].split("@", 1)[0]` against `{s.get("source_id") for s in card.get("source_freshness", {}).get("sources", []) if s.get("source_id")}` (falling back to `bundle["sources"]` when a single card has no mapped `source_id`), and scope active card-link + provision-link baseline parity in `compare_chat_against_rule_cards` to `expected_kind == "answer"` cases while verifying ` paired_cards` case-ID alignment without flagging changed-source `conflict` or `refused` cases as missing active card links.
* Action owner: planning_parent
* Exact resolving evidence: `P01-T02` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-phase-details.md` specifies `source_id`-correlated provision projection from `bundle["sources"]` in `deterministic_rule_cards` and scopes active card-link parity to `expected_kind == "answer"` cases.
* Decision route: planner_direct (direct planner correction; no user decision required)

### PC-002 [High]: Exclude `actual` outputs from `case_set_sha256` and bind `answer_contract`, `false_refusal`, and empty-bundle abstentions into per-check gate outcomes

* Related IDs: Deliverable 1, C1, C2, C3, C8, C10, P01, P01-T01, P02-T01
* Evidence: docs/adrs/0005-regulatory-compliance-assistant.md:296-303, docs/adrs/0005-regulatory-compliance-assistant.md:329-345, src/core/src/zava_forward/core/regulatory_chat.py:235-256, .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-plan.md (Functional Requirements, Acceptance Criteria), .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-phase-details.md (P01-T01, P02-T01)
* Concern: Three grading details in `P01-T01` are ambiguous: (1) `evaluate_citation_and_refusal_gates(cases, ..., agreement=None)` computes `case_set_sha256` over `cases`, which also carry `actual` runtime responses; if `actual` is included in the digest, `OwnerGateAgreement` cannot be pre-agreed before seeing model outputs (ADR 0005:343-345) and mutating `actual` in `P02-T01` negative tests invalidates the SHA into `"unknown"` instead of failing the gate with `"fail"`; (2) `grade_chat_turn` returns `citation_validity` and `answer_contract`, yet `GATE_RULES` has only `citation_validity` and `refusal_accuracy` (`overall_pass = None`), leaving unclear which gate fails if `answer_contract` is `False` (for example `requires_human = False`, hijacked `safe_next_action`/`who_decides` on source injection, or missing `out_of_cadence` card metadata) and how `citation_validity` avoids `0/0` on the all-refusal `adversarial` subset while `false_refusal` fails `refusal_accuracy`; (3) `RegulatoryChat.bundle` returns identical empty `sources = []`, `cards = []`, `conflict = False`, `refusal = None` for `missing`, `future`, `partial`, and `held` fixtures, so `render_extractive_answer` cannot recover four distinct source-state refusal strings from `bundle` alone.
* Impact: Including `actual` in `case_set_sha256` breaks output-mutation testing under agreed gates; leaving `answer_contract` unmapped lets template/stale-label/source-injection regressions slip past both `GateResult` records; and expecting distinct refusal reasons across `missing`/`future`/`partial`/`held` bundles causes `DEFAULT_EVAL_CASES` to fail against real `RegulatoryChat.bundle`.
* Smallest useful change: Clarify `P01-T01` so that: (1) `case_set_sha256` hashes only canonical case specifications (`id`, `set_name`, `scenario`, `question`, `expected`) plus `prompts`, excluding `actual`; (2) a case passes `citation_validity` iff `turn_grade["citation_validity"] and turn_grade["answer_contract"]` (where `expected_kind == "refused"` requires `actual["kind"] == "refused"` and `actual["citations"] == []`, keeping both `graded` and `adversarial` denominators equal to subset length), and `refusal_accuracy` counts a case accurate iff `refusal_confusion` is `correct_refusal` or `supported_response` (so `unsafe_non_refusal`, `false_refusal`, `invalid_response`, refused-turn `answer_contract` failure, or any answered prompt in `ADVERSARIAL_PROMPTS` fails `refusal_accuracy`); and (3) empty-bundle abstentions (`missing`, `future`, `partial`, `held`) use a consistent core refusal contract when `bundle["sources"]` is empty and `bundle["refusal"]` is `None`.
* Action owner: planning_parent
* Exact resolving evidence: `P01-T01` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-phase-details.md` documents the `actual`-excluded `case_set_sha256` canonical payload, exact per-set/combined `citation_validity` and `refusal_accuracy` pass predicates incorporating `answer_contract` and `false_refusal`, and unified empty-bundle refusal handling.
* Decision route: planner_direct (direct planner correction; no user decision required)

### PC-003 [Medium]: Specify strict positive paired lift, human review effort accounting, `rule_hazard_misses` fail-closed semantics, and clock-free time validation

* Related IDs: Deliverable 2, C1, C7, C8, C11, P01-T01, P01-T02, P02-T01
* Evidence: docs/adrs/0005-regulatory-compliance-assistant.md:5, docs/adrs/0005-regulatory-compliance-assistant.md:341-351, src/core/src/zava_forward/core/gates.py:82-92, src/core/src/zava_forward/core/regulatory_usage.py:53-73, src/core/tests/regulatory_fixture.py:17, .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-plan.md (Non-Functional Requirements, Acceptance Criteria), .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-phase-details.md (P01-T02)
* Concern: Four edge contracts in `P01-T01`/`P01-T02` need explicit bounds: (1) if `benefit_thresholds` sets non-regression floors (`min_completion_gain = 0.0`, `min_comprehension_gain = 0.0`, `max_elapsed_ratio = 1.0`), checking only `>=` and `<=` would mark a zero-lift tie (`chat == cards` on every metric) as `measured_value = True`, contradicting ADR 0005 ("added value over that baseline") and `P02-T01` ("zero/negative lift ... force `cards_only`"); (2) `observations` includes `review_seconds` and `economics()` requires `"Measured human review effort"`, but `P01-T02` does not state how `review_seconds` enters elapsed/effort ratio checks (`elapsed_seconds + review_seconds`) or observation pairing rules (exact 1:1 `cards` and `chat` rows per `(role, participant, task)` across `repairs`, `housing`, and `grants`, with `hazard_missed` flagged `critical` even on unpaired rows); (3) `rule_hazard_misses=None` in `compare_chat_against_rule_cards` is not distinguished from `0` (or guarded against `False == 0`); and (4) the NFR forbids clock calls, yet `gates.check(..., on=None)` calls `date.today()` (`gates.py:85`) and `compare_chat_against_rule_cards(..., now=None)` omits what happens when `now is None` during `authorise_model_run` / `cost_per_accepted_check`, while setting `GateRule.effective_date` later than `Fixture.NOW` (`2026-10-16`) would yield `UNKNOWN` on fixture dates.
* Impact: Without strict positive-lift and `elapsed_seconds + review_seconds` accounting, chat could ship on zero net benefit or hidden human review cost; without clock-free `now=None` fail-closed behavior and `effective_date = "2026-10-16"`, graders could call wall-clock time or fail unexpectedly at `Fixture.NOW`.
* Smallest useful change: Update `P01-T01` and `P01-T02` to: (1) require strict positive aggregate lift on at least one dimension (`completion_gain > 0`, `comprehension_gain > 0`, or total effort ratio `(elapsed_seconds + review_seconds)` `< 1.0`) in addition to meeting per-role `benefit_thresholds`, exact 1:1 `(role, participant, task)` pairing across all three roles, and unconditional `critical` counting of any `hazard_missed is True`; (2) require `type(rule_hazard_misses) is int and rule_hazard_misses == 0` for `measured_value = True` (treating `None` as unmeasured and `> 0` as critical failure); and (3) set `GATE_RULES` `effective_date = "2026-10-16"` (ADR 0005 date), construct `GateResult` without `date.today()`, and fail closed when `now is None` or timezone-naive in `authorise_model_run` and `cost_per_accepted_check`.
* Action owner: planning_parent
* Exact resolving evidence: `.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-plan.md` and `P01-T01`/`P01-T02` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-14/216-citation-eval-2142-phase-details.md` state strict positive-lift + `review_seconds` effort checks, 1:1 role pairing, `type(rule_hazard_misses) is int` fail-closed handling, `effective_date = "2026-10-16"`, and clock-free `now=None` fail-closed semantics.
* Decision route: planner_direct (direct planner correction; no user decision required)
