## Verdict

* Verdict: Revise
* Rationale: The additive module split (`discovery_contract.py` plus backward-compatible enrichments to `discovery_register.py` and `discovery_flow.py`) and KB documentation design are sound, but four planner-correctable gaps remain: (1) `P01-T01` allows `discovery_register.Constraint` to emit `owner_validated=True` and `finding_status="owner_validated_company_finding"` after domain-only `decide()` without a nominated discovery owner or `contradicting_evidence` item; (2) `P01-T02` conflates per-hypothesis acceptance with register/pack sign-off when hypotheses are legitimately `DISPUTED`/refuted, omits boolean enforcement on the 5-part constraint test, and leaves direct `NominatedDiscoveryOwner` construction unchecked; (3) `P01-T02` flow evaluation omits active-time non-imputation, `paused`/`cancelled` states, zero-event candidate retention, and `BG-011` `accepted handover` prerequisite checks; and (4) `P03-T02` hardcodes `/tmp` for virtualenv setup and blurs `rpi-implement` vs `rpi-review` commit sequencing under the repository no-amend rule.

### PC-001 [High]: `P01-T01` promotes `discovery_register.Constraint` to `owner_validated=True` on domain-only `decide()` without nominated discovery owner sign-off or `contradicting_evidence`

* Related IDs: `FR-4`, `FR-5`, `AC-3`, `AC-4`, `P01-T01`, `P02-T01`, `C1`, `C3`, `C7`, `C8`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-plan.md, .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-phase-details.md, src/core/src/zava_forward/core/discovery_register.py, src/core/src/zava_forward/core/discovery_flow.py, src/core/tests/test_discovery_register.py
* Concern: Caller Objective 2, `FR-5`, and `AC-4` require every discovery output across `discovery_contract`, `discovery_register`, and `discovery_flow` to carry `owner_validated=False` and `finding_status="candidate_hypothesis_not_company_finding"` until a nominated human discovery owner (with `ENDPOINT_AGREED` on `"accepted handover"`, a validated dictionary, and both supporting and contradicting evidence) records formal validation, raising `GovernanceViolationError` on any invalid promotion. In `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/discovery_register.py`, `decide()` validates only a domain role without any `DiscoveryOwner` or dictionary check, accepts placeholder/AI validator strings, permits `no_contradiction_note` when `contradicting_evidence` is empty, and allows direct construction `Constraint(..., status="VALIDATED", validated_by="x")` with zero evidence. Under `P01-T01`, deriving `Constraint.owner_validated` and `Constraint.finding_status` directly from `status == VALIDATED` promotes `Constraint` and `register_rows()` outputs to `"owner_validated_company_finding"` without a nominated discovery owner or `contradicting_evidence`, while changing `decide()` itself would break `src/core/tests/test_discovery_register.py` assertions on legacy `is_company_finding`. In addition, `P01-T01` adds governance-stamped `loop_summary()` in `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/discovery_flow.py` but leaves `leg_days()` without an equivalent stamped `leg_summary()` helper.
* Impact: Callers of `discovery_register.decide()`, `Constraint(...)`, or `register_rows()` can produce `owner_validated=True` and `finding_status="owner_validated_company_finding"` without any nominated discovery owner, agreed endpoint, validated dictionary, human-actor check, or `contradicting_evidence` record, bypassing the core Issue #124 governance guard.
* Smallest useful change: Update `P01-T01` and `P02-T01` so `discovery_register.Constraint` preserves legacy `is_company_finding` for existing `decide()` tests, while keeping `owner_validated: bool = False` and `finding_status == "candidate_hypothesis_not_company_finding"` until an explicit nominated-discovery-owner validation helper confirms `DiscoveryOwner.status() == "ENDPOINT_AGREED"`, zero `dictionary_gaps`, non-empty `supporting_evidence` and `contradicting_evidence`, and non-placeholder/non-AI human actors (with `__post_init__` raising `GovernanceViolationError` on forged `owner_validated=True`), and add `leg_summary()` alongside `loop_summary()` in `discovery_flow.py`.
* Action owner: planning_parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Revised `P01-T01` and `P02-T01` in `.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-plan.md` and `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-phase-details.md` decouple legacy `is_company_finding` from nominated-owner `owner_validated` / `finding_status`, enforce `GovernanceViolationError` on invalid promotion in `discovery_register.py`, and include `leg_summary()` in `discovery_flow.py`.
* Decision route: direct_planner_correction

### PC-002 [High]: `P01-T02` governance guard conflates hypothesis acceptance with register/pack sign-off when candidate constraints are refuted/`DISPUTED`, omits 5-part constraint test pass checks, and leaves direct `NominatedDiscoveryOwner` construction unchecked

* Related IDs: `FR-1`, `FR-4`, `FR-5`, `AC-1`, `AC-3`, `AC-4`, `P01-T02`, `P02-T01`, `C1`, `C3`, `C13`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-plan.md, .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-phase-details.md, docs/brds/retrofit-flow-discovery-brief.md
* Concern: Three contract rules in `P01-T02` are ambiguous or incomplete: (1) `P01-T02` states one blanket rule across every report, hypothesis, register, and pack requiring "non-disputed domain validation" for `owner_validated=True`. Because the 5 seed constraints in `docs/brds/retrofit-flow-discovery-brief.md` are candidate hypotheses to test rather than pre-confirmed facts, contradicting evidence will legitimately leave one or more hypotheses `DISPUTED`/rejected while confirming others; requiring all 5 hypotheses to be accepted/non-disputed makes a realistic `RankedConstraintRegister` or `Phase0DiscoveryPack` impossible to validate, whereas pack sign-off must also never flip `DISPUTED` or `PROPOSED` child hypotheses to `owner_validated=True`, and `LifecycleDictionaryReport` / `FlowDiscoveryReport` need explicit report-level validation rules independent of constraint hypotheses. (2) `FR-4` and `P01-T02` require a `ConstraintTestAssessment` object before acceptance, without requiring all 5 criteria from `docs/brds/retrofit-flow-discovery-brief.md` (`Constraint Test`) to be `True` when accepting/promoting a hypothesis. (3) `FR-1` requires placeholder, AI/automated, and self-nominated (`nominated_by == owner`) owner records to report non-agreed status, but `P01-T02` specifies actor checks on helper constructors without requiring `NominatedDiscoveryOwner.status()` itself to reject direct dataclass instantiation with invalid actors; additionally, the plan (`validate_constraint_hypothesis`) and phase details (`validate_constraint_by_domain_owner` / `validate_constraint_by_discovery_owner`) name different functions.
* Impact: Implementing the blanket pack invariant either dead-locks `validate_discovery_pack()` whenever contradicting evidence refutes any of the 5 seed hypotheses, or erroneously promotes `DISPUTED`/failed-constraint-test hypotheses or direct-constructed placeholder `NominatedDiscoveryOwner` instances.
* Smallest useful change: Revise `FR-1`, `FR-4`, `FR-5`, `P01-T02`, and `P02-T01` to align helper names and define explicit per-artifact promotion rules: (a) `NominatedDiscoveryOwner.status()` enforces human, non-placeholder, non-AI, and `nominated_by != owner` rules directly; (b) `ConstraintHypothesis` acceptance/promotion requires non-empty `supporting_evidence` and `contradicting_evidence`, all 5 `ConstraintTestAssessment` booleans `True`, role-matched human domain-owner acceptance (`!= proposed_by`), `ENDPOINT_AGREED`, zero-gap dictionary, and exact nominated-discovery-owner sign-off (`!= proposed_by`); and (c) `RankedConstraintRegister` and `Phase0DiscoveryPack` validation requires `ENDPOINT_AGREED`, zero-gap dictionary, all hypotheses adjudicated with two-sided evidence (zero unresolved `PROPOSED` hypotheses, while any `DISPUTED` hypotheses remain visibly preserved inside the validated register/pack with `hypothesis.owner_validated=False` and `hypothesis.finding_status="candidate_hypothesis_not_company_finding"`), and nominated-discovery-owner sign-off across dictionary, flow report, and register.
* Action owner: planning_parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Plan `P01-T02` and phase details `P01-T02` / `P02-T01` specify aligned function names, direct actor validation inside `NominatedDiscoveryOwner.status()`, all-`True` 5-part `ConstraintTestAssessment` gating on acceptance, and distinct report/hypothesis/register/pack governance invariants that preserve `DISPUTED` child hypotheses as non-findings inside a validated pack.
* Decision route: direct_planner_correction

### PC-003 [Medium]: `P01-T02` flow dictionary and `evaluate_property_flow` omit active-time non-imputation, `paused`/`cancelled` states, zero-event candidate retention, and `BG-011` `accepted handover` prerequisites

* Related IDs: `FR-2`, `FR-3`, `AC-2`, `P01-T02`, `P02-T01`, `C1`, `C2`, `C9`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-plan.md, .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-phase-details.md, docs/brds/retrofit-flow-discovery-brief.md, docs/brds/agentic-retrofit-programme-brd.md
* Concern: Four Phase 0 flow requirements in `docs/brds/retrofit-flow-discovery-brief.md` and `docs/brds/agentic-retrofit-programme-brd.md` are omitted or underspecified in `P01-T02`: (1) Brief `Time Attribution` item 2 requires calculating *"Active working time where reliable start and finish evidence exists"*, and `Lifecycle events` includes `pause` and `restart`, yet `P01-T02` defines only 6 `TimestampSpec` entries without `active_work_started_at` / `active_work_completed_at`, risking `active_days` being imputed as `calendar_days - wait_days` when active start/finish timestamps are absent; (2) Brief `Discovery Proposition` explicitly names *"incomplete, paused, excluded, cancelled and reopened cases"*, while `P01-T02` terminal/exception states list only `excluded`, `withdrawn`, and `reopened_aftercare`, omitting `paused` and `cancelled`; (3) Brief `Population` requires keeping candidate properties identified in the baseline window even when they have zero downstream stage/event records; and (4) BRD `BG-011` (`docs/brds/agentic-retrofit-programme-brd.md`, lines 693-697) specifies that `accepted handover` requires preceding professional approval, resident decision, physical and documentary quality acceptance, accessible resident handover, and no unresolved defect/reopen, with intended energy outcome tracked separately, which `evaluate_property_flow` does not check before counting `accepted handover`.
* Impact: Flow evaluation could impute unrecorded active working time, reject or drop `paused`, `cancelled`, or zero-event candidate homes, or count an `accepted handover` timestamp that lacks required preceding quality/resident/design events.
* Smallest useful change: Update `FR-2`, `FR-3`, `P01-T02`, and `P02-T01` to add active-work start/finish timestamp specs (returning `active_days=None` rather than `calendar - wait` when active timestamps are absent), add `paused` and `cancelled` exception/terminal states, accept baseline candidate property records in `evaluate_property_flow` so zero-event homes stay visible, and flag an `accepted handover` event recorded without preceding `design approved`, `resident works decision recorded`, `physical acceptance`, `evidence acceptance`, and `resident handover` events (or with an unresolved post-handover `reopen`) in `gaps` while keeping intended energy outcome separate.
* Action owner: planning_parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `FR-2`, `FR-3`, `P01-T02`, and `P02-T01` specify active-work timestamp specs with `None` non-imputation, `paused` and `cancelled` states, baseline candidate retention, and `BG-011` `accepted handover` prerequisite gap checks.
* Decision route: direct_planner_correction

### PC-004 [Medium]: `P03-T02` hardcodes a `/tmp` virtualenv path and creates a self-referential commit/amend conflict across `rpi-implement` and `rpi-review`

* Related IDs: `NFR-2`, `AC-5`, `AC-6`, `P03-T02`, `C12`, `C14`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-plan.md, .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-phase-details.md, .github/copilot-instructions.md
* Concern: First, `What You May Not Know`, `NFR-2`, `Dependencies`, and `P03-T02` mandate creating a Python 3.12 virtualenv under `/tmp` and running `pip install -e "src/core[dev]"`. Execution environments that forbid writing to `/tmp` or lack outbound PyPI access for build isolation will fail even when Python 3.12, `ruff`, and `mypy` are available locally or via a workspace/home-directory virtualenv with `--no-build-isolation --no-deps`. Second, `P03-T02` runs during `rpi-implement` before `rpi-review`, yet instructs committing *"after `rpi-review` artifacts are finalized (or updated cleanly so the final branch commit holds all RPI tracking artifacts)"* AND recording the verified commit SHA inside `.copilot-tracking/changes/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-changes.md` while ending with an empty `git status --short`. Editing `changes.md` after committing to insert that commit's SHA dirties the worktree, and amending the commit after `rpi-review` violates `.github/copilot-instructions.md` (*"obtain the owner's permission before amending an existing commit"*).
* Impact: `P03-T02` can block on no-`/tmp` sandbox rules or offline `pip` build isolation, or cause either a dirty working tree or a policy-violating `git commit --amend`.
* Smallest useful change: Update `What You May Not Know`, `NFR-2`, `Dependencies`, and `P03-T02` to: (a) avoid `/tmp` completely by reusing an available Python 3.12 toolchain or creating a temporary workspace-local / `~/.cache` virtualenv (using `pip install --no-build-isolation --no-deps -e src/core` when `ruff` and `mypy` are already installed, then removing temporary venv and `src/core/src/*.egg-info` before `git add`), and (b) specify non-amended additive commits: commit code, tests, docs, and `changes.md` (recording validation command outputs before committing rather than self-referencing the commit's own SHA) at the end of `P03-T02`, then record `rpi-review` artifacts in a second additive commit with the Copilot trailer.
* Action owner: planning_parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Revised plan and phase details remove `/tmp` paths, specify `--no-build-isolation` editable install + worktree cleanup, and define two clean, non-amended local commits across `rpi-implement` and `rpi-review`.
