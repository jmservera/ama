## Verdict

* Verdict: Revise
* Rationale: The overall architecture (exercising authoritative `Store` and `SqlStore` transitions rather than a parallel simulator, paired with fail-closed hardening in `gates.py`, `store.py`, and `resident_decisions.py`) is sound, well-scoped, and directly proves both `#81` exercise-scenario invariants. Every finding is a bounded, direct planner correction requiring zero user decisions.

### PC-002 [High]: Fail-closed bypasses in `P01-T01` for truthy non-boolean `released` values and malformed legacy `safeguarding` fields

* Related IDs: `FR-PLAN-01`, `AC-1`, `P01`, `P01-T01`, `P03-T01`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/issue-81-safety-consent-gates-phase-details.md (`P01-T01`); src/core/src/zava_forward/core/taskqueues.py:160-185; src/core/src/zava_forward/core/gates.py:85-98, 249-260; src/core/src/zava_forward/core/store.py:234-257; src/core/src/zava_forward/core/synthetic/resident_preview_fixture.py:88
* Concern: `P01-T01` states that `is_open_safety_item(item)` treats a safeguarding item as closed *"only when `released is True` or normalized `status in {"closed", "released", "resolved"}`"*, but `taskqueues.is_open_safety_item` (`taskqueues.py:182`) actually checks `if bool(item.get("released")): return False`, which treats non-empty strings such as `"false"`, `"no"`, `"pending"`, or integer `1` as closed. Delegating directly to `is_open_safety_item` in `gates.booking_gates` and `Store._open_safety_items` violates `gates.check`'s strict `state is True` boolean contract. Second, `P01-T01` guards malformed `safeguarding_items` in `Store._open_safety_items` but omits malformed legacy `case["safeguarding"]` (for example `safeguarding="corrupt"`, `safeguarding=123`, or `safeguarding=["corrupt"]`), which `taskqueues.raw_safeguarding_items` silently drops to `[]`, allowing `Store.require_safety_clear` to pass.
* Impact: Cases with `released="false"` / `released="pending"` or malformed legacy `safeguarding` values can pass `gates.booking_gates` and/or `Store.require_safety_clear`, violating `FR-PLAN-01` and `AC-1`.
* Smallest useful change: Revise `P01-T01` so `gates.booking_gates` and `Store._open_safety_items` enforce strict boolean `released` semantics (`released` when present and not `None` must be `bool`, closing only on `released is True`, or when `released is None` and `isinstance(status, str)` with `status.strip().casefold() in {"closed", "released", "resolved"}` for standalone safeguarding items; non-boolean `released` fails closed as `UNKNOWN` in `booking_gates` and unverified safety in `_open_safety_items`), and fail closed when `"safeguarding"` is present and is anything other than `None`, a note-only `dict` disjoint from `SAFEGUARDING_ITEM_KEYS` (preserving `resident_preview_fixture.py:88`), an item `dict`, or a `list` of `dict`s. Include both edge cases in `P03-T01`.
* Action owner: `planning_parent`
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `P01-T01` and `P03-T01` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/issue-81-safety-consent-gates-phase-details.md` specifying strict boolean `released` validation and malformed `case["safeguarding"]` fail-closed guards.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction

### PC-003 [High]: Wall-clock nondeterminism (`as_of` / `now` propagation) and missing `RESIDENT_FIELDS` retention entry in `P01-T02` and `P02-T01`

* Related IDs: `FR-PLAN-02`, `FR-PLAN-03`, `NFR Determinism and Offline Execution`, `AC-2`, `P01-T02`, `P02-T01`, `P03-T01`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/issue-81-safety-consent-gates-plan.md (`Non-Functional Requirements` -> `Determinism and Offline Execution`); .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/issue-81-safety-consent-gates-phase-details.md (`P01-T02`, `P02-T01`); src/core/src/zava_forward/core/resident_decisions.py:42-44, 960-1052, 1079-1097; src/core/src/zava_forward/core/gates.py:85-98, 232-274
* Concern: Although `P01-T02` gives `validate_authorised_works_exception(case, measure, context, *, now=None)` an explicit `now` parameter, neither `resident_gate(c, bindings=None)` nor `_resident_decision_gate(c, bindings=None)` accepts optional `now`, so `retention_schedule(c)`, `authority_for(...)`, and `validate_authorised_works_exception(...)` inside `_resident_decision_gate` evaluate against host wall clock (`controls.today()` / `datetime.now(UTC)`) even when `verify_exercise_safety_and_consent_gates(as_of="2026-11-15")` pins `as_of`. Similarly, if the returned verification summary includes raw mutation/audit timestamps (`_now()`) or temporary workspace paths, repeated calls across a clock tick will fail the NFR structural-equality requirement. In addition, `P01-T02` introduces `case["authorised_works_exceptions"]` without adding `"authorised_works_exceptions"` to `RESIDENT_FIELDS` (`resident_decisions.py:42-44`), leaving resident-specific exception and notification records unpurged by `delete_resident_records`.
* Impact: Verification results can drift with the host system clock, fail repeated-run equality assertions in `P03-T01`, and retain resident exception payloads past retention deletion.
* Smallest useful change: Update `P01-T02` to (1) add `"authorised_works_exceptions"` to `RESIDENT_FIELDS` and reject memory/sensor references (`controls.is_memory_reference`, `controls.SENSOR_REF`) in exception `rule_source`/`reason`/`notification_ref`, and (2) add optional keyword-only `*, now: str | None = None` to `resident_gate` and `_resident_decision_gate` (defaulting to `None` for backward compatibility and threading normalized `today`/`now` to `retention_schedule`, `authority_for`, and `validate_authorised_works_exception`). Update `P02-T01` so `as_of` (normalized to ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` and deterministic UTC instant `f"{as_of}T12:00:00+00:00"` when date-only) is passed to `resident_gate(..., now=...)`, `validate_authorised_works_exception(..., now=...)`, and `gates.booking_gates(..., on=as_of_day)`, Store-transition fixtures use wide deterministic validity/expiry bounds (e.g., `2099-01-01` vs `2025-01-01`), and the returned report omits wall-clock timestamps and temp directory paths.
* Action owner: `planning_parent`
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `P01-T02` and `P02-T01` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/issue-81-safety-consent-gates-phase-details.md` specifying `RESIDENT_FIELDS` registration, optional `now` threading in `resident_gate` / `_resident_decision_gate`, `as_of` normalization, and wall-clock-free report payloads.
