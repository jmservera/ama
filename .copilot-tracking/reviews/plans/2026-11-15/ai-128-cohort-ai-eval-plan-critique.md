### PC-001 [High]: Non-human, placeholder, or expired approvals and non-human dispositions can still set `activation_allowed=True` and `fairness_evidence=True`

* Related IDs: FR-5, AC-6, AC-7, C2, C6, C7, C11, P01-T02, P02-T01, P02-T02
* Evidence: `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/cohort_eval.py:175-206,473-498`, `src/core/tests/test_cohort_eval.py:90-96,127-137,246-250`, `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/ai-128-cohort-ai-eval-phase-details.md` (`P02-T01`, `P02-T02`)
* Concern: In `cohort_eval.py:175-187`, `_thresholds_approved(manifest)` checks only `named(approval.get(k))` (not `named_human(approval.get(k))`), ignores `_has_invalid_or_expired_date(approval, now)`, and accepts placeholder strings such as `"unapproved"` or `"to be named"` whenever all three `OWNER_KEYS` are populated. As a result, a manifest whose `_owner_approval_status` is `"unapproved"` (`lawful_analysis_fields_approved == False`) still passes `_thresholds_approved(manifest) == True`, gets `activation_allowed == True`, and on `synthetic=False` with zero blockers still gets `fairness_evidence == True` even after `P02-T02` replaces `MEASUREMENT_BLOCKERS` with `not blockers`. In addition, `cohort_eval.py:473-478` validates `disposition["by"]` with `named(...)` instead of `named_human(...)`, allowing a system/agent string (for example `"AI agent"` or `"Copilot"`) to accept `DISPOSABLE` blockers (`threshold_exceeded`, `unjustified_exclusion`) and flip `activation_allowed` to `True`. Neither `P02-T02` nor `P02-T01` tightens these human-approver checks or validates optional threshold keys (`max_abstention_rate`) inside `_thresholds_approved`.
* Impact: Violates AC-6 and the caller requirement to keep `fairness_evidence=False` and `activation_allowed=False` whenever independent human/policy/threshold approval is absent. Note that fictional human labels in existing fixtures (`"Data lead (fictional)"` in `cohort_eval_fixture.py:49` and `"RAI owner"` in `test_cohort_eval.py:130`) already satisfy `named_human(...)` (`owner_approval_status == "pending_owner_approval"`), so upgrading to `named_human` plus placeholder/date rejection preserves all existing offline tests (`test_cohort_eval.py:14,127,246`) while closing the non-human/placeholder/expired bypass.
* Smallest useful change: Update `P02-T02` (and `P01-T02` assertions) so `_thresholds_approved(manifest, now)` requires `all(named_human(approval.get(k)) for k in OWNER_KEYS)`, rejects non-human/unapproved placeholder tokens (`"unapproved"`, `"unknown"`, `"todo"`, `"tbd"`, `"to be named"`), rejects `_has_invalid_or_expired_date(approval, now)`, and validates any supplied optional rate threshold (`max_abstention_rate`, `max_abstention_gap`) with `_limit(...)`; require `owner_approval_status != OWNER_APPROVAL_UNAPPROVED` for `fairness_evidence`; and require `named_human(disposition.get("by"))` for valid human disposition in both `cohort_eval.py` and `paired_equity.py`.
* Action owner: planner (`planning_parent`)
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `P01-T02`, `P02-T01`, and `P02-T02` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/ai-128-cohort-ai-eval-phase-details.md` specifying `named_human`, placeholder/expired-date rejection in `_thresholds_approved` and `disposition`, optional threshold limit validation, and `owner_approval_status != "unapproved"` gating on `fairness_evidence`.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction (does not contradict confirmed user decisions)

### PC-002 [High]: `cohort_eval` allows omitting `ranking_policy` on AI/ranking tasks and `paired_equity` leaves `policy` execution and `ai_task` output contracts underspecified

* Related IDs: FR-2, AC-2, AC-6, C1, C2, C4, C12, P01-T01, P01-T02, P02-T01, P02-T02
* Evidence: `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/paired_equity.py:99-103,129`, `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/ranking.py:114-124,151-178`, `src/agents/src/zava_forward/agents/cohort_ai_eval.py:67-84,99-118`, `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/ai-128-cohort-ai-eval-phase-details.md` (`P02-T01`, `P02-T02`)
* Concern: Three Criterion 2 gaps remain across the two target modules:
  1. In `P02-T02`, `cohort_eval.evaluate` verifies `ranking_policy` only when `manifest.get("ranking_policy")` or `ranking_policy` kwarg is supplied, whereas `non_ai_comparison` is required when `manifest.get("task_kind") == "ai_task"`. An `ai_task` manifest (or a manifest supplying `non_ai_observed` / `task_kind in {"ranking", "prioritisation"}`) that omits `ranking_policy` altogether would bypass `unapproved_ranking_policy` and still obtain `activation_allowed=True` (and `fairness_evidence=True` if `synthetic=False`). Existing callers (`cohort_eval_fixture.py`, `cohort_ranking_eval.py`, `cohort_ai_eval.py`) do not set `task_kind` or `non_ai_observed`, so requiring `ranking_policy` whenever `task_kind in {"ai_task", "ranking", "prioritisation"}` or `non_ai_observed` / `non_ai_baseline` is present preserves 100% backward compatibility while closing the omission bypass.
  2. In `P02-T02`, `ai_regression_vs_non_ai` is phrased as "when AI increases unjustified exclusions/deprioritisations over the deterministic non-AI baseline", which could be implemented as an aggregate count check rather than per-case and per-subgroup regression detection (including AI lowering service or abstaining without deterministic fallback on a case that passed in `non_ai_observed`), and optional `non_ai_observed` / `ranking_policy` kwargs must be bound into `input_digests` when supplied.
  3. In `P02-T01`, `paired_equity.py:99,129` currently runs `ranking.rank` with default `ranking.CURRENT` instead of binding a caller-supplied `policy` when `rank is None` (catching malformed policy rules fail-closed as `ranking_policy:invalid_policy`), and `P02-T01` does not define the accepted `ai_task` callable return contract (supporting both `Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]]` ranked item lists and `Mapping[str, Any]` task envelopes carrying `items`, `status`, `abstained`, `fallback_to_non_ai`/`source`, `service_level`, and `inferences`).
* Impact: Without closing the `ranking_policy` omission bypass, per-case/per-subgroup non-AI regression check, and `policy` / `ai_task` execution contract, AI evaluations can pass without ranking-policy ownership/hash/rule-order approval or misalign between P01 tests and P02 implementation.
* Smallest useful change: Specify in `P02-T02` that `ranking_policy` is mandatory (emitting non-disposable `unapproved_ranking_policy` when absent) whenever `manifest.get("task_kind") in {"ai_task", "ranking", "prioritisation"}` or `non_ai_observed` / `manifest.get("non_ai_baseline")` is supplied, that `ai_regression_vs_non_ai` checks per-case and per-subgroup regressions against `non_ai_observed`, and that supplied kwargs are recorded in `input_digests` when non-`None`; specify in `P02-T01` that `rank is None` binds validated `policy` into `ranking.rank` and document both `Sequence` and envelope `Mapping` return shapes for `ai_task`.
* Action owner: planner (`planning_parent`)
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `P02-T01`, `P02-T02`, `P01-T01`, and `P01-T02` sections in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/ai-128-cohort-ai-eval-phase-details.md` locking mandatory `ranking_policy` conditions, per-case/per-subgroup `non_ai_comparison` regression rules, `policy`-bound default ranker execution, and `ai_task` return contracts.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction (does not contradict confirmed user decisions)

### PC-003 [High]: Digest-bound `protected_characteristic_approval` and `context_dimension_approvals` create a circular SHA-256 self-invalidation with `manifest_digest`

* Related IDs: FR-6, AC-7, AC-8, C2, C9, C11, P01-T01, P01-T02, P02-T01, P02-T02
* Evidence: `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/cohort_eval.py:161-174`, `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/paired_equity.py:156`, `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/ai-128-cohort-ai-eval-phase-details.md` (`P01-T01`, `P01-T02`, `P02-T01`, `P02-T02`)
* Concern: `cohort_eval.manifest_digest(manifest)` (`cohort_eval.py:161-163`) hashes `{k: v for k, v in manifest.items() if k != "approval"}`. `P01-T02` (tests 5 and 6) and `P02-T02` require digest-bound `protected_characteristic_approval` and `context_dimension_approvals` on the manifest alongside `approval`. If `manifest_digest` excludes only `"approval"`, inserting `"manifest_digest": manifest_digest(manifest)` inside `manifest["protected_characteristic_approval"]` or `manifest["context_dimension_approvals"]` changes `manifest_digest(manifest)` itself, making self-consistent binding impossible and invalidating `manifest["approval"]["manifest_digest"]` (`unapproved_thresholds`) whenever approvals are attached in sequence. Three tightly coupled AC-7/AC-8 contract details are also left implicit:
  1. `paired_equity.py` (`P01-T01` test 6, `P02-T01`) requires digest-bound `protected_fields_approval` (and `context_approvals` / `expectation_approval`) without exposing a public `input_digest(pairs, anchors)` helper.
  2. Neither task enumerates the exact `PROTECTED_CHARACTERISTIC_FIELDS` set, risking omission of UK Equality Act 2010 characteristics (`age`, `disability`, `gender_reassignment`, `marriage_and_civil_partnership`, `marital_status`, `pregnancy`, `maternity`, `pregnancy_and_maternity`, `race`, `ethnicity`, `ethnic_origin`, `nationality`, `religion`, `belief`, `religion_or_belief`, `sex`, `gender`, `sexual_orientation`) or false-positive blocking on operational fields present in existing fixtures (`priority_support`, `adjustment`, `support_need`, `support_or_contact_need`, `notes`).
  3. In `cohort_eval.py:190-217`, `_owner_approval_status` and `_live_gaps` check only `manifest.get("synthetic") is not False`; if `synthetic=False` is combined with `generated=True` or `cohort_origin in {"generated", "constructed", "synthetic"}` (`P01-T02` test 5), `_owner_approval_status` and `live_activation_allowed` could still treat the generated cohort as live-approved, and `P02-T02` says `population_fairness_established: False when generated/synthetic` rather than `population_fairness_established: False` unconditionally (sample cohort evaluations never establish population fairness).
* Impact: Without excluding all approval-envelope keys from `manifest_digest` (and providing `paired_equity.input_digest`), P01/P02 approval tests will hit circular digest mismatches; without locking `PROTECTED_CHARACTERISTIC_FIELDS` and generated-cohort live-approval guards, fixtures can false-positive or leak `live_activation_allowed=True` on generated cohorts.
* Smallest useful change: Exclude `"approval"`, `"protected_characteristic_approval"`, and `"context_dimension_approvals"` in `cohort_eval.manifest_digest` (which is byte-identical for all existing manifests lacking the two new keys) and optionally provide helper binders; expose `paired_equity.input_digest(pairs, anchors)` for digest-binding `protected_fields_approval`, `context_approvals`, and `expectation_approval`; lock the exact `PROTECTED_CHARACTERISTIC_FIELDS` set excluding operational adjustment fields; and treat `_is_generated_cohort(manifest)` identically to `synthetic` in `_owner_approval_status`, `_live_gaps`, `export_record`, and unconditional `population_fairness_established: False`.
* Action owner: planner (`planning_parent`)
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `P02-T01` and `P02-T02` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/ai-128-cohort-ai-eval-phase-details.md` defining approval-envelope exclusion in `manifest_digest`, `paired_equity.input_digest`, the exact `PROTECTED_CHARACTERISTIC_FIELDS` tuple, `_is_generated_cohort` live-gap enforcement, and `population_fairness_established: False`.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction (does not contradict confirmed user decisions)

### PC-004 [High]: `cohort_eval` `service_lowered` and `prohibited_inference` checks miss `case["cohorts"]` schemas and do not propagate to subgroup `errors`/`failed` or `detected_defects`
