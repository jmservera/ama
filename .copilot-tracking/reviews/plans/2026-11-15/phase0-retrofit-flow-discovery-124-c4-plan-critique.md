* Verdict: Revise
* Rationale: The c4 plan's core architecture (unkeyed in-process provenance seals paired with `hmac`-SHA256 `signing_key` cross-process authentication, 5-shape `Mapping` governance guards, non-blank dictionary schema digests, and full-sequence `lifecycle_dictionary_gaps` validation) directly targets `RV-001` through `RV-005`, but four substantive specification flaws and loopholes (`PC-001` through `PC-004`) would either reject valid `ConstraintHypothesis` mappings at runtime or leave bypass paths open during adversarial review unless corrected before `rpi-implement`.

### PC-001 [High]: Hypothesis `Mapping` role-check bug (`domain_validated_by` vs `domain_validated_role`) and mixed-shape `Mapping` classification collision bypass

* Related IDs: `RV-002`, `RV-004`, `FR-C4-2`, `AC-C4-2`, `P01-T01`, `P02-T01`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-plan.md (`FR-C4-2`), .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-phase-details.md (`P01-T01` item 3), and src/core/src/zava_forward/core/discovery_contract.py (`ConstraintHypothesis.__post_init__` at lines 1569-1610, `enforce_governance_guard` at lines 1435-1565)
* Concern: First, both `FR-C4-2` and `P01-T01` item 3 specify checking `domain_validated_by in domain_owner_roles`, whereas `domain_validated_by` holds a human name (for example `"Domain Owner 1"`) and `domain_validated_role` holds the role matched against `domain_owner_roles` (`discovery_contract.py:1597-1598`); in addition, `FR-C4-2` reads `discovery_owner_validated_by` only when `owner_validated_by` is absent, letting a malicious hypothesis mapping supply `"owner_validated_by": "Dev Patel"` alongside a proposer or invalid `"discovery_owner_validated_by"` at top-level check time, and `_is_hypothesis_mapping` omits `"rank"`, `"code"`, `"summary"`, `"intervention_class"`, `"review_status"`, `"domain_validated_*"`, `"discovery_owner_validated_*"`, and `"decision_note"`, allowing candidate mappings (`owner_validated=False`) with `"review_status": "OWNER_VALIDATED"` to evade `_verify_hypothesis_mapping`. Second, `P01-T01` item 3 defines five independent `any(k in m ...)` shape predicates without rejecting mappings that match more than one shape or carry unexpected keys, so a malicious dict mixing keys from two shapes (for example a valid `LifecycleDictionaryReport` dict plus forged `accepted_handover_property_ids` or `hypotheses`) bypasses the second shape's verifier under `if/elif` dispatch or smuggles unverified attributes.
* Impact: Implementing `domain_validated_by in domain_owner_roles` as written rejects every valid `asdict(validated_hypothesis)`, `asdict(validated_register)`, and `asdict(validated_pack)` payload, while missing multi-shape collision and extra-key checks leaves an `enforce_governance_guard` bypass for hybrid `Mapping` payloads.
* Smallest useful change: Update `FR-C4-2`, `P01-T01` item 3, and `P02-T01` so that: (1) each of the five governed `Mapping` shapes (`FlowDiscoveryReport`, `LifecycleDictionaryReport`, `ConstraintHypothesis`, `RankedConstraintRegister`, `Phase0DiscoveryPack`) has disjoint, complete signature key sets and explicit allowed/required key sets (allowing optional `"governance_hmac"` on flow reports and `RankedConstraintRegister.to_rows()` summary keys only in candidate mode when `review_status != OWNER_VALIDATED`); (2) `enforce_governance_guard` evaluates all five shape predicates in both candidate and validated modes, immediately raising `GovernanceViolationError` if `len(matched_shapes) > 1`, if `len(matched_shapes) == 0` when `owner_validated is True`, or if a matched governed mapping contains unexpected keys outside its allowed set; and (3) `_verify_hypothesis_mapping` unconditionally uses `discovery_owner_validated_by` as the discovery-owner signer, checks `domain_validated_role in domain_owner_roles` (with `domain_owner_roles` validated as a non-empty non-string `Sequence[str]`), `is_valid_human_actor(domain_validated_by)`, case-insensitive proposer separation on both `domain_validated_by` and `discovery_owner_validated_by`, strict `int` (not `bool`) `rank >= 1`, exact `bool` `True` across all five `constraint_test` keys, non-string `Sequence` containers on two-sided evidence, and `review_status != OWNER_VALIDATED` whenever `owner_validated is False`.
* Action owner: planning parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `FR-C4-2` in `.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-plan.md` and `P01-T01` / `P02-T01` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-phase-details.md` specifying `domain_validated_role in domain_owner_roles`, unconditional `discovery_owner_validated_by` actor extraction, strict `len(matched_shapes) > 1` collision rejection, and allowed-key enforcement, plus an assertion in `P02-T01` test 2.
* Decision route: direct planner correction

### PC-002 [High]: Pack `Mapping` verification ignores embedded `discovery_owner` mismatches and serialized or unvalidated `dictionary` mappings

* Related IDs: `RV-002`, `RV-005`, `FR-C4-2`, `FR-C4-4`, `AC-C4-2`, `P01-T01`, `P02-T01`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-phase-details.md (`P01-T01` item 3 bullet 4) and src/core/src/zava_forward/core/discovery_contract.py (`_dictionary_schema_digest` at lines 553-608, `Phase0DiscoveryPack.__post_init__` at lines 1833-1895, `enforce_governance_guard` at lines 1529-1544)
* Concern: `P01-T01` item 3 bullet 4 never inspects `m["discovery_owner"]` on a pack `Mapping` and checks `m["dictionary"]` only when `isinstance(m["dictionary"], LifecycleDictionary)`. However, `dataclasses.asdict(pack)` serializes both `pack.discovery_owner` and `pack.dictionary` into nested `dict` objects, not dataclass instances, and `_dictionary_schema_digest` hashes only structural spec fields without `validated_by`, `validated_on`, or `validated_role` (`discovery_contract.py:553-608`). Consequently: (1) a validated pack `Mapping` whose `"discovery_owner"` dict is unnominated, uses `"practical completion"`, or names a different owner (`"Farah Khan"`) passes `enforce_governance_guard(m, discovery_owner=dev_patel, dictionary=validated_dict)`; (2) a validated pack `Mapping` whose `"dictionary"` dict is `asdict(build_default_lifecycle_dictionary())` (49 open gaps) or a mismatched dictionary `dict` passes `enforce_governance_guard`; and (3) a candidate `asdict(pack)` passed to `enforce_governance_guard(m)` with `dictionary=None` skips schema-digest parity checks between `m["dictionary"]` and `m["dictionary_report"]` / `m["flow_report"]`.
* Impact: Serialized `Phase0DiscoveryPack` mappings (`asdict(pack)` or JSON dicts) can carry an unvalidated `dictionary` or mismatched `discovery_owner` while still passing `enforce_governance_guard`, leaving `RV-002` and `RV-005` partially open on pack mappings.
* Smallest useful change: Update `FR-C4-2`, `FR-C4-4`, `P01-T01` item 3 bullet 4, and `P02-T01` so `_verify_pack_mapping` strictly parses/coerces any present `"discovery_owner"` (`NominatedDiscoveryOwner` or `Mapping` with strict calendar-date fields) and `"dictionary"` (`LifecycleDictionary` or `Mapping` reconstructed into spec dataclasses so `LifecycleDictionary.__post_init__` and `lifecycle_dictionary_gaps` run), raising `GovernanceViolationError` on malformed types; binds nested candidate/validated sub-artifact checks to `effective_dictionary = dictionary if dictionary is not None else pack_dictionary`; requires `_dictionary_schema_digest(pack_dictionary) == _dictionary_schema_digest(dictionary)` whenever both are present; and when `require_validated=True`, requires `not lifecycle_dictionary_gaps(pack_dictionary)` (when `"dictionary"` is present), `pack_owner.is_ready and pack_owner.owner.strip() == expected_owner` (when `"discovery_owner"` is present), and non-blank `contract_version == CONTRACT_VERSION` (when `"contract_version"` is present).
* Action owner: planning parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `FR-C4-2` / `FR-C4-4` in `.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-plan.md` and `P01-T01` / `P02-T01` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-phase-details.md` specifying embedded `discovery_owner` and `dictionary` dataclass-or-`Mapping` parsing, gap checking, and caller-kwarg parity, plus assertions in `P02-T01` test 2.
* Decision route: direct planner correction

### PC-003 [Medium]: `FlowDiscoveryReport` HMAC canonical payload omits `notice`, and `Mapping` / `signing_key` checks allow type coercion and unvalidated non-flow keys

* Related IDs: `RV-001`, `FR-C4-1`, `AC-C4-1`, `P01-T01`, `P02-T01`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-plan.md (`FR-C4-1`), .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-phase-details.md (`P01-T01` item 4), and src/core/src/zava_forward/core/discovery_contract.py (`_FLOW_REPORT_DIGEST_KEYS` at lines 926-948, `FlowDiscoveryReport.to_json_dict` at lines 1138-1151)
* Concern: `FR-C4-1` and `P01-T01` item 4 compute `governance_hmac` over `[calculation_digest, dictionary_schema_digest, owner_validated, finding_status, owner_validated_by, owner_validated_on, validation_note]`, omitting `notice`, which is also excluded from `_FLOW_REPORT_DIGEST_KEYS`. A cross-process caller can therefore strip `NOT_FINDING` (`notice=""`) from a signed candidate JSON payload or inject misleading approval prose into `payload["notice"]` without invalidating either `calculation_digest` or `governance_hmac`. Similarly, on unkeyed candidate mappings (`owner_validated=False`, `signing_key=None`), `_EVALUATED_FLOW_REPORT_DIGESTS` does not authenticate `notice` or sign-off metadata, so candidate mappings must reject stripped `notice != NOT_FINDING` or non-empty `owner_validated_by` / `owner_validated_on` / `validation_note`. Finally, `P01-T01` item 4 reconstructs HMAC inputs via `bool(mapping.get("owner_validated"))` and `str(mapping.get(...))` rather than enforcing exact field types (`bool`, `str`, `None` or `_strict_iso_date`) first, and only validates `signing_key` inside `_verify_flow_report_mapping` instead of at the top of `enforce_governance_guard`.
* Impact: Leaves `payload["notice"]` mutable across authenticated cross-process JSON transport and permits candidate `Mapping` sign-off metadata spoofing or silent acceptance of malformed `signing_key` arguments on non-flow inputs.
* Smallest useful change: Update `FR-C4-1`, `P01-T01` item 4, and `P02-T01` to: (1) include `notice` as the 8th element in the canonical HMAC JSON array `[calculation_digest, dictionary_schema_digest, owner_validated, finding_status, owner_validated_by, owner_validated_on, validation_note, notice]`; (2) validate exact field types (`isinstance(owner_validated, bool)`, `isinstance(..., str)` for `finding_status`, `owner_validated_by`, `validation_note`, `notice`, and `None` or `_strict_iso_date` for `owner_validated_on`) before computing/comparing the HMAC, and on candidate mappings (`owner_validated=False`) require `notice == NOT_FINDING`, blank `owner_validated_by` and `validation_note`, and `owner_validated_on is None`; and (3) call `_validate_signing_key(signing_key)` at the entry of `enforce_governance_guard` whenever `signing_key is not None`.
* Action owner: planning parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `FR-C4-1` in `.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-plan.md` and `P01-T01` / `P02-T01` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-phase-details.md` covering `notice` in the 8-element HMAC payload, strict candidate `notice`/sign-off checks, and entry-level `_validate_signing_key`, plus a `notice`-tampering assertion in `test_cross_process_json_roundtrip_mapping_passes_guard_and_rejects_tampering`.
* Decision route: direct planner correction

### PC-004 [Medium]: Incomplete `datetime`-subclass rejection across spec/governance helpers and duplicate-gap hazard for existing 49-gap dictionary tests

* Related IDs: `RV-003`, `FR-C4-3`, `AC-C4-3`, `NFR-C4-2`, `P01-T01`, `P02-T01`
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-plan.md (`FR-C4-3`), .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-phase-details.md (`P01-T01` item 1), src/core/src/zava_forward/core/discovery_contract.py (lines 160-220, 235-395, 480-550, 659-690, 1569-1725), and src/core/tests/test_discovery_contract.py (lines 196, 217, 243)
* Concern: First, `FR-C4-3` omits `datetime`-subclass rejection, while `P01-T01` item 1 checks `not isinstance(validated_on, datetime)` only inside `StageSpec.validated`, `EventSpec.validated`, `TimestampSpec.validated`, `WaitStateSpec.validated`, and `ReturnLoopSpec.validated` without requiring `isinstance(validated_on, date)` (leaving non-`None` non-date runtime objects truthy) and without rejecting `datetime` arguments in `validate_stage_spec`, `validate_event_spec`, `validate_timestamp_spec`, `validate_wait_state_spec`, `validate_return_loop_spec`, `NominatedDiscoveryOwner.status()` / `nominate_discovery_owner` / `agree_discovery_endpoint`, `validate_lifecycle_report`, `ConstraintHypothesis.__post_init__`, `RankedConstraintRegister.__post_init__`, `validate_constraint_*`, and `validate_discovery_pack`. Second, `P01-T01` item 1 says to keep all required-code presence checks and also validate every item in `dictionary.stages`, `events`, `timestamps`, `wait_states`, and `return_loops`; if the required-code loops continue emitting validation gaps in addition to the full-sequence loops, `build_default_lifecycle_dictionary()` will emit `98` gaps instead of `49` and break `test_discovery_contract.py:196` (`15 + 15 + 8 + 7 + 4`) and `:243` (`len(gaps) == 5`). Also, `ts.non_imputation_rule` (`discovery_contract.py:513`) and `wait.unknown_cause_rule` (`:527`) currently omit `.strip()`.
* Impact: Partial `datetime` checks allow `validate_stage_spec(..., on=datetime(...))` or `ConstraintHypothesis(..., domain_validated_on=datetime(...))` to construct inconsistent records, and double-looping in `lifecycle_dictionary_gaps` breaks existing deterministic gap-count unit tests.
* Smallest useful change: Update `FR-C4-3`, `P01-T01` item 1, and `P02-T01` to: (1) define `_is_calendar_date(value: object) -> TypeGuard[date]` (`isinstance(value, date) and not isinstance(value, datetime)`) and enforce it across all five `*Spec.validated` properties, all five `validate_*_spec` functions (raising `ValueError`), `NominatedDiscoveryOwner` helpers/status, `LifecycleDictionaryReport`, `FlowDiscoveryReport`, `ConstraintHypothesis`, `RankedConstraintRegister`, `Phase0DiscoveryPack`, and `validate_*` promotion functions; and (2) specify that `lifecycle_dictionary_gaps` runs duplicate-key checks first, missing-required-key presence checks (`if key not in by_key`) second, and a single per-entry completeness/cross-reference/role/validation `elif` pass over every item in each sequence (using `.strip()` on `non_imputation_rule`, `accountable_domain`, and `unknown_cause_rule`, plus `stage.order >= 1` and non-blank `entry_event`/`exit_event`/`applies_to`), preserving exact `49` default gaps and existing gap message strings while asserting `datetime` rejection in `P02-T01` test 3.
* Action owner: planning parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `FR-C4-3` in `.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-plan.md` and `P01-T01` / `P02-T01` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/phase0-retrofit-flow-discovery-124-c4-phase-details.md` specifying `_is_calendar_date` across spec/governance surfaces, single-pass per-item gap emission preserving the 49-gap baseline, and `datetime` subclass assertions in `P02-T01` test 3.

## Strengths and Residual Risk

* Restoring `_EVALUATED_FLOW_REPORT_DIGESTS` and 5-tuple `_VALIDATED_FLOW_REPORT_SEALS` for `signing_key=None` preserves backward compatibility with existing in-process JSON round-trip tests (`src/core/tests/test_discovery_contract.py:1068`) while closing unkeyed candidate promotion and metric-recomputation forgery (`RV-001`).
* Requiring an explicit `>= 16`-byte `hmac`-SHA256 `signing_key` minted exclusively from live `_is_evaluated_report_instance(self)` (and 5-tuple-sealed when validated) `FlowDiscoveryReport` objects cleanly separates in-process convenience from cross-process cryptographic authenticity without external dependencies.
* Removing `"dictionary_schema_digest"` from `_FLOW_REPORT_SIGNATURE_KEYS` resolves the `LifecycleDictionaryReport` mapping collision (`RV-004`) cleanly once paired with `PC-001` multi-shape collision checks.
* Accepted residual risk: Real-world Zava North Homes human sign-offs (`OD-14`, `OD-16`, `OD-18`, `OD-36`) and optional synthetic CSV extract ingestion remain explicitly outside the Phase 0 stdlib rule-layer scope.
