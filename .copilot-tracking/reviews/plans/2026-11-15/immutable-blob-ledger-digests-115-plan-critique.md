### PC-001 [Critical]: Simultaneous `allowProtectedAppendWrites` and `allowProtectedAppendWritesAll` on `immutabilityPolicies@2024-01-01` violates Azure Storage ARM mutual exclusivity

* Related IDs: FR-4, AC-3, AC-4, P02, P02-T01, P02-T02, C7, C13
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/immutable-blob-ledger-digests-115-plan.md (`FR-4`, `AC-3`, `P02-T01`), .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/immutable-blob-ledger-digests-115-phase-details.md (`P02`, `P02-T01`, `P02-T02`), infra/modules/storage.bicep (lines 79-86 and 95-102), and Azure Storage ARM `ImmutabilityPolicyProperty` schema (`The 'allowProtectedAppendWrites' and 'allowProtectedAppendWritesAll' properties are mutually exclusive.`)
* Concern: `FR-4`, `AC-3`, `P02-T01`, and `P02-T02` instruct adding `allowProtectedAppendWritesAll: false` alongside `allowProtectedAppendWrites: false` on `digestRetention` (`Microsoft.Storage/storageAccounts/blobServices/containers/immutabilityPolicies@2024-01-01`) in `infra/modules/storage.bicep`. Static `az bicep build` allows both optional boolean fields because Bicep type definitions do not encode a `oneOf` exclusion, but Azure Storage Resource Provider rejects `PUT .../immutabilityPolicies/default` requests when both `allowProtectedAppendWrites` and `allowProtectedAppendWritesAll` are present in `properties`, even when both are `false`. Existing `digestRetention` and `rankingArchiveRetention` in `infra/modules/storage.bicep` rightly set only `allowProtectedAppendWrites: false` and omit `allowProtectedAppendWritesAll`.
* Impact: Implementing `P02-T01` as written passes offline Bicep compilation yet breaks live Azure Resource Manager `what-if` and deployment of `infra/modules/storage.bicep`.
* Smallest useful change: Keep `allowProtectedAppendWrites: false` and omit `allowProtectedAppendWritesAll` on `digestRetention` in `infra/modules/storage.bicep`. Revise `FR-4`, `AC-3`, `P02-T01`, and `P02-T02` so `assert_retention_controls` in `infra/tests/test_bicep_policy.py` asserts `props.get("allowProtectedAppendWrites") is False`, `"allowProtectedAppendWritesAll" not in props`, and mutual exclusivity across container immutability policies (`not ("allowProtectedAppendWrites" in props and "allowProtectedAppendWritesAll" in props)`), while `test_mutations_reject_missing_wrong_or_zero_retention` rejects setting `allowProtectedAppendWrites = True`, setting `allowProtectedAppendWritesAll = True`, or setting both properties simultaneously. Keep `verify_container_retention_lock` in `src/core` requiring `allowProtectedAppendWrites is False` and `allowProtectedAppendWritesAll in (False, None)` (and rejecting simultaneous boolean keys if raw ARM template properties are passed).
* Action owner: planning_parent (`planner-direct`)
* Exact resolving evidence: Revised `FR-4`, `AC-3`, `P02-T01`, and `P02-T02` preserving single-property `allowProtectedAppendWrites: false` in `infra/modules/storage.bicep` and enforcing mutual exclusivity plus append-write rejection in `infra/tests/test_bicep_policy.py`.
* Decision route: `planner-direct` (direct planner correction)
