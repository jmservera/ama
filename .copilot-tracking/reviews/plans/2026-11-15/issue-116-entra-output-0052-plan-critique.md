## Verdict

* Verdict: Revise
* Rationale: The candidate plan aligns well with the architectural constraint to reuse `projection.py`, `data_protection.py`, `ai.py`, and `ai_tasks.py`, and correctly preserves manual storage, reading, and download when document model dispatch is held. However, six concrete implementation defects in `P01-T01`, `P01-T02`, `P02-T01`, `P02-T02`, and `P03-T01` would break primary `Grants and Evidence Officer` and `Retrofit Coordinator` workflows, fail existing `src/api` tests (`test_ai_tasks.py`, `test_continuity_epoch_export.py`, `test_ai_consumption_api.py`, `test_chat_isolation.py`), weaken `ZF_DATA_MODE=actual` document gating, and leave live leakage paths open in `Store.ai_run` fallback templates, `Store.ai_priority`, `ai_tasks.review`, and `GET /api/ai/report`. All six findings are direct planner corrections within confirmed scope.
