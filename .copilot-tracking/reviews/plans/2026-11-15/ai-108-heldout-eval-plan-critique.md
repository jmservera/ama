### PC-003 [High]: P01-T01 threshold approval binds only thresholds_digest instead of full frozen manifest_digest and omits named-human live-approval guards

* Related IDs: FR-1, AC-1, P01, P01-T01, P01-T02
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/ai-108-heldout-eval-phase-details.md (lines 74-81, 132-135), src/core/src/zava_forward/core/cohort_eval.py (lines 183-242), src/agents/src/zava_forward/agents/heldout_freeze.py (lines 109-160), and src/core/tests/test_rai_threshold_live_approval_guard.py (lines 46-116).
* Concern: Caller Criterion 1 requires freezing versions, independently labelled held-out split, expected outcomes, critical errors, cohorts, sufficient samples, and approved thresholds together before evaluation. `P01-T01` defines `approve_thresholds(thresholds, *, approved_by, approved_at)` with `threshold_approval.thresholds_digest` covering only `thresholds`. Unlike `cohort_eval.approve` (`manifest_digest` over the manifest minus `approval`) and `heldout_freeze.manifest_digest` (manifest minus `threshold_approval`), hashing only `thresholds` permits post-approval edits to `versions`, `split_seed`, `cohorts`, `critical_errors`, or `held_out` cases without invalidating approval. `P01-T01` also omits repository guards rejecting automation/agent/placeholder approver strings, invalid ISO `approved_at` dates, and `synthetic=True` manifests when evaluated in `live=True` mode.
* Impact: Post-approval mutation of held-out cases, expected outcomes, cohort definitions, or versions would pass `check_heldout_freeze` undetected, and synthetic or agent-attributed approvals could be accepted where named-human approval is required.
* Smallest useful change: Update `P01-T01` and `P01-T02` so threshold approval binds both `thresholds_digest` and full `manifest_digest` (canonical SHA-256 of the manifest excluding `threshold_approval`), validates named-human `approved_by` (rejecting empty, automation/agent, or placeholder tokens matching `cohort_eval` conventions) and ISO-8601 `approved_at`, blocks `synthetic=True` manifests when `live=True` (while permitting explicit offline harness execution with `live_activation_allowed = False`), and adds tamper tests for post-approval edits to both `thresholds` and `held_out`/`versions`/`cohorts`.
* Action owner: Planning parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `P01-T01` and `P01-T02` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/ai-108-heldout-eval-phase-details.md` specifying `manifest_digest` binding, named-human/ISO-date/live-synthetic guards, and tamper unit tests.

### PC-004 [High]: P02-T01 and P03-T01 candidate comparison omits multi-lever config-diff detection, unknown-liability fail-closed economics, and required default-baseline fields

* Related IDs: FR-3, FR-4, AC-3, AC-4, AC-5, P02, P02-T01, P03-T01, C11, C12
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/ai-108-heldout-eval-phase-details.md (lines 186-215, 259-263), src/core/src/zava_forward/core/ai_consumption.py (lines 553-640), src/agents/src/zava_forward/agents/candidate_comparison.py (lines 130-185), and src/api/tests/test_eval_candidate_regression_gate.py (lines 67-105).
* Concern: Three gaps weaken `tokenomics_eval.compare_candidate` and its `POST /api/ai/candidate-comparison` wiring: (1) `compare_candidate(baseline, candidate, *, lever)` trusts the caller-supplied `lever` string without checking `candidate` lever/configuration metadata (`config` keys `{model, prompt, routing, caching}` and/or `changed_levers`) to verify that only that single lever actually changed between `baseline` and `candidate` (so a candidate changing both `routing` and `model` or `routing` and `caching` could pass if submitted with `lever="routing"`); (2) `P02-T01` computes token/cost deltas via `ai_consumption.Usage` / `Pricing` without specifying fail-closed behavior when candidate or baseline has `unknown_liabilities > 0`, missing/malformed usage, `status == "insufficient"`, or `actual_serving_version` mismatch (`is_saving` must be `False`, cost saving `"Unknown"`, and verdict `"failure"` or `"not_comparable"`); and (3) `P03-T01` states `POST /api/ai/candidate-comparison` defaults omitted `baseline` to the frozen held-out baseline summary, without requiring `heldout_eval.run_heldout_eval()` to emit a baseline comparison object carrying `case_ids`, `accepted_case_ids`, `manifest_sha256`, `split_seed`, `config`, quality counts, and usage needed by `compare_candidate` (including detecting loss of any baseline-accepted case).
* Impact: Multi-lever candidate bundles could evade single-lever isolation, candidates with `unknown_liabilities > 0` or unverified serving versions could be misreported as valid comparisons or savings, and omitting `baseline` on `POST /api/ai/candidate-comparison` would lack per-case acceptance and lever-config baselines.
* Smallest useful change: Update `P01-T01`, `P02-T01`, and `P03-T01` so: (a) baseline and candidate records include `config: {model, prompt, routing, caching}` (and optional `changed_levers`), and `compare_candidate` verifies that non-identical config keys match `[lever]` (rejecting zero-lever mismatches, unknown/duplicate levers, or multi-lever changes unless isolated one lever at a time); (b) `unknown_liabilities > 0`, missing/unverified usage, `status != "pass"` (`"fail"` or `"insufficient"`), or `serving_version` mismatch force `is_saving = False`, `cost_saving = "Unknown"`, and non-pass verdict with explicit blocking reasons; and (c) `heldout_eval.run_heldout_eval()` includes a structured `baseline_candidate` payload (`case_ids`, `accepted_case_ids`, `manifest_sha256`, `split_seed`, `config`, metrics, usage) used when `POST /api/ai/candidate-comparison` omits `baseline`.
* Action owner: Planning parent
* Exact resolving evidence: Updated `P01-T01`, `P02-T01`, and `P03-T01` in `.copilot-tracking/details/2026-11-15/ai-108-heldout-eval-phase-details.md` (and `FR-3`/`FR-4` notes in `.copilot-tracking/plans/2026-11-15/ai-108-heldout-eval-plan.md`) covering config-lever diffing, unknown-liability/serving-version fail-closed rules, and `baseline_candidate` schema, backed by unit tests in `P02-T01` and `P03-T01`.

### PC-005 [Medium]: P01-T01 prompt/tuning isolation check risks short-label shingle false positives and omits per-task legacy prompt render arguments

## Strengths and Residual Risk
