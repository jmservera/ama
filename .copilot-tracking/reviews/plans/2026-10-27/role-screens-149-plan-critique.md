### PC-001 [High]: Cleanup lacks the required archive and restore proof

* Related IDs: P03-T02; retention policy; preserved original RPI evidence.
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/details/2026-10-27/role-screens-149-phase-details.md:78-82
  permits proceeding after ownership selection and mentions the unqualified
  git clean -fdxq command.
  docs/reference/method/tracking-retention-policy.md, Explicit Cleanup
  Contract, additionally requires a finite approved manifest, full tracking
  snapshot preservation, archive readback, selected-member restore/hash
  verification, drift refusal, protected-byte checks and removal journaling.
* Concern: Identifying disposable output is not the complete preservation
  contract. Nor does an inspected selection mechanically limit a later
  repository-wide clean. Current untracked plan/details and other active
  evidence must not become cleanup collateral.
* Impact: Irrecoverable removal of original or owner-owned evidence is
  possible before publication; an otherwise valid gate cannot repair it.
* Smallest useful change: Make P03-T02 conditional on the cited preservation
  steps for any selected tracking removal. Require deletion to be constrained
  to the exact approved finite path list, with a matching final dry-run and
  refusal on drift or unselected inputs. If clean-input requirements cannot
  be met safely, stop rather than broaden cleanup. Preserve the locked
  zero-test-removal rule.
* Action owner: Planning parent, specifying the implementation operator's
  cleanup prerequisites.
* Exact resolving evidence: Revised P03-T02 names the manifest, archive/readback
  and restore/hash proof, protected-byte checks, journal and exact-path
  execution boundary, or explicitly chooses no cleanup and a blocked gate
  when forbidden inputs remain. Later execution must retain those receipts;
  this critique does not claim they already exist.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction. Existing cleanup authorization
  does not remove retention obligations; no additional deletion authority
  should be inferred.

### PC-002 [High]: Publication needs explicit fail-closed Review and gate exits

* Related IDs: P03-T01, P03-T02; engineering acceptance criteria; one-gate budget.
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/details/2026-10-27/role-screens-149-phase-details.md:71-73
  says to route Review findings as later work, then :82-87 proceeds to gate
  and publication.
  .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-10-27/role-screens-149-plan.md:88-89 accepts a
  receipt or retained failure and findings disposed/routed, without stating
  which outcomes prohibit pushing.
  tools/rpi-checks.py:152-175 requires clean inputs and the current main base;
  :2191-2247 distinguishes verification and the lock-bearing gate invocation.
* Concern: Preserving adverse evidence is necessary but does not make an
  unresolved access, safety, evidence-integrity or engineering failure
  eligible for publication. The plan also lacks an explicit stop when the
  single gate fails, its lock cannot be acquired, immediate verification
  fails, or fetched main/candidate inputs invalidate its receipt.
* Impact: An operator could treat a recorded failure or a deferred blocking
  finding as completion and publish an unqualified candidate, or consume an
  unauthorized second gate to recover.
* Smallest useful change: Define publication-blocking Review dispositions
  separately from accepted residuals and deferred human acceptance.
  Require a passing authorized receipt plus successful exact-head/current-base
  partial-scope verification for parent publication. State that retained
  failure is a truthful blocked closeout, not permission to push. After a
  failed attempt or invalidated receipt, stop and retain evidence; do not
  rerun, resume, switch to --all or silently rewrite branches. Resolve any
  need for further validation through the owner, outside this authorization.
  Keep the original single Review; do not plan another to overwrite it.
* Action owner: Planning parent.
* Exact resolving evidence: P03 contains an explicit result-to-action table
  for blocking Review findings, permitted residuals, gate failure, lock
  contention, receipt/base/head/input drift and successful verification.
  Only the successful eligible path reaches parent push/PR publication;
  child engineering evidence remains separately required, not aggregate proof.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction. A future exhausted-budget or
  unresolved blocking outcome may require owner direction, but no such new
  decision is needed to fix this plan now.

  misleading aggregate trace can undermine independently reviewable diffs.
* Smallest useful change: Add one finite #225-#233 delivery matrix with the
  required order, branch/base, module or trace path, existing permitted role,
  read contracts and intentional deferrals. Allocate host/navigation/shared
  harness ownership to P01 and make #233 an explicit trace-only exception
  to the role-module rule, with its own draft PR to the same parent.
  Reference exact child sources/evidence without importing their code into
  the parent. Specify how script inclusions remain independently reviewable.
* Action owner: Planning parent.
* Exact resolving evidence: The plan/details contain all nine issue rows,
  exactly the authorized child-to-parent/parent-to-main topology, no invented
  roles, no ninth role script, and an identified owner/path for manifest
  updates and #233's eleven-perspective/five-theme cross-child trace.
  Any deferred obligation remains labelled, not accepted.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction within the selected architecture;

### PC-004 [Medium]: The locked test budget lacks a branch-executable allocation

* Related IDs: P01-T01, P02-T01, P02-T02; Validation and Test Ownership;
  maximum 12 role-specific unit methods and 2 browser-check files.
* Evidence: .copilot-tracking/plans/2026-10-27/role-screens-149-plan.md:117-125
  lists broad targets, limits and semantic goals but no selected test names,
  per-child allocation or execution commands. Details promise local unit and
  real-browser evidence for each child without identifying how independent
  branches use the two allowed browser files.
  src/api/checks/design_system_browser_check.py:226-288 provides a reusable
  synthetic local-server runner. src/api/checks/workbench-manifest.json
  contains partial/deferred perspectives and several empty queues, not proof
  of new role scenarios. tools/rpi-checks.py:1534-1585 invokes fixed existing
  browser checks, not arbitrary new role-check files.
* Concern: A parent run with no installed modules cannot establish child
  behavior. Existing sparse fixtures also do not by themselves prove each
  promised populated, missing/stale/denied or same-home scenario. Without
  allocation, eight independent implementations can exceed the ceilings or
  leave required checks unexecuted while the parent gate still passes.
* Impact: The fixed budget and exact-child evidence acceptance criteria cannot
  be reliably enforced; browser-file existence could be mistaken for execution.
* Smallest useful change: Specify the two browser-file owners/paths and their
  selector or extension mechanism; allocate the at-most-12 role-specific
  unit methods across children. Give commands and working directories for
  shared-host checks, each installed child and existing API/workbench
  regressions. Map synthetic fixture IDs and expected results to the required
  semantic checks, including denied requests, no new writes, late-response/
  session isolation, explicit selection/reset and complete accessible hazard
  evidence. Run applicable role checks explicitly locally rather than claim
  the unchanged gate discovers them.
* Action owner: Planning parent.
* Exact resolving evidence: A branch-by-branch validation matrix identifies
  test methods, fixtures, role selectors, commands, expected observations and
  exact-source evidence destinations; allocations stay within 12 methods,
  2 browser files, no dependencies and zero removals. The parent-empty state
  and each child-installed state are distinct. Later reports record actual
  results without marking human or issue acceptance complete.
* Decision route: Direct planner correction; do not silently increase budgets.
