## Rules given to every rewriter

* Humanizer skill patterns plus the user's rules: clear and direct, varied sentence length, active voice, practical and specific, usable numbers, "you" and "your" only where natural for the reader (a research paper keeps its first person "I" and "we"), no emojis, no dashes as prose separators, no banned words, no "not only/just X but also Y", "From X to Y", "That's not X. It's Y", no metaphors or analogies, no "Why it matters" section.
* Preserve every number, citation key, `\cite`, `\ref`, `\label`, macro, environment, table row, link target, code span, file path, hash, date and claim.
* No invented facts or sources.
