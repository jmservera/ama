# Details: keyboard probe correction C2 (2026-11-10)

* Shadow shape regex: `^rgba?\(([^)]*)\) (-?[\d.]+)px (-?[\d.]+)px ([\d.]+)px ([\d.]+)px$`; visible when alpha (fourth channel, default 1) is above 0 and spread is at least 2. Inset, multiple shadows or other shapes are treated as unsupported and fail.
* Helper cases: ring passes; `rgba(0, 90, 158, 0) 0px 0px 0px 2px` fails; `rgb(0, 90, 158) 0px 0px 0px 0px` fails; transparent black and an unsupported list fail.
* Browser negative: inline CSSOM sets `outline: none` and `box-shadow: rgba(0, 90, 158, 0) 0px 0px 0px 2px` on the focused element; must fail "focus visible at <stage>".
* Reset negative: stage `reset-status`, remove `role` with text intact; must fail "status contract at reset-status".
* Known limit: the supported-shape constraint rejects other legitimate ring shapes loudly; a product restyle needs a deliberate probe update.

## Critique amendments (applied)

* Supported ring requires zero offsets and zero blur and spread of at least 2px; add an offset-shadow regression case.
* The transparent-shadow mutation verifies the computed shadow and disabled outline inside the same browser call and throws otherwise.
