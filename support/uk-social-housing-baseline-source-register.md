## Purpose

This register links every benchmark family used in
[UK Social Housing Baseline Research](./uk-social-housing-baseline-research.md)
to its supporting publication, underlying dataset or direct report.

The register distinguishes primary data from contextual evidence. Derived
figures for the fictional 60,000-home association are arithmetic translations,
not values published by the source organisations.

## Energy, Stock and Cost References

### EN-01 English Housing Survey energy efficiency

Primary use:

* 74% of social rented homes at EPC/EER C or above
* Approximately 26% below C
* Change in the A/B, C and D band distribution
* Modelled cost to reach C by tenure, starting band and archetype

Links:

* [English Housing Survey 2024 to 2025, Chapter 2: Energy efficiency](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/chapters-for-english-housing-survey-2024-to-2025-headline-findings-on-housing-quality-and-energy-efficiency/chapter-2-energy-efficiency)

Publication date: 29 January 2026. The physical dwelling data are centred on
April 2024.

## Citation and Use Notes

* National values must not be presented as Proper North Homes performance.
* Unweighted TSM landlord medians are research calculations from RS-03.
* Programme-specific results must retain their scheme, date and cohort.
* Legal requirements must be checked against guidance in force on the decision
  date.
* Planning assumptions have no external reference and exist to test sensitivity.
* Direct PDF and XLSX links may change when publishers replace documents. The
  associated publication page should remain the primary discovery route.
