## Scope and Retrieval Status

This review covers dated regulatory, funding and sector claims in the
*Everything But the Software* paper and the owner-selected Cowork v2
supporting pages, including the build handoff, speaker script, backup slides,
cost evidence and linked English Housing Survey statistics report. It excludes
the paper's software-engineering studies and the presentation's separate Azure
price assumptions.

Official-source retrieval was attempted on 2026-10-29. GOV.UK and SEC lookups
failed at DNS resolution, and official-source searches timed out. No official
page content was retrieved in this review. The sector statistics report is
dated 2026-10-23; that is the report-level last recorded review date, not a
per-source retrieval date. The paper's source ledger records its SEC release
check on 2026-10-27. The table gives these limits explicitly. `Unknown` means
that currentness could not be rechecked; it does not mean unchanged. `Changed`
means a discrepancy is established by comparing retained records, not by a new
official retrieval.

The table maps all 80 regulatory, funding and sector figure rows in sections
1-4 of the statistics report to their sources by row identifier. The report's
five Azure pricing rows are outside this review's scope.
