Nothing was created, changed or deployed. Sign-in values were read at runtime by the probe and are not recorded. Follows [the 2026-11-24 end-to-end note](./issue-2245-live-010431-2026-11-24.md).

## Findings

* The live API is revision `ca-cutover-test-demo-api--0000060` (created 2026-11-24T08:11:30Z, 100% traffic, image digest `sha256:c9b0a187...`), newer than the revision 0000059 of the earlier note. Its environment lists `ZF_DEMO_AI_SUSPENSION_OVERRIDE`, `ZF_CHAT_DEMO_ACTIVATION`, `ZF_DEPLOYMENT_PROFILE` and `ZF_ENV=production`.
* Thread creation: 8 of 8 `POST /api/chat/threads` returned 200 (3.4 to 7.6 s) and the 5 question threads returned 200 ([probe-chat-result.json](./issue-2245-live-101805-2026-11-24/probe-chat-result.json), script [probe_chat.py](./issue-2245-live-101805-2026-11-24/probe_chat.py), case page [screenshot](./issue-2245-live-101805-2026-11-24/screens/case-page.png)).
* The model is called live. Container logs on revision 0000060 show three `AI_USAGE capability=chat-case-conversation calls=1 outcome=returned` lines at 10:12:57, 10:13:28 and 10:13:44 UTC (a concurrent session, not this probe).
* This probe's five asks all returned `model: workflow-only`. Logs at 10:13:56, 10:21:26 and 10:21:33 UTC record `AI_LIMIT_BREACH reason=call-budget-limit route=workflow-only`. Root cause: `chat_demo_meter.ensure` seeds a synthetic authority of `CALLS = 20` model calls per UTC day (`src/core/src/zava_forward/core/chat_demo_meter.py`); the ledger holds the 21st call in `ai_consumption` before any provider request. Test and demo traffic on the same day used the cap.
* Defect still open: a budget hold is reported to the user as `rules only because the assistant is not available`, while the widget banner says answers are generated by an AI model. The hold carries no distinct outcome through `ChatModel` and `ChatPanel`. A cleaner message ("the daily demo allowance is used, resets at 00:00 UTC") needs a `src` change and is left for owner selection.
* The "Model calls: 1" proof cannot be repeated today. The allowance resets at 00:00 UTC; a live re-probe after that is the only remaining live proof.

## Regression test

`ChatDemoMeterOnSqlStore.test_daily_demo_budget_exhaustion_holds_the_next_question_before_dispatch` in `src/api/tests/test_continuity.py` (real `SqlStore` and ledger, demo env): 20 calls return, the 21st is `financial-held`, exactly 20 provider requests are sent and one `call-budget-limit` alert is recorded. `test_continuity` passes (25 tests). The test passes on current code; it pins the cap behaviour that explains the live observation, so a change to the cap or to hold-before-dispatch fails it.
