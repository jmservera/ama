Task: issue #2244, follow-up from [run 2](./issue-2244-live-r2-2026-11-23.md). Demo scope per owner decisions 2026-11-18: read-only Azure, no drill, no production change. The issue has no tick-box list; C1 to C5 come from [the first 2026-11-23 note](./issue-2244-live-2026-11-23.md).

## Evidence

* `az containerapp show -g ama -n ca-cutover-test-demo-api` (existing login, no secrets read): active revision `ca-cutover-test-demo-api--0000055`, Healthy. StartUp probe: initialDelay 1 s, period 3 s, failureThreshold 40, window 121 s.
* Retry budget from `mssqlstore.py`: 8 attempts, each waiting at most 15 s (`@LockTimeout = 15000`), with sleeps of 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 30 and 30 s: 8 x 15 + 91 = 211 s.
* A writer holding the lock for more than 121 s therefore makes the platform kill the replica while retries are still running.

## Change

`infra/modules/containerapps.bicep` API StartUp failureThreshold 40 to 80 (window 241 s, within the existing 300 s test ceiling). `infra/tests/test_container_hosting_completeness.py` gains a test that derives the retry budget from the store constants and requires the window to exceed it; it fails at 40 and passes at 80. `infra/tests/test_bicep_policy.py` pins the new value. Not deployed. Residual risk: the law store and the SQL store retry in sequence, so two worst-case waits would exceed 241 s.
