## Findings

* No generative answer: the demo store holds an open all-scope AI suspension (`GET /api/continuity`, [probe-continuity-result.json](./issue-2245-live-2026-11-23/probe-continuity-result.json)), recorded 2026-11-18 with reason "Demo probe: negative test", evidence `SYN-221-PROBE`, restart null. While it is open, `_ask` in `chat_panel.py` never calls the model. The suspension is a leftover of a negative test and clearing it is a human restart action under incident control, so it was not touched.
* The banner and replies already say so: `/api/chat/status` demo disclaimer ends "AI answers are on hold: an open suspension covers AI work, so replies use rules only", the widget shows it ([probe-widget-banner.json](./issue-2245-live-2026-11-23/probe-widget-banner.json), [widget-banner.png](./issue-2245-live-2026-11-23/screens/widget-banner.png)), and free-form replies say "no model was called".
* Skills and routes that call the model: any thread with permitted sources, a configured model, no open suspension and a question outside the rules-first intents (`unblock`, `claim`, `pas2035`, `sensor`). Those four intents answer from decision rules by design ("Model calls: 0"). A thread without a case has no sources and abstains. The three smoke questions (EPC and stock, funding claim, quality inspection) hit rules-first or suspended paths.
* 500 on thread creation: not reproduced. Eight consecutive creates and five more in the ask matrix all returned 200 (53 to 793 ms) and the console tail held no `http-error` line. Log Analytics stays denied, so the 2026-11-22 cause is unread. The most plausible cause is the SQL write-lock timeout seen at the same time (issue 2244). `_unhandled` answered that with 503 only for GET and HEAD (issue 2237), so a POST thread create returned the plain 500. Fixed: `POST /api/chat/threads` now answers 503 with `Retry-After: 5` and the logged cause `sql-write-lock-busy`; other POSTs keep the 500 because a failed POST may follow a committed step.
