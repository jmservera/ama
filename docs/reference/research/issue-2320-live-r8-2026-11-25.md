## Code-level cause

On a miss the briefing runs `run_text_task`, then `add_record`, which holds `store.lock` and waits for the SQL application lock. #2379 capped that wait at 2 s and answers with an unrecorded fallback, but the fallback was not cached. With a lock held by someone else, every following GET repeated the full 2 s wait while holding `store.lock`, so quiet calls clustered at 2.1 to 2.4 s and concurrent calls queued (waves of about 4 to 6 s per call, up to 57 s). The 10 to 16 s calls are the same wait multiplied by queueing behind other requests that need `store.lock`.

## Fix

`Store._briefing_unrecorded` now keeps the unrecorded answer in `_briefing_busy` for `BRIEFING_BUSY_TTL_S` (10 s). A later GET with the same key returns it at once, with no recompute, no `store.lock` wait on SQL and no write. A successful recording removes the entry. The answer still says it was not saved. Files: `src/core/src/zava_forward/core/store.py`, `sqlstore.py`.
