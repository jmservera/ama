## Defect and fix (local commit, not deployed)

`ChatModel` collapsed the `financial-held` outcome into `provider-unavailable`, so a budget hold produced "uses rules only because the assistant is not available", contradicting the AI banner. Now a call, token or money budget hold becomes outcome `allowance-used`, and the panel replies that the daily demo allowance is used up, no model was called and it resets at 00:00 UTC. Other financial holds keep the old wording. Tests: `test_chat_model.py`, `test_chat_panel.py` (the three existing budget-hold tests now expect `allowance-used`).
