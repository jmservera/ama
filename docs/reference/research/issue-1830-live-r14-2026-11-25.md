## Findings

* At head `c67289436e`, load average 10 to 19: `rpi-checks.py --preflight api:test_production_config_a11y_check` passes and says it is development feedback, not a receipt; `api:test_does_not_exist` and `../etc/passwd` are BLOCKED; 62 timing and preflight tests pass.
* The direct #1828 check `checks/map_fullscreen_browser_check.py` (run from `src/api` with `PYTHONPATH=.:src:../core/src:../agents/src`, pinned axe-core 4.10.3, sha256 prefix `880970c081707360`) was intermittently red. Two of the first three runs failed with `filter label and focus fit at 390x568`; that failure did not recur in 8 later sequential runs.
* A distinct failure recurred under parallel load: axe `target-size` on the first two `.map-marker` elements in state `expanded-320` (partially obscured, 28 by 20 px). Instrumented copies (assertions unchanged, extra output only) failed 5 of 30 parallel runs.
* Reading: the expanded-width loop resizes the viewport and scans after only a marker count check, while Leaflet's debounced 200 ms resize read and marker re-layout can still be in flight. `settle_map_reads` already exists for this and was not used there. Added one call before the scan; no assertion removed or loosened.
* After: 18 of 18 parallel runs passed with the same pinned axe. This is a before and after failure rate under synthetic load (5 of 30 against 0 of 18), not a proof that the cause reading is the only one; the 390x568 focus-fit failure was not reproduced and is not claimed fixed.
* ruff check, ruff format check and `python3 -m mypy` in `src/api` are clean. The script is not in the scenario manifest, so no recipe hash changed.
