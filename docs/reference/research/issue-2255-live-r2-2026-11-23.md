## Why no source change

The clock fails closed in `hazard_clock` (`src/core/src/zava_forward/core/taskqueues.py`) when `aware_at` or `response_due_at` is missing, which is the correct behaviour. The fix is data: `infra/scripts/seed_demo_hazard_clock.py` back-filled the fields. `SqlStore.merge_seed` is insert-only, and the generator of the demo dataset is not in this repository. Surviving a re-seed needs a data-changing re-seed of the demo store or a generator change outside the repository; neither is in scope for this read-only pass. The seeder is idempotent; re-run it after any re-seed.
