## Why model calls stopped on 2026-11-25

`ai_consumption.py` fail-closes: a reservation without settlement after 40 s marks the budget `liability-unknown`, and every later reservation in that budget is held. The demo meter seeded one budget per UTC day, so one slow settlement (asks of 17 to 24 s plus `sql-write-lock-busy`) stopped the model until midnight. The hold is a deliberate finance control and is not relaxed here.

## Fix (local commit, not deployed)

* `chat_demo_meter.py` seeds one synthetic authority per UTC hour (abutting windows, same 20 calls, 100000 tokens, amount 1 per slot), so a held settlement pauses the model for at most that hour. Test: `test_continuity.py::test_each_hour_gets_its_own_budget_so_a_held_hour_does_not_hold_the_day`. Worst case is 24 slots a day; the demo spend stays synthetic and the owner cap is USD 700.
* The wording fix from the 2026-11-25 note (`spend-held` outcome) is carried in this commit; it was only on the unmerged branch `test/code-live-20261125T231506Z`.
