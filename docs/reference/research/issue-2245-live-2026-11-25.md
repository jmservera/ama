## Defect and fix (local commit, not deployed)

The 2026-11-24 fix named only the three budget-limit holds. Any other `financial-held` reason (for example `liability-unknown`, `authority-unknown`, `price-unknown`) still collapsed to `provider-unavailable`, so the reply said "the assistant is not available" beside an AI banner. Now they produce outcome `spend-held` and the reply says the demo spending control is holding AI answers and no model was called. Budget limits keep the allowance wording; guard denials and token failures keep the unavailable wording. Files: `chat.py` (agents), `chat_panel.py` (core). Tests: `test_chat_model.py`, `test_chat_panel.py` (API).
