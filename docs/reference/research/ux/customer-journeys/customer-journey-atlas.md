## Main finding

Design around reliable commitments and usable outcomes, not an agent per
department. The role-agent contributions suggest that repeated explanations,
unclear receiving ownership, conflicting evidence, avoidable visits and
premature completion claims could burden almost every journey. This is a
cross-role hypothesis, not a measured finding about Zava North Homes.

The opportunity is to let people specify an outcome and constraints, then
have bounded assistance gather evidence, investigate gaps, prepare options
and draft the next useful step. People retain consequential decisions.
Deterministic records, controls and reminders remain essential even when an
agent is unavailable. AI cannot create qualified labour, funding or agreement.

## Evidence and scope

This is desk research and divergent ideation requested by the owner, not a
new formal Design Thinking coaching session, completed stakeholder research,
approved requirements or a build specification. No canonical deck, customer
card, application deployment, live communication or spending is authorised.

| Classification | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |

| Owner-confirmed context | Not-for-profit status, Excel coordination and absence of Dataverse/Dynamics, as recorded in maintained sources |
| Owner-reported practice | The reviewer found draft-BRD descriptions of an executed award covering roughly half potentially eligible homes, technician write-ups and resident phone reports. Award documents/conditions and service performance remain unverified |

Nine `company-*` research instruments contributed: Assets, Programme,
Safety, Finance, Procurement, Resident Engagement, Neighbourhood Housing,
Data/Performance and Executive Reviewer. None represents a real employee
with privileged knowledge. Proxy maps are especially important to validate
with the relevant profession or affected user rather than accepting a
neighbouring role's account.

The companion files contain 27 stage-based maps; this atlas adds the
leasehold-service staff map and two missing subjourneys. This covers the
currently identified role families, not every possible job title or
validated completeness. Designer/coordinator, inspector/evaluator,
CEO/Board and legal/privacy/control reviewers share some map stages but
retain distinct decisions. No shared portal or daily screen is assumed.

## One connected service journey

| Stage | What users are trying to achieve | Key handoff / current pain hypothesis | Agent-assisted improvement hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Report or nominate | Resident/officer communicates need; data staff establish identity | Story repeated; nomination confused with hazard intake | Preserve source account and propose recipient-scoped links; human urgent intake remains independent |
| 2. Understand home and household | Assessor records condition; liaison establishes safe contact and adjustments | Incomplete records; inaccessible visit | Prepare cited contradictions/questions and practical visit brief, not invented observations |
| 3. Decide scope and feasibility | Professionals design; resident decides specific discretionary works; Finance/Assets assess affordability | Generic consent; changed design fails to reach reviewers | Compare scope versions and propose affected reviews/options; deterministic dependency controls remain authoritative |
| 4. Coordinate ready work | Contracts accepts competent capacity; Programme releases within gates; household confirms arrangements | Offered slot becomes promise; readiness checked too late | Rehearse the day and propose compatible visit options within committed capacity |
| 5. Deliver and capture | Installer performs authorised work; supervisor handles changes | Offline capture; informal variation; provider no-show blamed on resident | Structure confirmed worker evidence and draft exception questions; no inferred work or contract instruction |
| 6. Inspect and remedy | Independent inspector accepts/returns; contractor rectifies | Completion counted before independent acceptance | Raise evidence gaps and explain human return reasons; inspection proceeds without AI |
| 7. Handover and live with measures | Resident understands controls and has aftercare; evaluator reviews outcomes | Confusion, discomfort or higher bills hidden by closure | Prepare human-reviewed explanations and a sourced concern pack; never blame or promise savings |
| 8. Reconcile and learn | Finance distinguishes claim/cash; analyst reports; reviewers challenge | Cash, handover and programme closure collapsed | Assemble scoped evidence; deterministic ledgers/reports show unresolved work. Portfolio AI analysis stays later exploratory |

Safety intake, safeguarding, complaints and withdrawal are parallel routes,
not sequential gates that must wait for retrofit. Consent, technical
readiness, work release, contractor completion, independent QA, resident
handover, complaint remedy and financial closure remain separate.

## Filling the three gaps found by the executive reviewer

### Leasehold-service staff current-state map

This is a proxy hypothesis derived from the [reviewer](./reviewer.md),
resident ownership map and draft-BRD home-ownership handoffs, not lease advice.

| Stage / actions and touchpoints | Motivation | Pain hypothesis | Gain / ownership and exception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identify tenure/home/building through property and lease records | Route the right service | Household incorrectly treated as tenant | Verified relationship; data owner resolves ambiguity without merging guessed identities |
| Obtain applicable lease and proposed scope; request Legal review | Explain responsibility honestly | Missing terms; generic answer | Legal-reviewed position; missing evidence remains unknown and holds affected decisions |
| Coordinate process/cost evidence with Legal, Finance and Programme | Avoid unexpected liability | Scope, consultation and payment responsibilities conflated | Distinct human decisions and explicit unknowns; no agent interprets obligations |
| Explain reviewed position accessibly and record questions | Enable participation | Resident bounced between teams | Named update owner; access, consultation and works decisions remain distinct |
| Track changed scope, dispute, defect or charge query | Preserve accountability after package progress | Building completion erases household dispute | Affected package reviews retained; authorised specialists decide, complaints/redress remains usable |

Invented ask: "Prepare a responsibility-question pack from this scope and
the reviewed records, without interpreting my lease." Assistance can retrieve
permitted clauses and approved interpretations, cite gaps and draft questions.
Legal validates interpretation; Finance validates cost; staff approve every
resident message. Ambiguous lease identity stops reuse. Compare with a
tenure checklist and human case conference, counting review and disclosure risk.

### Nomination, prioritisation and challenge subjourney

| Stage | Current pain/motivation hypothesis | Improved journey and bounded help |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Officer nomination or resident self-referral | Need may be absent from stock flags | Record original account and correctable identity; agent proposes cited links only |
| Acknowledge and establish route | Resident does not know who owns request | Human-reviewed accessible explanation; emergency concerns enter independent human hazard intake immediately |
| Review eligibility/priority | Missing data could exclude the home silently | Deterministic scoring and explicit unknowns; authorised people investigate, decide exceptions and explain |
| Challenge/correct | Resident cannot see how to contest an error | Preserve competing evidence, named reviewer and accessible challenge; agent drafts evidence comparison, never reprioritises |
| Update and revisit | Correction does not reach dependent decisions | Data/domain owners correct approved records and re-review affected decisions; no automatic acceptance or sanction |

Measure nomination-to-owned-review, erroneous exclusions, challenge resolution
and resident effort, including cases with and without original risk flags.
Do not interpret non-response or support needs as lower priority.

### Controlled source-to-rule change subjourney

| Stage | Current pain/motivation hypothesis | Improved journey and bounded help |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Detect and preserve candidate source change | Important change missed or failed retrieval looks unchanged | Approved source-monitoring captures version/provenance; failed checks create owned exceptions |
| Review authority, currentness and applicability | Guidance confused with law or future commencement | Named Legal/compliance/funding/professional owner checks official evidence; agent may propose differences only |
| Trace affected work | Approved meaning not reflected in forms/tests | Draft impact list covering rules, journeys, communications, requirements and evidence; domain owners validate |
| Approve activation | Download mistaken for authority to change live controls | Authorised humans approve version and effective date; deterministic deployment/change control applies |
| Notify, verify and recover | Old work loses its historical basis | Retain prior versions, review affected cases, test rollback and notify through approved routes; no agent activates policy |

Measure missed changes, false impact suggestions, review effort and update
traceability against source alerts plus a manually maintained impact matrix.
No fresh legal-currentness check was made by this research.

## Divergent opportunities beyond another chatbot

These invented requests illustrate possible goals, not interview quotations.
An agent can select permitted sources, investigate discrepancies and iterate
on a cited draft within access, step, time and cost limits. It cannot give
itself approval or treat a completed tool call as achieved user outcome.

P means a task candidate consistent with existing bounded support, not an
approved pilot feature. E means exploratory, separately governed future scope.
Portfolio narrative, analytical supervision and cash rehearsal are E.

| ID / user request | What assistance could do on their behalf | Authority boundary / value to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O01 P, officer: "Let them hear my story once" | Draft a purpose/recipient-scoped case passport with corrections | Staff verify sharing and original account; fewer repeat explanations without excess disclosure |
| O02 P, liaison: "Find promises nobody owns" | Extract candidate commitments and conflicting dates for human confirmation | Deterministic owned-task reminders; fewer broken updates, not invented deadlines |
| O03 P, repairs: "Where did this referral lose the baton?" | Assemble sent/received/accepted/rejected evidence and draft chase questions | Sender retains chase; fewer unowned referrals, not diagnosis or clock changes |
| O04 P, resident: "Fit this around my ordinary day" | Draft a resident-confirmed disruption budget for noise, care, work and outages | Humans agree feasible support; no automatic compensation or compliance score |
| O05 P, resident: "Negotiate options, not my consent" | Compare constraints and committed slots, including pause/deferral | Resident chooses and staff confirm; fewer preference breaches, no auto-booking |
| O06 P, assessor: "Prepare tomorrow without guessing" | Assemble cited home facts, contradictions and missing observations | Assessor validates; fewer preparation/revisit errors |
| O07 P, coordinator: "Show what changed and who must review" | Suggest semantic dependencies and affected/unaffected scope | Deterministic invalidation and independent owners; fewer escaped stale reviews |
| O08 P, supervisor: "Rehearse the day before the van leaves" | Check a job story for missing materials, changed choices or absent QA | Human dispatch/release authority; fewer abortive visits, no created capacity |
| O09 P, planner: "Can one visit replace three?" | Propose compatible task bundles within competence/capacity/preferences | Resident and authorised staff agree; less total disruption, no urgent-response delay |
| O10 E, planner: "Investigate the blockage, not just queue age" | Form competing evidence-linked explanations from notes/events | Separate analytical sandbox; confirmed causes after review, no reprioritisation |
| O11 P, installer: "Turn my spoken notes into an evidence draft" | Structure confirmed capture against checklist, preserving originals | Worker confirms; QA accepts; less net rework, no fabricated photos/readings |
| O12 P, QA: "Tell me precisely what evidence is missing" | Raise mismatch/reuse/gap flags and explain human return reasons | Independent physical/evidence assurance even with no flags; critical misses/false flags |
| O13 P, resident: "Help me practise using the controls" | Draft optional accessible explain-back questions from approved manuals | Human/interpreter reviews; no comprehension score or inferred consent |
| O14 P, household: "Build the case for investigating my higher bills" | Assemble optional source evidence and unanswered aftercare questions | Competent investigation and human redress; no causation/blame/savings promise |
| O15 P, representative: "Give me only the briefing I am authorised to see" | Prepare scoped questions and flag expired/unclear authority | Resident controls arrangement; no assumed delegated works consent |
| O16 P, Finance: "Reuse the evidence, never the approval" | Suggest valid references across review packs with changed-scope warnings | Independent version-specific approvals; fewer duplicate requests without stale reuse |
| O17 P, grant preparer: "Challenge this pack before the reviewer does" | Rehearse unsupported statements against applicable verified terms | Internal draft only, human submission; useful omissions versus false objections |
| O18 E, Assets/Finance: "Rehearse the cash cliff and displaced work" | Suggest source-linked adverse scenarios around deterministic calculations | Finance/service owners validate and humans decide; no AI portfolio supervision |
| O19 E, executive: "Keep the counterclaim beside every success claim" | Build a claim/evidence/counterevidence comparison across versions | Later assurance experiment; no generated approval or audit opinion |
| O20 E, analyst: "Who bears the hidden cost of our faster queue?" | Suggest hypotheses about effort shifted to residents/officers and coverage bias | Lawful scoped aggregates and human interpretation; no inferred vulnerability or ranking |
| O21 P, operator: "Show what is stranded in this outage" | Draft business-impact questions from failed runs and pending owners | No automatic replay/release; controlled runbook recovery remains primary |
| O22 P, control reviewer: "Which records could this rights request touch?" | Produce source-linked inventory and unknown-copy questions | Controller accountability, DPO advice and authorised decisions; no erase/refuse action |
| O23 P, leasehold officer: "Which responsibility questions remain unanswered?" | Build a question pack from approved scope/reviewed evidence | Legal/Finance retain interpretation/cost; no charge or lease conclusion |
| O24 P, source owner: "Trace this approved source change to affected work" | Propose rule/message/test/evidence dependencies with uncertainty | Human interpretation/activation and deterministic change control |

## Five design themes and tensions

| Theme | Philosophy and representative ideas | Constraint that must survive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continuity rather than repeated intake | Case passport, promise tracker, referral baton | Purpose-scoped disclosure, safe contact, accepted ownership |
| Resident-controlled coordination | Disruption budget, preference options, bundled visits | Choice/withdrawal, realistic capacity, urgent safety route |
| Evidence that carries its context | Visit pack, voice draft, evidence passport, change scout | Provenance, unknowns, independent review, no transferred approval |
| Prevention through rehearsal | Dispatch rehearsal, grant challenge, future counterclaims/cash scenarios | False assumptions visible; future portfolio scope separate |
| Accountable recovery and learning | Aftercare concern pack, outage questions, rights/source-change inventories | Human remedy, deterministic controls, no automatic legal/policy decisions |

Do not optimise one team's minutes by transferring work to another team,
resident or subcontractor. Appointment utilisation can conflict with choice;
evidence reuse with privacy; faster starts with quality; attractive grants
with whole-life investment; automation with the capacity to review its drafts.

## Candidate learning tests, not approved prioritisation

Start with connected synthetic tasks on the retained programme lineage, not
a replacement dataset. Documentation itself requires no inference or cloud
spending. Any subsequent delivered AMA application iteration retains the
addendum's actual AI/SQL/Azure requirement; a useful non-AI comparator does
not waive that requirement elsewhere in the delivered build.

| Test bundle | Journey question | Evidence and failure cases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story-once plus promise ownership | Can intake and receiving teams reduce resident repetition without unsafe disclosure? | Wrong identity, changed safe number, limited advocate authority, rejected referral, model failure; repeat-story and total effort measures |
| Pre-visit pack plus readiness rehearsal | Does assistance prevent avoidable failed visits beyond a checklist? | Provider no-show, absent materials/QA, withdrawn choice, wrong design version; net preparation/review time and visit outcomes |
| Change-impact plus evidence reuse | Can teams avoid stale reviews without needless re-review? | Changed assessment, building/sibling dependency, conflicting EPC, ambiguous source; critical misses and false review burden |
| Accessible handover plus aftercare | Does explanation help someone use the home and obtain remedy? | Non-digital route, interpreter, unexplained higher bills, resident report versus normal sensor; task comprehension, comfort and unresolved concern |
| Review-ready funding evidence | Does assistance improve evidence flow beyond structured templates? | Unknown route, stale award terms, unsupported variation, duplicate invoice, delayed cash; total effort/error, never presumed award/cash |

For each, compare the same evidence and deterministic controls with and
without assistance; count preparation, checking, correction, coordination,
resident effort, assurance and technology/support cost. Freeze definitions
and record negative/Inconclusive results. Saved minutes are capacity until
Finance validates a cashable change. Synthetic results prove only what their
fixtures test, not throughput, grant capture or lived benefit.

Validate desirability through voluntary, accessible real user research if a
real customer becomes available: observe environment, walk through a recent
case, inspect workarounds, test constraints, then explore unmet needs.
Include declined/deferred work, failed visits, reopened defects/complaints,
non-digital households and supplier fieldwork, not only successful cases.
Use invented requests as conversation starters, not leading confirmation.
No resident research was conducted here.
