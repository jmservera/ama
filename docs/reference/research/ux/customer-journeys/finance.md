## Current-state journey hypotheses

Existing-record workflows, not agent-led futures. Approval and payment
execution are separate jobs; staffing/delegations need validation.

### A. Capital and grant preparer

Motivation: defensible mission investment. Gain: traceable packs, less chasing.

| Stage | Actions and channels | Pain / emotion hypothesis | Gain, handoff and exception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Establish need | Gather Programme scope, property IDs, technical evidence and Finance budget extracts through spreadsheets, email and meetings. | Unease about conflicting versions. | Named source owners; unresolved identity returns to Data, not a guessed match. |
| 2. Establish route | Locate applicable application route, terms and any award; distinguish opportunity from committed funding. | Deadline anxiety; missing agreement. | Funding owner confirms route; unknown route blocks preparation. |
| 3. Assemble case | Reconcile cost, eligibility, provider contribution, cash timing and resident value; draft application for authorized review. | Re-keying frustration; optimistic cash assumptions. | Separate cost/cash schedules to independent approver; no commitment implied. |
| 4. Maintain award/claim evidence | Following evidenced decisions, link installed measures, accepted QA, invoices and award conditions; prepare claim lines. | Worry about unsupported scope. | QA/contract owners correct gaps; variations reopen dependent reviews only. |
| 5. Reconcile and learn | Compare submitted claims, responses and bank receipts; record residuals and closure evidence. | Relief mixed with unresolved receivables. | Treasury confirms cash; Programme owns outstanding defects; closure waits for reconciliation. |

### B. Independent capital and grant approver

Motivation: independent affordability challenge. Gain: defensible decisions.

| Stage | Actions and channels | Pain / emotion hypothesis | Gain, handoff and exception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Receive review request | Inspect preparer identity, scope, version and decision requested in pack/email/meeting. | Concern that receipt becomes assumed approval. | Explicit acceptance of review ownership; self-prepared pack needs another reviewer. |
| 2. Test affordability | Compare long-term plan, annual envelope, match funding and liquidity with Treasury evidence. | Tension between warm homes and scarce capacity. | Record opportunity cost against safety/new supply; unknown headroom escalates, not passes. |
| 3. Challenge evidence | Trace eligibility statements and totals to approved records; inspect exceptions and declarations. | Fatigue from polished but unsupported narratives. | Source-linked exceptions to preparer/QA; no technical certification by Finance. |
| 4. Decide within authority | Record approval, conditional return or escalation; authorized human separately submits application/claim. | Pressure to meet a deadline. | Decision tied to exact version; material change invalidates affected approval; no automatic submission. |
| 5. Review outcome | Compare award, claim response, cash and variances at review/closure meetings. | Concern over apparent success without cash. | Require residual action owner; Board receives qualified outcomes, not nominal grant capture. |

### C. Payment officer

Motivation: accurate, authorized payments. Gain: reliable instructions, not
substitute QA or grant approval.

| Stage | Actions and channels | Pain / emotion hypothesis | Gain, handoff and exception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Receive invoice | Register invoice and source reference through the existing finance intake, channel unconfirmed. | Duplicate-entry suspicion. | Unique invoice identity; suspected duplicates go to controlled investigation. |
| 2. Match evidence | Compare authorized scope/order, invoice quantities and accepted milestone evidence. | Frustration at missing QA or variations. | Specific mismatch to contract owner/preparer; supplier evidence is not independent acceptance. |
| 3. Verify authorization | Check delegated decision, independent reviewer and approved payee details through established controls. | Anxiety about urgent overrides. | Unknown authority or changed bank details returns to controlled verification, not agent inference. |
| 4. Execute payment | Authorized officer uses established payment process after independent approval. | Pressure when grant cash is late. | Treasury resolves liquidity; grant receipt is not automatically a supplier-payment condition. |
| 5. Reconcile closure | Match bank settlement to ledger, invoice and residual balance; investigate failures. | Relief only after settlement evidence. | Failed/reversed payment stays open with owner; invoice paid does not mean grant received. |

### D. External grant/funder reviewer - PROXY ONLY

No first-person funder authority or validated procedures/preferences.
Motivation hypothesis: consistent funding assurance. Gain: inspectable,
data-minimized packs.

| Stage | Actions and channels | Pain / emotion hypothesis | Gain, handoff and exception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Receive application | Inspect route, template, applicant authority and proposed outputs through prescribed channel, currently unknown. | Uncertainty from misrouted packs. | Route-specific human review; wrong template returns for correction. |
| 2. Assess request | Examine evidence, contribution and delivery plausibility against actual applicable terms. | Concern about unsupported promises. | Questions to authorized applicant contact; no implied award. |
| 3. Record award outcome | Authorized funder process communicates decision/conditions; applicant records exact version. | Ambiguity if conditions disappear in summaries. | Award distinguished from application and cash; applicant confirms understanding. |
| 4. Review claim | Inspect measure-level evidence and approved changes; issue queries or disposition through actual process. | Review fatigue from repeated attachments. | Clear correction trail to Finance; claim submission is not acceptance. |
| 5. Confirm cash/review closure | Reconcile authorized disbursement and outstanding queries; applicant verifies receipt independently. | Concern about premature closure. | Traceable disposition; later corrections stay visible, with actual follow-up terms unknown. |

## Scenario guardrails and validation

**Illustrative scenario**, approved exercise inputs [S2, EXA-010/015], not
forecast: provider base = GBP 20,000 cost - GBP 7,500 fictional grant =
GBP 12,500. Pre-receipt exposure: GBP 20,000. Eligible match = GBP 16,000 -
GBP 7,500 = GBP 8,500; ineligible cost: GBP 4,000. Test volume, unit
cost/inflation, contingency, eligibility and receipt timing. Actual cash
schedule, terms and approval evidence remain needed.

Count checking, rework and assurance across all roles; report technology cost.
Compare frozen packs with checklist/ledger handling. The 25% review-time
criterion [S5] is proposed, not achieved. Released time is capacity until a
budget action makes it cashable. Synthetic results cannot prove grant gain.

Show role, source/date/version, gaps, owner, receiver and chase owner;
keyboard-readable comparisons and recoverable views. Receipt is not accepted
handoff [S6]. No unrestricted resident contact/health disclosure.

### Three validation questions

Ask sequentially, starting with question 1:

1. Where is the executed award or evidenced funding route, with complete terms?
2. Which independent identities/delegations govern preparation, capital approval,
   submission, payment authorization and execution?
3. Which approved cash baseline and pack support paired effort/error testing,
   including review and corrections?

## Exact finance control audit (2026-10-17, #94)

This is executable local engineering evidence, not a new interview, delegated
authority or live payment result. The issue's three criteria are narrower than
full PRD FR-018/AC-021/042. Current BRD FR-018 describes accepted human handoff
and RULE-003 describes hazard-service ownership; the issue's BRD finance
numbering is stale and is not silently relabelled here.

| Exact issue criterion | Current engineering evidence | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nothing paid by the product | Existing closed finance action set has no payment adapter; authenticated external-status notes preserve approved budgets, commitments and grant/cash records through reopen | Human-entered external status is not settlement evidence or product execution |
| Match exceptions are line-level | All exceptions identify affected invoice lines, including aggregate/duplicate blockers; missing budget/commitment/milestone and ambiguous commitment prevent a clean match | Supplied milestone reference is not verification of actual acceptance authority |
| Preparer cannot approve | Current registered identity plus captured email/code/OID prevents self-approval through aliases; distinct staff in the same permitted role can approve | Synthetic identity mechanics do not establish real company delegations |

The [core contract and repeatable checks](../../../../../src/core/README.md#record-only-finance-controls-94)
and [authenticated API contract](../../../../../src/api/README.md#record-only-finance-api-94)
preserve holds, independent disposition and record-only payment notes.
Acceptance rechecks current match inputs instead of trusting a previous
`Matched` label. A new unresolved duplicate blocks acceptance of the original
until explicitly resolved. JSON caught write failures restore prior finance and
ledger; SQLite/SQL Server use existing transactions, current staff checks and
rowversion conflicts. Fourteen cases passed against isolated local SQL Server
on 2026-10-17, including fresh role/OID, rollback, reopen and concurrent writers.
Separate JSON files do not promise abrupt-crash atomicity. No private dataset,
real provider/financial commitment, inference or Azure action was used.

Independent review identified a persistent file-replacement failure that could
retain a false approval audit when the recovery save also failed. The
coordinator correction now rolls the ledger back independently of that retry,
with a failing-first primitive-level regression and 55 passing focused
JSON/SQLite/HTTP/finance cases afterward. If state restoration itself cannot
persist, the error explicitly marks the durable outcome potentially unknown
and requires rereading persisted records before retry. The original review
verdict is not relabelled as a fresh independent pass.

#93 is hierarchy, not a dependency. #90 procurement/capacity and #84 technical
signoffs remain separate. The only actual shared production touchpoint is the
small `SqlStore.save` finance validator hook; Store/server and signoffs are
unchanged. Engineering acceptance of these exact criteria is not full PRD,
browser/accessibility, funding/legal, live-platform or human adoption acceptance.
