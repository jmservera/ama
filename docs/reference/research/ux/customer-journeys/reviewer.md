## Two bounded creative assistance ideas

Sector-informed hypotheses, later exploratory only: neither changes initial
deterministic supervision/reporting [C2, Agentic Architecture Brief].

1. **Counterclaim escrow.** Invented ask: "Keep the strongest contrary
   evidence beside each investment claim." A read-only agent proposes
   claim-to-source links and unresolved counterclaims when permitted pack
   versions change. Inputs: authorized papers, source versions, frozen
   definitions and limitation records; no unrestricted resident notes.
   Reviewer checks citations; domain owners answer; deterministic condition
   status remains authoritative. Missing versions yield "not comparable",
   not reconstructed history. Non-AI comparator: annotated paper, evidence
   index and independent peer challenge. Test critical unsupported claims
   missed, false objections and total preparation/review/correction effort.
   No generated assurance opinion or approval.

2. **Opportunity-cost rehearsal.** Invented ask: "If grant cash is late,
   whose service or review workload absorbs our proposed acceleration?"
   An agent suggests source-linked questions across Finance, Programme,
   Housing and QA; approved spreadsheets calculate stresses. Inputs:
   scoped commitments, cash timing, capacity and declared scenarios.
   Finance validates arithmetic, service owners validate consequences;
   Board/delegated owners alone choose trade-offs. Unknown displaced work
   prevents a conclusion. Non-AI comparator: facilitated pre-mortem with
   cash/capacity workbook. Test confirmed omissions, invented dependencies,
   all-role review cost and comprehension, not assumed avoided losses.
   Human workshop remains available if assistance fails.
