## Classification and scope

**Case-study fact:** 60,000 homes, small-team spreadsheet coordination; delivery constraints are unproven [S1, Business Challenge]. **Owner-confirmed company fact:** not-for-profit (2026-09-26), Excel coordination and no Dataverse/Dynamics estate (2026-10-03) [S2, Implementation Options]. Legal form, charitable status, surplus policy and board priorities remain **Unknown company detail** [S3, Priority Company Gaps].

**Synthetic scenario assumption / exercise assumption:** generated 60,000-home stock/retained 980-case lineage is not customer observation. Unmatched relationships stay unknown; test cohorts cannot replace lineage; invented details do not justify publication [S2, Subsequent Application Exercise Builds, 2026-10-11].

All maps are **Sector-informed hypotheses**, not interviews or emotional observations. "Current" describes hypothesised fragmented-record work, not implemented features. Channels, staffing and thresholds remain unknown. Coverage is all four assigned perspectives, not every programme user.

Data stewardship/test design is within this fictional mandate; Digital is adjacent. DPO/legal/control and independent audit/regulatory assurance are **proxy hypotheses outside mandate**, not simulated authority. Residents and domain owners retain decisions [S3, Role and Decision Map; S4, Stakeholders and Roles].

Separate mission, equity and stewardship from financial resilience/whole-life cost; never optimise shareholder return. No benefit or authority is inferred.

## Current map 1: data steward / performance analyst

Job hypothesis: make a defensible, reproducible account of where homes wait, without converting incomplete records into invented certainty [S5, Time Attribution; S6, Stock Prioritisation].

| 1. Reporting request | Agree population, as-of date and definitions through Programme discussion/read-only extracts. | Explain delivery. | Unease over shifting definitions. | Stable denominator/scope. | Benefits owner freezes definitions; missing baseline stays unknown. |
| 2. Sources disagree | Compare namespace-qualified IDs, buildings, dates and values in reconciliation worksheets. | Prevent wrong-home evidence. | Frustration with plausible matches. | Conflict queue/resolution owner. | Asset data owner resolves identity; no fuzzy merge/latest-wins rule. |
| 3. Evidence changes | Trace versions, scope and dependent approvals; preserve corrections/history. | Report valid review status. | Anxiety over stale approval. | Affected dependencies/unaffected siblings. | Control owners re-review; analyst cannot renew or transfer approvals. |
| 4. Analysis requested | Deterministically reconstruct active/queue/pause intervals, including incomplete/excluded/reopened cases. | Distinguish competing constraints. | Pressure to attribute everything. | Counts, coverage and contrary evidence. | Disputed causes remain disputed; Programme/Contracts/Resident teams validate. |
| 5. Report challenged | Supply accessible tables, calculations, exclusions and caveats through controlled export. | Avoid overstated attribution. | Defensiveness over caveats. | Versioned, correctable explanation. | Separate dashboard/benefits scorecard; seasonal outcomes and missing timestamps stay pending. |

Supporting fields: source authority, ID crosswalk, event entry/exit, handoff acceptance, evidence/dependency/code versions [S2, EXA-003/013; S4, RULE-015, FR-033; S5, Minimum Evidence Specification]. ## Future opportunities: invented asks, not customer quotations

Every row is a **Sector-informed hypothesis / invented concept**. Future AI analytics and reporting narratives are explicitly outside the current pilot. Initial reporting, supervision, calculations and dependent-approval controls remain deterministic [S4, Digital Foundation and Authority; S6, Programme Supervisor].

| Invented ask / concept | Agent task and proactive assistance | Required sources / access | Approval boundary / fallback | Net-value test / non-AI comparator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "Which earlier decisions might this correction undermine?" Evidence change blast-radius scout | Suggest semantic dependencies across approved document versions; proactively draft affected/unaffected review list with citations and uncertainty. | Read-only evidence, dependency graph, crosswalk and approval versions; scoped case/field rights. | Owners confirm affected scope; deterministic invalidation remains authoritative. Missing links stay unknown; use manual checklist. | Held-out changes: missed dependencies, false review burden and net reviewer minutes versus explicit dependency graph plus manual tracing. |
| "Try to disprove our clean handover story." Adversarial audit rehearsal | Draft counterexamples, evidence questions and withheld-case tests; proactively identify claims lacking reconstruction. | Approved assurance exports, frozen definitions, negative fixtures and limitation register; no unrestricted resident access. | Actual auditors choose scope and conclusions; no certification. Human checklist survives failure. | Blind seeded gaps: critical misses, unsupported accusations, corrections and total review cost versus peer review and deterministic audit tests. |
| "Who pays the hidden cost of our faster queue?" Fairness friction detective | Suggest competing explanations for contact/access friction and effort shifted to residents or officers; proactively flag coverage bias. | Purpose-approved aggregates, channel/adjustment categories, queue events and effort studies; suppression and access controls. | Engagement/Housing/Data/DPO validate interpretation; no inferred health, refusal, ranking or persuasion. Use descriptive stratified BI. | Seeded scenarios and later lawful testing: actionable confirmed issues, false signals, recording burden and distributional outcomes versus cohort tables and staff listening. |
| "What could this privacy request touch?" Rights-request lineage navigator | Draft a cited inventory of related records, exports and dependent reports; proactively surface unknown copies and holds. | Approved request metadata, data-class inventory, access/disclosure logs and retention references; least privilege. | DPO/Legal assess rights and response; never erase, refuse or approve interpretation. Manual inventory remains available. | Inventory completeness, false links, unnecessary exposure and total handling effort versus register searches and human case checklist. |
| "What work is invisible during this outage?" Recovery handoff companion | Draft role-scoped business-impact questions and pending-owner summaries from failed runs. | Approved run/version metadata, monitored queues and existing recovery procedure; no operational write tools. | Operator/control owners authorise recovery; never replay or release gates. Deterministic queue/runbook remains primary. | Seeded outages: missed/unowned tasks, false alerts and recovery-review effort versus monitoring rules and rehearsed manual runbook. |

Across concepts, test on constructed, clearly labelled records first. No company/resident data goes to vendors or third parties; any later proposal requires separate authorised processing review. Preserve source/model/template versions, human accept/edit/reject reasons and exportable evidence [S4, FR-044]. Stop expansion if critical omissions, unsupported claims, disclosure or shifted burden negate value. Saved minutes are released capacity, not cash without Finance validation.

## Three research questions

1. Which human owners can confirm identity, field authority and dependent-review invalidation using conflicting-source examples, and which relationships must remain unresolved?
2. Can each scoped user identify the next owner, caveat and manual route in a keyboard-accessible conflict/outage/privacy scenario without mistaking a draft or receipt for approval?
3. Which future concept beats its non-AI comparator after correction, assurance, support, resident burden and whole-life cost, without worsening rights, equity or financial resilience?
