## Journey 1: Asset Executive, current-state hypothesis

Job: convert finite investment into safe resident/asset outcomes through
portfolio reviews and investment papers, not every home's case screen (C3/C5/C6).

| Stage | Actions and touchpoints | Motivation; pain/emotion hypothesis | Desired gain; handoff and exception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Trigger | Receive under-delivery, condition concern or annual planning request through Programme briefings and spreadsheets. | Protect existing homes; urgency and uncertainty about the binding constraint. | Defined decision/problem; Programme and Data investigate, Safety handles urgent hazards separately. |
| 2. Establish baseline | Compare stock/EPC, condition coverage, planned maintenance and building dependencies with data owners. | Avoid wrong interventions; frustration at conflicting extracts. | Traceable confidence/unknowns; Data resolves conflicts, competent specialists interpret condition. Missing records trigger investigation, not exclusion. |
| 3. Shape options | Compare cohorts, sequencing and displaced repairs/development investment with Finance and Programme. | Mission stewardship and lifecycle value; tension between grant timing and lasting benefit. | Capacity-backed alternatives; Finance tests gross cash and affordability, Procurement verifies capacity, Residents retain choice. Unsecured funding stays uncertain. |
| 4. Recommend/approve | Assemble evidence, exclusions and downside cases; approve only within actual delegation or seek sponsor/board review. | Defensible decisions; anxiety over hidden commitments. | Named approvals and boundaries; no invented monetary limit. Safety, technical, resident and Finance decisions remain independent. |
| 5. Assure outcomes | Review accepted handovers, recurring defects, resident experience, cash and variance; feed learning into asset plan. | Real improvement rather than activity; possible disappointment if starts mask returns. | Rephase or pause affected investment through humans; Programme owns recovery, QA accepts quality. Reopened work and seasonal outcomes remain visible. |

## Journey 2: CEO/Board sponsor and assurance reader

Job: judge mission, affordability and assurance. CEO sponsorship differs from
board decisions. Accessible papers may suffice; no shared screen or portal
is assumed (C1/C2/C3/C7).

| Stage | Actions and touchpoints | Motivation; pain/emotion hypothesis | Desired gain; handoff and exception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Agenda trigger | CEO receives Assets proposition; board considers a capital/risk request through its actual governance route. | Advance resident mission; scepticism about technology-first urgency. | Explicit decision and non-AI alternative; sponsor requests missing justification. Governance route remains unknown. |
| 2. Read assurance | Inspect balanced paper: baseline, denominators, unknowns, resident outcomes and separate grant states. | Understand what is evidenced; overload or false reassurance from headline dashboards. | Reconciled, accessible tables; Assets/Data explain provenance. Illustrations must not imply achieved handovers or grant cash. |
| 3. Challenge | Question affordability, displacement, safety, capacity and adverse outcomes with Finance and independent specialists. | Preserve resilience and trust; discomfort with optimistic assumptions. | Credible downside and dissent; specialist owners answer. Missing assurance is an unresolved condition, not presumed clearance. |
| 4. Decide conditions | Record human decision, scope, conditions and accountable sponsor using established processes. | Maintain meaningful accountability; concern over ambiguous delegation. | Clear decision record; actual authorized body determines envelope. Exercise approval cannot authorize procurement, consent, safety release or live spending. |
| 5. Review/renew | Compare assured outcomes and guardrails before another tranche; request corrective action or further evidence. | Sustain mission without locking in failure; cautious confidence. | Independent benefit evidence and explicit pause/exit choices; Assets owns recommendations, Finance cash, Programme recovery. Pending seasonal benefits defer claims. |

## Agent-assisted opportunities

Programme supervision/reporting remain deterministic: no initial-pilot AI
summarisation (C2/C5). Candidates are not deployed/approved capabilities.
Measurements include full human review; none is a forecast.

| Placement and invented request | Agent task/proactive action; required data | Autonomy/approval; failure/fallback | Testable gain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Upstream pilot candidate: Assets asks, "Which conflicting EPC facts need owner review before this cohort discussion?" | Reconcile permitted extracts/notes; proactively surface missing dates and contradictions. Needs source authority, identity crosswalk, versions and named owners. | Source-bound proposals only; data owners confirm. No ranking/priority changes. Ambiguous identity or model failure retains unknowns and manual comparison. | Critical conflicts missed; cohort preparation and review minutes versus rules/manual reconciliation. |
| LATER exploratory, non-obvious: Assets asks, "Which proposed works might duplicate or strand next year's maintenance?" | Build a measure/component dependency graph; flag possible abortive sequences before review. Needs technical scope, component lives, maintenance dates, costs and building links. | Advisory edges, never rescheduling or release. Specialists validate compatibility; unavailable plans yield incomplete analysis and a manual sequencing workshop. | Validated clashes found; net review effort; later evidenced avoided rework, not assumed savings. |
| LATER exploratory, non-obvious: CEO asks, "Pre-mortem this tranche if grant cash arrives late and QA returns double." | Generate source-bound failure pathways and missing contingency questions proactively; deterministic calculations supply sensitivities. Needs versioned cash, capacity, defects and approved scenario assumptions. | No programme supervision, risk acceptance or capital commitment. Finance/Safety/Assets challenge outputs; unsupported scenarios discarded; facilitated human pre-mortem remains available. | Material risks missed, reviewer effort and decision comprehension versus human workshop. |
| LATER exploratory, non-obvious: assurance reader asks, "Which claims in this paper changed evidence or denominator since our last decision?" | A claim-to-evidence change ledger flags broken support, stale sources and unresolved conditions rather than writing a reassuring summary. Needs prior papers, definitions, approvals and evidence versions. | Draft challenge list only; human assurance owner validates; no policy, hold or consent interpretation. Missing versions block comparison; manual annotated-paper review. | Unsupported claims detected, false alarms and assurance preparation/review time. |

Non-AI comparator: versioned data, scoring rules, maintenance calendar,
exception queues, human-owned condition log, reconciled scorecard and downside
workshop. AI cannot create crews, capital or resident agreement.
