Not fixed by this release, seen live:

* `GET /api/assistants/forecast` answers 503 "The forecast could not finish in time" after 13 to 17 s on both runs (container log `status=503` at 12:32:45, 12:34:42, 12:36:20). This is the #2304 path and is the likely confounder named in #2320.

* `?mode=workflow` still takes 9.6 to 13.9 s (it is a write path, not covered by the fix) and `/api/assistants` took 17 s once.

## Chat call cap and allowance close

* Two free-form questions were asked on case `ZF-2025-0031`. The first returned 200 after 38.75 s with `model: workflow-only` and the reply "This reply uses rules only because the assistant is not available". The log records `AI_LIMIT_BREACH reason=call-budget-limit route=workflow-only` at 12:33:32 UTC. This matches the 20 calls per UTC day demo cap pinned by the test in #2359: the hold is live and fails closed; the wording problem noted in the [0000060 note](./issue-2245-live-101805-2026-11-24.md) persists. The second thread create returned 503 (write-lock-busy).
* A "Model calls: 1" answer was not seen today because the day's allowance was already used.
* `close_allowance_after_restore` runs only after a point-in-time restore. A restore is a drill on the live database, outside the owner's demo authority, so it was not run. The live database has `retrofit` at Standard, backup storage redundancy Local and short-term retention 14 days (`az sql db show`, `str-policy show`).
