PAS 2035:2023 offers comprehensive guidance for energy retrofitting in existing homes, ensuring efficiency, sustainability, and improved performance.

## Conduct effective retrofits with PAS 2035

[View the BSI executive briefing](https://www.bsigroup.com/siteassets/pdf/en/insights-and-media/insights/white-papers/retrofit_standards_executive_briefing.pdf).

* PAS 2035 sets out best practice for end to end energy efficiency and 'whole house' retrofits.
* PAS 2035 is crucial for achieving net zero targets and decarbonization of the built environment.
* Its use is mandatory for some government funded projects, ensuring aligned energy efficiency.
