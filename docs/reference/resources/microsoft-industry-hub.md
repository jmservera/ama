## Purpose

The Microsoft Industry Hub is an internal Microsoft sales-enablement site on
Seismic. It helps the project find use cases and solutions that Microsoft has
already delivered with other customers, so the Zava Forward proposal can reuse
proven patterns rather than design everything from scratch.

The nonprofit section is the closest match to the customer. Zava North Homes
is a not-for-profit housing association, as described in the
[Housing Association Primer](../research/sector/housing-association-primer.md).
Public sector and social services material is also relevant because social
landlords share regulated, resident-facing service obligations.

## Nonprofit Hero Use Cases

The nonprofit hero use case decks group the leading patterns into four use
cases, each described with buyer personas, business outcomes, customer
evidence and Microsoft and partner solutions:

1. Automate manual nonprofit program operations
2. Translate program data and documents to improve participant outcomes
3. Improve frontline response and resource allocation
4. Streamline operations with AI-powered case and compliance management

Named buyers include the Director of Operations, Program Director, Compliance
Director, Operations Manager and Finance Director, with case managers and
customer support as influencers. Source:

## Lessons for the Proposal

These are sector-informed hypotheses drawn from the hub material:

* Build inside the tools staff already use. King County Housing Authority
  framed adoption as moving forward with AI inside existing guardrails.
* Avoid adding data entry and avoid replacing every legacy system as a
  prerequisite. The UCAN training scenario, a fictional workshop case, shows
  case managers rejecting a third unified-data initiative that adds screens
  or needs a separate login.
* Unified data and identity matching are the foundation for early warning.
  An agent trained on incomplete records can flag the wrong people and destroy
  frontline trust.
* Position AI as supporting human judgement and stewardship. The FY27
  nonprofit messaging framework uses this framing, which matches the approval
  boundaries in the Zava Forward design.
* Funders and boards expect continuous, auditable impact reporting. This
  supports the Quality Verification Agent and grant reporting evidence packs.

## Evidence Rules

* Classify hub material as internal comparator evidence. A result achieved by
  another customer is not a fact about Zava North Homes or a forecast for
  the Zava Forward programme.
* Record the hub item title, date viewed and the claim it supports. Keep the
  customer's context, such as sector, scale and country, next to any figure.
* Prefer public customer stories when citing evidence in a deliverable.
* Do not copy confidential customer names, figures or documents into this
  repository unless the material is approved for that use. Prefer a short
  summary and a link.
* Keep the regulatory, resident consent and human approval boundaries in the
  repository instructions. A prior deployment does not show that a control is
  sufficient for a UK social landlord.
