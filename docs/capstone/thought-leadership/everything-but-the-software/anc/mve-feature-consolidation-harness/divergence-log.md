## 2026-10-24: Human Intervention Measurement

The owner requested HIR after a focused review found no operational definition
in the paper. This amendment precedes execution according to the protocol's
recorded unexecuted status; no experiment was launched in this revision.

* Added observed HIR and intervention-demand rate as secondary descriptive
  measures, plus event counts, reasons, actions and active human minutes.
* Defined the denominator as attempted feature cycles, including their
  assigned post-feature step and failed cycles. Change requests are separate.
* Kept primary runs unassisted. Corrective coaching or code/test repair is a
  retained, labelled deviation without replacement, not an invisible rescue;
  any confirmatory sample shortfall must be reported as such.
* Separated routine launches, planned evaluation, required approvals and
  safety stops from corrective rescue. Lower HIR is not inherently better.
* Added per-run reporting and a run-resampled interval for the B-A demand-rate
  difference. Hypotheses, thresholds, arms, budgets and adoption gates remain
  unchanged; no results, registration or protocol freeze are claimed.

Event-coding rules and the runner's enforcement of this policy must be frozen
before the pilot with the rest of the protocol. The amendment changes only
the published specification; it does not implement or validate an event runner.
