\section{Supplement}\label{sec:supplement}

The Supplement gives the evidence needed to examine and reproduce the
analysis: source qualifications, measurement definitions, detailed results
and execution records. It also includes checks that could distinguish the
case explanations, an assumed cost model and proposed follow-up designs.
Those checks and designs remain unperformed or unapproved as specified.
Section~\ref{sec:experiment} explains the registered base and the disclosed,
unregistered amendments. Historical freezes below are version-specific.

For the result tables, $P_{10}$ means the final ten-feature checkpoint and
CR1 the withheld regional-access extension. \armlegend\ Full source and
digest identifiers are consolidated in the end register
(Table~\ref{tab:source-register}); historical records retain their own scope.
