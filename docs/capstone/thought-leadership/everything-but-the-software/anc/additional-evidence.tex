\subsection{Additional source qualifications}\label{app:additionalevidence}

Retained literature claims reflect specific scope and formatting details in
the inspected primary texts. Gloaguen et~al.'s Table~3 records the
developer-versus-LLM comparison on CTXbench as $p=0.038$, alongside
percentage-formatted p-values in the surrounding prose; the manuscript uses
the table value. Borg et~al.'s two-phase study (151 participants, 95\%
professional developers) was conducted in late 2024 with IDE and chat
assistants predating autonomous coding agents: Phase~1 was observational,
while Phase~2 randomised human evolution of Phase~1 solutions without AI
assistance and found no significant differences in completion time or
CodeHealth~\cite[Secs.~1, 2.2, 4]{borg2025echoes}. In the SEC's Knight
Capital order (Exchange Act Release No.~\nolinkurl{34-70694}, Paragraphs~1, 12--17) and
companion press release \nolinkurl{2013-222}, a 2005 change moved cumulative-quantity
tracking in discontinued Power Peg code without retesting it, and a July
2012 deployment omitted one of eight SMARS servers, triggering millions of
child orders, more than four million executions from 212 parent orders, and
a loss exceeding USD~460 million~\cite{sec2013knight}. Non-essential
citations that had only abstract, excerpt or unverified third-party caption
access were removed rather than retained with unresolved gaps.

Merge-outcome studies measure different populations and outcomes.
Peralta et al.\ started with 9,799 human-reviewed agent PRs and manually
inspected 717. The 35.7\% clear-failure share among rejections and 15.4\%
explicit reviewer-involvement share among merges describe that manual sample
~\cite{peralta2026merged}. Murphy-Hill et al.\ estimate roughly 24\% more merges among adopters in an
observational rollout study~\cite{murphyhill2026agents}. Delivered value
was outside that output measure. AIDev contains
932,791 agent-authored PRs from five agents across 116,211 repositories
~\cite{li2026aidev}. Its size does not establish how many user outcomes were accepted.

Pavlenko et al.'s World Model offers an additional context-management
design: preserve raw evidence, derive rebuildable views and retrieve compact,
task-specific context with provenance and access controls
~\cite{pavlenko2026factory}. Applied to this case, that would mean giving
a sign-in worker the relevant role/staff-binding decision and its source,
while preserving the larger discovery record for other tasks.
Its effect on the AMA case was not tested.
