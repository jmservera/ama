\subsection{Additional source qualifications}\label{app:additionalevidence}

Some sources contain discrepancies or were only partly accessible.
In Laban et~al.'s arXiv v1, the abstract and Section~6.1 report a 39\%
relative performance drop, while Figure~1's caption says 35\%.
The 14 November source check left that discrepancy unresolved. The main
text uses 39\% from the results passage; neither figure measures repository
decay.
Gloaguen et~al.'s Table~3 records the developer-versus-LLM comparison as
$p=0.038$, despite percentage-formatted p-values in the surrounding prose.
The manuscript uses the table value. Besker et~al.'s 43-developer/23\%
claim has institutional-abstract support; the full original and the
tool-generation qualifier in the Echoes of AI source remain subject to the
access limits recorded in the ancillary source review.
The Knight Capital citation now identifies SEC press release 2013-222,
the source supported by the retained primary-page inspection, instead of
the unavailable related order. A fresh full-page request returned HTTP 403;
indexed SEC text corroborated the title, date, order counts and loss.
This correction does not claim inspection of the order or a fresh audit
of all cited works.

Merge-outcome studies measure different populations and outcomes.
Peralta et al. started with 9,799 human-reviewed agent PRs and manually
inspected 717. The 35.7\% clear-failure share among rejections and 15.4\%
explicit reviewer-involvement share among merges describe that manual sample
~\cite{peralta2026merged}. Murphy-Hill et al. estimate roughly 24\% more merges among adopters in an
observational rollout study~\cite{murphyhill2026agents}. Delivered value
was outside that output measure. AIDev contains
932,791 agent-authored PRs from five agents across 116,211 repositories
~\cite{li2026aidev}. Its size does not establish how many user outcomes were accepted.

Pavlenko et al.'s World Model offers an additional context-management
design: preserve raw evidence, derive rebuildable views and retrieve compact,
task-specific context with provenance and access controls
~\cite{pavlenko2026factory}. This offers an alternative to continually expanding the required read path.
Its effect on the AMA case was not tested.
