\subsection{Additional source qualifications}\label{app:additionalevidence}

Retained literature claims reflect specific scope and formatting details in
the inspected primary texts. Gloaguen et~al.'s Table~3 records the
developer-versus-LLM comparison on CTXbench as $p=0.038$, alongside
percentage-formatted p-values in the surrounding prose; the manuscript uses
the table value. Borg et~al.'s two-phase study (151 participants, 95\%
professional developers) was conducted in late 2024 with IDE and chat
assistants predating autonomous coding agents: Phase~1 was observational,
while Phase~2 randomised human evolution of Phase~1 solutions without AI
assistance and found no significant differences in completion time or
CodeHealth~\cite[Secs.~1, 2.2, 4]{borg2025echoes}. In the SEC's Knight
Capital order (Exchange Act Release No.~\nolinkurl{34-70694}, Paragraphs~1, 12--17) and
companion press release \nolinkurl{2013-222}, a 2005 change moved cumulative-quantity
tracking in discontinued Power Peg code without retesting it, and a July
2012 deployment omitted one of eight SMARS servers, triggering millions of
child orders, more than four million executions from 212 parent orders, and
a loss exceeding USD~460 million~\cite{sec2013knight}. Non-essential
citations that had only abstract, excerpt or unverified third-party caption
access were removed rather than retained with unresolved gaps.

Merge-outcome studies measure different populations and outcomes.
Peralta et al.\ started with 9,799 human-reviewed agent PRs and manually
inspected 717. The 35.7\% clear-failure share among rejections and 15.4\%
explicit reviewer-involvement share among merges describe that manual sample
~\cite{peralta2026merged}. Murphy-Hill et al.\ estimate roughly 24\% more merges among adopters in an
observational rollout study~\cite{murphyhill2026agents}. Delivered value
was outside that output measure. AIDev contains
932,791 agent-authored PRs from five agents across 116,211 repositories
~\cite{li2026aidev}. Its size does not establish how many user outcomes were accepted.

Pavlenko et al.'s World Model offers an additional context-management
design: preserve raw evidence, derive rebuildable views and retrieve compact,
task-specific context with provenance and access controls
~\cite{pavlenko2026factory}. Applied to this case, that would mean giving
a sign-in worker the relevant role/staff-binding decision and its source,
while preserving the larger discovery record for other tasks.
Its effect on the AMA case was not tested.


\subsubsection{Case detail moved from the main article}

\paragraph{Historical deployment example}
Software development had this problem long before coding agents existed, as
public post-mortems and classic maintenance accounts show. On the 1st of
August 2012, for instance, Knight Capital's automated router sent millions
of orders and obtained more than four million executions while processing
212 customer orders, costing the firm more than USD~460 million. The SEC
traced that public incident to a defective function left in the router after
a 2005 change, an incomplete deployment for a new trading programme across
one of eight servers, and inadequate controls; the new deployment activated
old code that was no longer meant to run~\cite{sec2013knight}. The failure
crossed a feature boundary and a seven-year interval, so understanding it
required examining the code and deployment state that the new programme
inherited.

\paragraph{Original article roadmap}
\noindent The investigation moves from earlier studies to my project and
then to a separate coding experiment:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item We review earlier studies of errors carried through conversations,
inherited code and maintained instructions, including published comparisons
of agent-written and developer-written histories (Section~\ref{sec:background}).
\item We trace the move from questioning requirements with agents to
coordinated feature delivery, then my return to trying the product and
requesting fixes. Five possible explanations are examined against episodes
in the delivery record
(Sections~\ref{sec:method}--\ref{sec:mechanisms}).
\item We test scheduled consolidation in a 315-trajectory, five-arm
exploratory study, then replay earlier workflows and have a separate AI
judge rate the execution evidence. The results distinguish implementing a
feature from preserving it and adapting it later. The study also exposed
a task too easy to demonstrate the planned coverage gain and incomplete
billing. Its earlier planning drew on the registered-report example of
Lulla et~al.~\cite{lulla2026protocol} and HVE Core's Minimum Viable Experiment method
(Sections~\ref{sec:harness} and~\ref{sec:experiment}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Coordinator-history recovery and model selectors}
Read-only recovery of the original coordinator history corroborates my
requests for RPI tracking, tmux workers and recurring checks, followed by
migration, budget and functional-checkpoint decisions. Later committed
instructions selected \texttt{claude-sonnet-5.5} for worker launches while
leaving the coordinator on Automatic. Actual historical CLI versions,
reviewer models and backend builds remain unavailable in the inspected
records. The evidence index distinguishes prescribed selectors, stored
instructions and assistant-reported outcomes.

\paragraph{Case extraction and analysis scope}
The original analysis and later follow-up use separate repository snapshots
(source records \sourceref{R01} and \sourceref{R02} in the end register).
Sources include the knowledge-base
log and index, requirements, architecture decisions, coordinator definition,
issues, pull requests and retained RPI records. My review adds
process clarification and an export of project issue states, retained as
\nolinkurl{anc/project-insights-2026-11-01.csv}. Source dates, historical
counts and extraction rules remain in the ancillary records. The main
account uses them to reconstruct the sequence and handoffs.

I analysed descriptive measures, traced references to recorded lessons and
used AI assistance to interpret selected episodes. I did not systematically
code every log entry or review every specification. Early entries were
reconstructed from history; later entries were written during the project.
The evidence record distinguishes my recollection from committed source and
execution observations. It also records missing links between assessment
scores, releases and deployed tasks.

External repositories and literature were selected for relevance to
inherited faults, instruction maintenance and outcome verification.
They supply contextual comparisons and a focused narrative synthesis.
The published-evidence figures reproduce reported values with source
locators; the external applications and original studies were not rerun.
The ancillary observation record preserves its purposive issue sample,
exclusions and retrieval boundaries. These sources are analysed separately
from the motivating project.

\paragraph{Participant-observer analysis and independent checking}
I selected the episodes, designed the coordinator and had an interest in
successful delivery. Those roles favour a coherent retrospective
explanation. Experience-based beliefs can differ from project
evidence~\cite{devanbu2016belief}; the same caution applies to AI-written
records and AI-assisted synthesis. Independent checking of O1--O5 against
retained source and execution records remains outstanding.
Jiang et~al.\ make mechanism analysis inspectable through matched trajectories,
evidence-linked labels and a separate human check of taxonomy construction,
while distinguishing that check from exhaustive human coding~\cite[Sec.~4, App.~A.2]{jiang2026skills}.
Here the five mechanisms organise selected episodes.
Their prevalence and causal effects require a different
design. The evidence index retains null release or execution identifiers
where a link still needs checking.

\paragraph{Original case validity and rival-explanation account}


The five categories overlap in this case. Missing identity and staff
bindings could reflect deferred migration, incomplete deployment acceptance
or a lesson that workers did not apply. The deployment comparison and
session-input trace in Table~\ref{tab:audittrail} could help distinguish
these explanations, while document and citation counts cannot. The available
evidence also cannot establish context overflow from a growing read path, or
disproportionate cost from an expensive gate alone. I did not measure
per-change gate time, avoided defects or human effort.

The source-layout migration, owner-selected priorities and delegated merging
changed alongside coordination. Scope additions, budget constraints, model/tool
changes and deployment conditions also differ between periods. Any of these
could help explain the outcome attributed to autonomy. The
\hyperref[sec:supplement]{supplementary rival-explanation register} retains the full
register, separating recorded circumstances from plausible but untested
alternatives.

Construct validity is limited by whitespace counts, lexical indicators and
selectively reviewed records. Appropriate caution can raise boundary-phrase
density, and the records cannot show whether a missing reference reflects
absent knowledge or missing documentation. I was coordinator designer,
participant and analyst, so I selected the episodes and may favour a coherent
retrospective explanation. AI-assisted synthesis shares these source limits
and needs independent checking.
Public records support recomputation but not independent corroboration of
every statement. The single exercise I designed and the purposively selected
external examples do not establish prevalence or general causal effects.
The separate experiment has its own identification limits in
Section~\ref{sec:protocollimits}.

\paragraph{Contextual comparisons from the opening.}

The migration instruction at coordinator turn 66 was ``build all new in
src, but migrate what's needed first''. The article paraphrases this
instruction; the case evidence index supplies the original locator.

Cunningham's 1992 account of WyCash described a financial product
continually adapted to market demands, where mature code was repeatedly
revised and rewritten; his technical-debt metaphor explained why shipping a
first-time implementation required later rewriting as understanding
improved~\cite{cunningham1992wycash}.

The external PhotoCraft project, an early-alpha reimplementation of
Photoshop, reports 631 of 631 menu items live, while its dated
assessment estimates about 76\% feature breadth and 45\%
readiness for real work~\cite{photocraft2026}. SWE-Maintain finds that agents solve
fewer later tasks when starting from their own earlier changes than from
the developers' changes, even though each task starts in a fresh
session~\cite{pan2026swemaintain}.
