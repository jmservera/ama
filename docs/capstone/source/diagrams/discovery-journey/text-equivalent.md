| Row | 01 Contact | 02 Survey | 03 Decide | 04 Installation | 05 Live with it |

| Human role, proposed | Housing | Assessor | Resident | Programme | QA / Housing |

| Pain (hyp.) | Story repeated | Needs missed | Rushed choice | Disrupted day | Concern lost |
| Gain (prop.) | Clear contact | Visit support | Pause / defer | Agreed visit | Owned help |

1. Contact: the resident reads an invitation or raises a concern through a
   safe phone, letter or assisted route. The need is to be heard and know who
   will respond. The repetition hypothesis motivates a verified account and
   named update contact, not indiscriminate story sharing. Housing checks
   identity, safe contact and any representative authority. Urgent safety
   concerns enter human repairs independently. Basis: R01, R03, R06 and R09.
2. Survey: the resident asks about access, duration and practical support.
   Missed language, format or household needs are a hypothesis, not a measured
   resident deficit. The proposed response is a supported visit and sourced
   preparation; the assessor owns observations, with liaison support.
   Survey access is not works consent or permission for wider disclosure.
   Basis: R06, R07, R10 and R11.
3. Decide: the resident weighs a specific works proposal, including disruption
   and unanswered cost questions. The pressure hypothesis motivates time,
   explanation and measure/scope-specific choice: agree, defer, decline,
   pause or withdraw. Changed scope needs a fresh supplied decision. The
   resident decides; liaison supports; professionals and Finance retain their
   own decisions. No silence, login, visit or representative relationship
   establishes works consent. Basis: R01, R02, R06 and R09.
4. Installation: the resident coordinates work, care and ordinary routines.
   Disruption and unreliable arrangements are hypotheses. The proposed gain
   is an agreed visit based on actual capacity and current preferences,
   with practical support and an owned update if plans change. Programme
   coordinates, Contracts confirms supplier capacity and the resident
   confirms arrangements. An offer is not a booking; provider failure is not
   resident refusal. Basis: R06, R07 and R14.
5. Live with it: the resident practises controls, asks for usable instructions
   and reports defects, discomfort or higher bills. Losing the concern after
   nominal completion is a hypothesis. Owned help means accessible aftercare,
   competent investigation and a visible open remedy. Independent QA accepts
   or returns evidence separately from resident handover, complaint response,
   remedy and Finance closure. Normal sensors cannot negate a resident report;
   no bill saving or compensation is promised. Basis: R06-R09, R11 and R15-R17.

The visible footer retains phone, letter and assisted visits, independent QA
and the urgent safety bypass to human repairs at any moment. Withdrawal,
safeguarding and complaints/redress also remain parallel routes, never rewards
for reaching the next stage. Operational contacts, support availability,
postholders and service times are unknown until confirmed.

## Method and Evidence Classification

This is bounded DT synthesis for presentation, not a new research session or
an assertion that the nine-method process has been completed. Presentation
fidelity does not increase research maturity.

The evidence chain is case brief -> official-sector context -> role research
instruments -> stage-based journey hypotheses -> pain/gain synthesis -> bounded
design choices -> tests. The arrows mean traceability of reasoning, not proof
that each preceding source independently validates the following claim.

## What the Product Can Earn Credit For

The positive story is that Zava Forward gives the research concrete, bounded
engineering form. The evidence supports more than an attractive concept,
without needing to claim a housing benefit that has not been measured:

* R14 records offer/commitment separation, distinct human/capacity evidence,
  current permissions and provider-failure handling in the demonstrator
* R15 records per-measure QA independence and evidence replacement that
  invalidates dependent acceptance/handover, preserving history
* R16 records owned update commitments and separate response/remedy states,
  including recovery of departed owners without silently closing open work
* R17 records five actual generative proposal paths and durable SQL content
  surviving a restart in the historical operator exercise

These records retain their original adverse or pending independent Review
outcomes where stated. They do not prove current deployment readiness,
professional competence, effective human oversight, resident comprehension,
fairness, savings, grant capture or accepted whole-journey delivery. Gains in
the drawing remain proposed. The optional resident portal/answer task is
separately proposed and gated; neither mandatory portal use nor live automated
resident communication follows from this map.
