The original [Fabric SVG](./fabric.svg) and [Power BI SVG](./power-bi.svg)
were extracted unchanged from Microsoft's linked
[icon archive](https://github.com/microsoft/fabric-samples/blob/main/docs-samples/Icons.zip),
members `v6.1.0/package/dist/svg/fabric_48_color.svg` and
`v6.1.0/package/dist/svg/power_bi_48_color.svg`.
The [Fabric PNG](./fabric.png) and [Power BI PNG](./power-bi.png) are
512 x 512 native PowerPoint rasterizations, not redrawn/recoloured logos.

The [official icon guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/fundamentals/icons),
retrieved 2026-10-30, permits architectural diagrams, training, slide decks
and documentation. Keep product labels nearby and the supplied appearance;
do not crop, rotate, flip or distort. Do not represent these as Zava's own
brand or evidence of partnership, deployment or platform approval. Microsoft
reserves other rights; this page does not grant broader trademark permission.

The [asset catalog](../catalog.json) pins archive/member/source/derived hashes,
retrieval date, dimensions and exact slide usage. The native builder preserves
aspect ratio and verifies original SVG, PNG and packaged usage. Fabric appears
on main pages 14 and 23; Power BI's official icon appears on page 14.

Retain all four image inputs and the catalog for reconstruction. The source
SVGs carry the original design; PNGs supply cross-viewer presentation support.
No package dependency, paid service or tenant action was added to obtain them.
