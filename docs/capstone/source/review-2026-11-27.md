## Communication decision

The original demo-first review below is retained. The cost follow-up at the
end records the current route and wider financial framing without replacing
the earlier findings or adverse outcomes.

The mentor's recommendation, supplied by the owner, is to enter the demo as
soon as possible. The revised pitch gives a twenty-second recommendation,
uses ten seconds to establish the synthetic demo boundary and starts the
employee-site walkthrough at 00:30. The complete hook ends at 02:20.
The business point appears during its first minute: an unresolved check has
an accountable next action, while AI cannot accept the works.

The audience is a mixed AMA panel and executive room. Their exact roles and
prior knowledge are unconfirmed. The sequence gives the sponsor a decision,
then demonstrates resident choice before explaining the mechanisms and cost.
Technical depth follows the product example, so a diagram answers a question
the audience already has.

| Audience preference | What earns attention | Proof and action |

| Sergeant / sponsor | A recommendation and visible next action | One bounded same-home validation, named review functions and a separately costed approval |

These are communication preferences, not personality classifications.

## Completed study, with denominator limits

The study audit read sections of the
[Everything But The Software README](../thought-leadership/everything-but-the-software/README.md),
the `main.tex` abstract and the dated study/publication evidence JSON.
The design is an exploratory ten-feature Python task, three models, five arms
and 21 runs per cell, with one held-out change request.

The 15 November aggregate reports 315 completed trajectories, but judging
uses a 313-of-315 snapshot and older text reports two quarantined trajectories.
The transition was not reconciled by this presentation review.
The primary complete-pair comparison reports +1.19 percentage points
(95% interval 0.10 to 2.22), conditional on owner-attested coverage and below
the paper's ten-point practical benchmark. Longer delivery already retained
most features. One Gemini comparison completed the change request in 16/21
read-only-review runs versus 8/21 consolidation runs, with two extension
outcomes unknown. Billing is incomplete for 55 attempts; reported credit
ratios apply to fully billed subsets, not every trajectory.

Execution and data collection were separate from the designer. Both authors
work at Microsoft; this is not independent institutional validation.
The study supports task-specific, limited findings with added resource cost,
not customer savings, universal productivity or housing effectiveness.
Concurrent uncommitted paper revisions are outside this presentation change.
