## The Promise

Prove one bounded story: a home has conflicting evidence, a person owns the
next action, AI can help prepare a response, and the system does not turn that
response into consent or approval.

The demo is ten minutes separate from the 30-minute presentation. Aim for
eight minutes of prepared demonstration and two minutes of interruption or
recovery. S18's closing 30 seconds belong to the presentation allocation,
not an extra demo minute.

This is a proposed runbook, not a record that these actions have been executed.
No cloud, directory, model or data mutation is authorised by this document.

## Route Selection Before the Meeting

| Route | Select only when | Say on entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L: live exercise | Exact chosen build passes the checks below and authorised bounded scope is ready | "This is the preflighted Azure exercise using constructed data. I will show only the authorised case and actions." |
| E: repository evidence | Live prerequisites are incomplete; bundled contracts and dated observations are available | "Live end-to-end acceptance is not established. This is a source-and-recorded-evidence walkthrough, not a live system or fresh model call." |
| U: unavailable | Neither route can be explained with inspectable evidence | "The demonstration is unavailable. I will explain the intended control and the evidence still required rather than simulate a pass." |

The source baseline's latest cutover pack records direct-route 403 responses,
tenant/Graph limitations and private publishing capacity/bootstrap blockers.
Earlier real Azure/SQL/model results do not make route L ready today.
Select E unless fresh, independently inspected evidence establishes L.
Do not repair infrastructure in front of the audience.

## Preflight Checklist

Complete on the actual planned device and deployment before the meeting;
record time, source/image version, owner and Pass/Fail/Blocked/Not run.
Keep all credentials, endpoints, identities and private case mappings outside
the deck and this package.

* Verify the welcome, health, browser login/callback, assigned staff role,
  correct case and genuine wrong-role denial. A public health response or
  login redirect alone is insufficient.
* Verify the intended Azure SQL state and persistence for the authorised
  constructed scope. Do not reseed or open a private data file for the demo.
* Confirm the exact permitted AI task, source/model/version, current authority,
  finite call/token allowance, expiry, processing terms and spend approval.
  Capture source and usage without resident data or credentials.
* If no authority exists, do not enable it for the meeting. Show the
  unreviewed-proposal contract on route E instead.
* Verify that manual case work and the independent safety route do not need
  optional AI. Do not trigger a real emergency referral as a demo action.
* Verify one planned controlled denial on a designated disposable fixture,
  not a destructive experiment on retained programme history.
* Confirm a reversible action plan and recovery owner. Do not change live
  policy, approve works, send messages, submit grants or authorise payment.
* Close notifications, private terminal history, tenant directory pages,
  billing account details and unrelated browser tabs. Use a clean presentation
  browser profile without changing the account's effective rights.
* Prepare route E in advance: the seven bundled diagrams, the relevant
  source-register records and one approved, non-identifying evidence summary.
  Never use private generated addresses/names as a slide asset.
* Rehearse on the chosen route. If a step is unavailable in that exact build,
  remove the live action and label it as a contract, rather than invent its UI.

No live case or screenshot is bundled because the retained exercise data
contains real geography and private constructed programme details.
The folder is usable for authoring without those data.

## Eight-Minute Core Route

The routes tell the same story, but they are not equivalent evidence.
Route E cannot satisfy live-AI, Azure SQL or real-user acceptance.

| Clock | Business beat and words | Route L: preflighted behaviour | Route E: portable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00:00-00:40 | "Watch the evidence, the owner and the boundary. This is constructed, not a resident case." | Show chosen build and current authorised role without account identifiers | Show the E-route label, E07/E13 classifications and diagram 01 |
| 00:40-01:50 | "A top-ranked home is not automatically ready." | Open the designated home's queue/context; show known versus unknown source state | Use S03's fictional home journey and E02 exercise assumption; do not claim a screenshot is live |
| 01:50-03:00 | "The conflicting fact has an owner and a dependent review." | Show the pre-arranged conflict, source/version and owned next action | Explain the source/version and review contracts from E07/E08; mark implementation versus live acceptance |
| 03:00-04:20 | "AI prepares a proposal; it does not resolve the domain decision." | Make at most the single pre-authorised call, or inspect its previously prepared unreviewed proposal with that date visible | Use diagram 04 and the dated five-path invocation observation; do not animate fake text generation |
| 04:20-05:20 | "Now check the cited source and what the model did not know." | Inspect permitted citations, output source and review state; show usage if available, otherwise unknown | Explain citation-presence versus semantic-truth limits; show 6,884/five as historical aggregate, never today's call |
| 05:20-06:25 | "A draft does not become consent or independent acceptance." | Show the preflighted review/control boundary; do not perform an irreversible approval | Show the separated offer/agreement, provider completion/QA and proposal/domain-action contracts |
| 06:25-07:15 | "This is the next accountable action, not a disappearing amber dot." | Return to current owned work and retained outcome; if no state was changed, say so | Show the fictional journey's owned unresolved next step and the exact missing acceptance |
| 07:15-08:00 | "This tells us what to test, not what to claim." | Debrief what was actually observed today, including limitations | Debrief that only contracts and historical observations were inspected; live session and value remain unproved |

At 08:00 stop advancing the prepared flow. Use the remaining two minutes
for a customer question or one recovery event. If unused, summarise the
evidence gate and go to S18; do not add extra unprepared features.

## Exact Transition and Debrief

Transition from S17:

> "We have talked about the architecture. Now judge whether the evidence,
> ownership and decision boundary are visible in one case. I will state
> what is live and what is recorded before we start."

Route E debrief:

> "We inspected the intended same-home control and the repository's dated
> engineering evidence. We did not establish a current staff session,
> perform fresh inference or measure a business benefit. Those are the
> reasons for the next validation gate, not claims hidden in this demo."

Route L debrief must be filled by the presenter from the actual observations,
not copied as a prewritten success statement:

> "Today we observed [name only the inspected behaviour]. We did not test
> the whole programme, real resident comprehension or operational scale.
> The remaining gate is [name the actual unmet evidence]."

The brackets in that live debrief are an instruction to record actual results,
not permission for Cowork to invent a completed outcome.

## Recovery, Once

| Event | Immediate action and words | Evidence consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-in, role or network failure | Stop after one bounded check. "This route is not available; I will use the labelled evidence view." | Switch L -> E and retain the failure; never bypass authentication or private networking |
| Model disabled or authority expired | "The optional AI path is correctly unavailable under this authority. The manual action remains owned." | No inference success claim; do not enlarge allowance or enable proactive scans |
| Model timeout, missing usage or invalid output | Show explicit failure/unknown if safe; move to manual work | No silent template-as-AI or missing-usage-as-zero; actual retries count toward cost |
| AI draft is wrong | Identify the unsupported claim, reject it and show the source | Failure is an evaluation finding, not an excuse to conceal the result |
| Evidence becomes stale | Do not approve; show the dependent-review boundary | An earlier draft is not current approval |
| Unexpected sensitive information | Stop screen sharing and leave the page | Do not capture it into the deck or transcript |
| Audience requests an irreversible action | "That needs its own authority and evidence; I will show the boundary instead." | No live sending, release, payment, tenant change or privacy relaxation |

## If Interrupted

Restate the question in the customer's terms. Give a 20-second answer, then
either show one relevant backup or park the exact question with an owner.
Return with: "Back to this home: what evidence and whose decision are needed
next?"

If two minutes are consumed before the AI step, skip optional interface
navigation and show diagram 04. Always reach the boundary and next action.
If more time is lost, end the demo honestly and use S18; do not overrun by
claiming the demo time is outside the total 40-minute meeting.

## Evidence to Record After Rehearsal or Delivery

Record actual route, timings, audience questions, failures, exact observations,
pending decisions and corrective actions. A prepared script does not count
as a transcript, stakeholder agreement, delivery rehearsal or live acceptance.
Never paste credentials, raw app payloads, case identities or private screenshots.
