## The Story of the Technique

The method connects a business problem to inspectable implementation:

1. Explore stakeholders, journeys and constraints before choosing agents.
2. Build prototypes and MVP iterations to expose bad assumptions.
3. Capture learning in the addendum, knowledge base, BRD, PRD and ADRs.
4. Decompose requirements into epics, features and stories with dependencies
   and acceptance conditions.
5. Use bounded HVE research/plan/implement/review loops, isolated branches
   and pull-request evidence to deliver and correct changes.
6. Retain human authority, unresolved blockers and outcome tests rather than
   calling generated code or merged issues a measured business benefit.

HVE is engineering discipline, not a reason to replace human engagement or
to claim an automatic productivity multiplier. Role instruments generated
questions and hypotheses; they did not become actual customer participants.

## Floors and Timing

All floors apply before total score: A at least 21/35, B at least 21/35,
C at least 9/15, D at least 9/15; no criterion scored 1; thought-leadership
artifact submitted; required masterclasses attended. A strong technical score
cannot offset communication or missing thought leadership. Missing artifact
means Incomplete, not merely a low score.

The guide requires that artifact no later than two weeks before panel day,
with two assigned pre-panel reviewers; the panel receives it five business
days before. Do not invent a panel date or attendance evidence.

The owner's explicit new request sets this package to 30 minutes plus a
10-minute demo. The old 15-minute memory/deck route is historical, not an
unresolved contradiction. The demo must still land its executive "so what"
in under two minutes, then use the remaining time for depth and recovery.
Actual panel slot compatibility remains for the presenter to confirm.

No final-deck score, live behaviour, rehearsal result, customer endorsement
or thought-leadership dissemination is created by this handoff.
