## Flagged Tension for the Owner

Acceptance criterion 6 asks for no price, saving or payback claim. S18 states
a modelled per-home figure and a 15,600-home total, labelled as an assumption.
The brief's "Claims to Keep Out" list excludes any price or "cost per home"
figure. These conflict. The owner must choose one of:

* Keep the labelled scenario, accept that criterion 6 is read as "no verified
  price", and keep the "not a verified price" caveat spoken, not only written.
* Remove the figure from the main route, keep it only in B25, and speak the gap
  and the method on S18.

This draft recommends the second option for the main route because a
secondary-source rate and an assumed x10 and x20 multiplier are not measured
evidence (B7), and a stated gap is rewarded on C3. This is a recommendation
for review, not a decision.

payback claimed.

"I count what each accepted proposal costs, above an agreed quality floor, not
tokens. One operator exercise used 6,884 tokens across five runs on one model.

do not have a verified price or a cost per accepted proposal, and I will not
estimate one today. The next gate measures it on held-out cases."

counts only when Finance confirms where the spend falls."
