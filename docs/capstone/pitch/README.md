## Deliberate Audience Sequence

The archetypes are communication preferences, not personality diagnoses.

| Preference / likely concern | What to hear and see | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sergeant: sponsor or executive needs a decision | Bottom line, bounded next step, owner and timing; recommendation before platform detail | A service inventory or an unpriced approval request |
| Therapist: resident/service perspective needs trust | Same-home journey, resident choices, assisted routes and owned exceptions | Invented resident testimony or framing non-digital access as resistance |
| Scientist: technical, Finance and assurance questions need proof | Alternatives, dated evidence, unknowns, comparison method and failure routes | Unsupported model superiority, implied legal sign-off or a capacity price as total cost |
| Evangelist: future opportunity needs a tangible path | Conditional reusable history and cross-source reporting after the control/value case | Presenting Fabric, autonomy or a portal as delivered benefit |

Lead with the decision, connect it to people, establish the evidence, then
describe the conditional future. Keep one factual core across those registers.

## Delivery At The Important Moments

* Keep posture still for the recommendation and the final ask; do not talk to the diagram.
* Use one measured gesture across the workflow, then separate resident, professional and Finance decisions.
* On page 13, trace the receiving chain once, then the resident permission boundary. Use a measured gesture for private endpoints; do not read every Azure label. State that averages are derived on read and Quality sensor linkage remains gated.
* On page 14, trace contact, choice and aftercare once; do not read every map cell. Distinguish the pain hypothesis, designed response and actual test.
* On pages 24-25, pause on "future, not today's product" and "ontology is preview"; explain the benefit in business terms before naming components.
* Say "historical observation", "source implementation", "proposed" or "not evaluated" where each applies.
* On page 23, distinguish the registered base from unregistered amendments
  before describing execution. Credit Isabel's data collection without
  claiming independent institutional validation or completed analysis.
* Slow the voice for currencies, denominators, alternatives and unknowns; do not rush caveats after a confident claim.
* Keep facial expression neutral and attentive during challenge; restate the objection before defending the design.
* On page 9, concede the earlier wording: the case already reserved approvals.
  Explain the real reductions without claiming the requested L3/L4 levels were
  inherently unsafe. Use the [dated review](../source/review-2026-11-08.md) for
  the decision trace and current evidence limits.
* Use dress appropriate to the actual executive setting, avoiding visual distractions that compete with the material.

For Q&A, restate the question, answer it directly, then add one supporting
detail or acknowledge the gap and how to resolve it. A simpler approach that
beats the proposed technology is a valid result, not a threat to the story.
Native buttons retain fixed destinations despite historical "Return to caller"
labels; navigate back deliberately rather than promise dynamic return.

## Strengthening The Four Challenged Criteria

The [story and architecture review](../source/review-2026-11-08-cxo.md)
separates written preparation from evidence of live behaviour:

* For B1, lead with the recommendation, then show the options, the same-home
  causal example and what evidence supports the next decision. Do not let
  the service inventory become the argument.
* For B4, practise variations beyond the appendix: rules beating AI, requested
  L4 flags, a failed supplier connection, changed household authority and an
  unsupported AI answer. Give a direct answer, then the mechanism and its limit.
* For C2, let the question finish, restate the actual concern and clarify an
  ambiguous challenge. Concede a sound point without treating silence as consent.
* For C3, defend a choice and disclose its unproved benefit before being asked.
  State what would reverse the choice, the missing evidence and its owner.
