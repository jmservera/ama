## Page 20 / S17

Seven checkpoints between an AI draft and a real-world decision.

S17 | Seven checkpoints between an AI draft and a real-world decision.
TIMING: 60 s for this slide (all builds together) · 17:40-18:40 on the 23:45 narration clock.

SAY
Here is how we keep AI useful and safe at the same time. Seven checkpoints sit between an AI draft and anything that happens in the real world.

The user is signed in. Their role and case scope are checked. The model only sees permitted evidence, with source and version IDs. The call is bounded, with a finite allowance and an expiry. The output is checked for schema, citations and freshness, and anything stale or broken stops. A person with current permissions accepts it or rejects it with a reason. And the real decision, consent, quality acceptance or a Finance approval, is a separate step with its own audit trail.

If anything fails, the work falls back to its owner, never to an approval. And safety hazards bypass the AI path entirely: they go straight to the people who own them.
